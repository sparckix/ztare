# JaggedThoughts Institutional Learning

> Up: [`docs/README.md`](../README.md)

JaggedThoughts is a learning investment institution. It searches public markets,
studies companies and funds, freezes forecasts, observes later outcomes, and
turns repeated patterns into executable—but always scope-bound—investment laws.
Those laws can change future research priority and paper-portfolio policy only
after they survive evidence that arrived after the law was created.

The product thesis is ambitious and concrete: expertise that normally lives in
case discussions, analyst notebooks, operating playbooks, and portfolio-manager
judgment can become a compounding graph of typed conjectures, counterexamples,
and settled consequences. Its payoff criterion is future paper-portfolio utility
after costs versus simple value, quality, momentum, factor, and no-edge controls.

The possible edge is institutional rather than one magic signal. The system can
search more coherent business configurations than one analyst, retain what
happened across companies without losing the conditions, and connect those
operating consequences to the expectations embedded in securities. It earns
usefulness only when that accumulated memory improves a later implementable
portfolio frontier after fees, tax, turnover, liquidity, and downside.

## In simple terms

```text
public evidence
  -> company / fund fingerprint
  -> comparable cohort
  -> candidate explanation or formula
  -> counterexample search
  -> future-only forecast
  -> later market or operating outcome
  -> retain, narrow, reject, or transfer
  -> research priority and paper portfolio
```

## The 100x claim and its kill test

The defensible 100x target is research throughput and memory, not a promised
investment return. One investigation should leave reusable assets for every
later investigation: a source-bound business fingerprint, an exact strategy
phenotype, a covered comparison interval, a counterexample, a frozen forecast,
and a settled decision consequence. The same machinery can then search many
companies and funds while preserving the case-specific details that an ordinary
screen loses.

The economic hypothesis is that this memory improves three decisions together:

1. which company, fund, or strategic question deserves scarce research time;
2. which expected-earnings distribution differs from the distribution implied
   by price; and
3. which collection of those opportunities improves the implementable portfolio
   frontier after costs and risk.

The hypothesis fails if added strategy memory does not improve held-out operating
forecasts, if added underwriting laws do not improve later security forecasts,
or if the complete learned policy cannot beat simple value, quality, factor,
equal-weight, and cash controls after costs. More generated analysis without one
of those transitions is throughput without edge and must not receive policy
weight.

The compounding loops are deliberately asymmetric:

```text
new evidence -> extend coverage; never erase prior covered intervals
counterexample -> narrow a future law; never relabel its parent sample
settled forecast -> update method routing; never rewrite the forecast
paper outcome -> update policy evidence; never grant brokerage authority
new substrate -> transport a mechanism question; never transport its verdict
```

The workbench projects those loops into one `InstitutionalEdgeMap`. Each row has
one outcome and one authority: research-question choice to later paper outcome,
prospective strategy moves to operating consequences, historical strategy
evidence to candidate laws, underwriting models to security outcomes, paper
allocation to after-cost utility, company state to later state paths, and
research allocation to decision evidence. Count units are explicit: 309 typed
historical events are not mislabeled as forecasts or settlements. The historical
edge has one admissible diagnostic cell and the research allocator has one of
eight required blocks; neither is reviewable. High activity cannot substitute
for the transition that owns the claim, and alpha status remains `unestablished`.

The adjacent `LearningCreditAssignment` projection asks which exact component
earned a later outcome. It reuses those contracts and the existing settlement
stores. A bundle outcome stays attached to its frozen bundle; question routing,
one law, or one fund implementation earns component credit only through a
same-information prospective comparison that varied that unit. Decision lineage
supplies provenance, while independently settled variation supplies credit. The
current projection finds zero earned components. Four legacy matched
question-policy pairs remain visible but cannot enter the candidate-leaf ITT v2
census; newly issued v2 units start at zero. Nineteen underwriting bundles, 5
law candidates, 16 fund programs, and 2 complete-policy runs are also visible,
but none has the required settled component comparison. The
projection has paper-learning authority only.

`LearningExperimentDesign` makes that refusal operational. It reuses an
identifiable trial already in flight, or names the smallest prospective
variation still owed: question-policy arms use candidate-leaf randomization
under one dossier contract; strategy alpha compares zero residual, pre-open
momentum, deterministic valuation and value-quality controls, and a typed
strategy expectation residual under one frozen procedure; a law policy may vary one promoted law;
and a fund comparison may vary one admitted implementation inside one sleeve.
The current epoch continues the first two families and the complete-policy
tournament. It blocks law and fund component tests because no law is promoted
and no sleeve has two admitted implementations. Each design carries exact
source hashes, expected Golden Store object kinds, a later outcome contract,
and zero capital authority; it never rewrites prior evidence as component
credit.

The next institutional boundary is stricter: `LearningCreditAssignment` is the
only projector from a settled experiment into recurring acquisition
budget or default-policy influence. The scarce learning object is a sealed
policy delta—one mandate, universe, evidence epoch, control/treatment pair,
varied component, horizon, cost basis, benchmark, and independently settleable
block. A strategy representation, law, ranker, fund implementation, portfolio
rule, or Newton/Lagrangian leaf may enter a zero-authority shadow experiment
without prior credit; it may not become a recurring default because its own
module says `promotion_eligible`. Complete-policy settlement remains the only
route to operator review. The first three enforced edges are future research-
question routing, activation response-question routing, and underwriting-
information method routing. Their shared policy
compilers require a hash-valid component-credit row, the exact learned-policy
source reference, and the applicable named use. Missing, stale, or mismatched
credit falls back to balanced assignment. The research-question check lives in
the reusable assignment kernel, so direct callers cannot bypass it with a
merely self-hash-valid winner; consumption also binds the current research-
learning aggregate, so a later pair set invalidates old credit. Activation
routing analogously requires `future_activation_question_routing` and an exact
current eligible-pair-set digest. The underwriting selector analogously
requires `future_underwriting_method_routing` against the exact source-ablation
hash before it can reduce the three-arm ablation to a preferred method. The live
selector also requires that source to remain the current closed-book ablation
epoch. Other module-local surfaces remain shadow challengers or operator-review
inputs.

`LearningExperimentActivation` then answers the operational question the design
alone cannot answer: was the comparison issued, is an exact job or pre-open
action scheduled, is its identity deduplicated, and can a later settlement earn
an independent block? It delegates mutation to the existing discovery,
subscription-research, closed-book, and settlement state machines. An odd final
candidate in a discovery stratum is now explicitly nonexperimental because its
same-epoch counterpart does not exist. Historical missed issuance windows are
reported but never repaired after outcomes may be knowable. The current
strategy audit has two legacy bindings, zero eligible five-model episodes, and
zero settlements. MRVL awaits an adjacent frontier preserving the exact choice
identities; SARO awaits exact event timing; four refinement jobs are queued.
These are acquisition boundaries, not outcome forecasts.

The durable scheduler watches the institutional-learning state hash. When new
cohort evidence changes that epoch, the existing capital-cycle service becomes
due and recompiles the opportunity book. Subscription calls remain bounded by
the shared daily gate; an exhausted gate waits for reset rather than switching
to an untracked provider.

An LLM may notice that low implied growth and durable earnings appear together
in attractive cases. The kernel translates that thought into a typed formula,
freezes its cohort and start time, and tests it on later benchmark-relative
returns. A subgroup reversal—oil producers, highly levered firms, or one market
regime, for example—is saved as a counterexample. A narrower successor may be
created, but it begins after the counterexample became available and cannot
reuse the breaking sample as confirmation.

This boundary follows the finance literature rather than treating an implied
return as a realized-return fact. Gebhardt, Lee, and Swaminathan's
[implied-cost-of-capital construction](https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-679X.00007)
is an expectations proxy. [Lee, So, and Wang](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/15-022_c2e110ad-f195-48e6-a00e-4aead7e33191_ec75cac3-3176-4a35-a117-1da07c8b5dcb.pdf)
evaluate such proxies by later-return predictive power and measurement error,
not by definitional identity. The engine
therefore freezes implied-return coordinates as forecasts and requires later
settlement. Its multiplicity gates and prospective cohorts respond to the
data-mining and decay problems documented by
[Harvey, Liu, and Zhu](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592.pdf) and
[McLean and Pontiff](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12365).

### Filing-time mechanism replay

Historical testing is split by producer and outcome. A deterministic compiler
can replay an unchanged accounting formula against filings available at each
cutoff without model-memory leakage. The current replay settles 436 next-fiscal-
year owner-earnings-margin episodes across 56 companies and 17 fiscal-year
blocks. The durability composite has pooled rank correlation 0.464, but current
owner-earnings-margin persistence reaches 0.779; the mean paired block
difference is -0.287. The cheaper persistence control therefore wins this task.

An expanding-time follow-up fits coefficients and feature standardization on
prior fiscal blocks only. Across 14 later blocks and 404 episodes, the
persistence control averaged rho 0.7087; adding durability averaged 0.6185, an
increment of -0.0902 (paired bootstrap interval -0.1781 to -0.0087; sign-flip
`p=0.0611`; 80%-power minimum detectable increment 0.1233). Incremental
durability information is therefore unsupported. The adverse-transition
follow-up remains unopened because its activation gate did not pass.

This is business-mechanism evidence, not return evidence. The current formula
was selected after the historical period and the companies are conditioned on
today's quality-coverage universe, so the result cannot support prospective
transfer, paper policy, or an alpha claim. Historical subscription-model answers
remain diagnostic because point-in-time source packets cannot remove information
embedded in model parameters. Security-return claims still require sealed future
episodes or a separately survivorship-safe mechanical replay.

### Strategy-event replay

The strategy clock now also asks a narrower historical question: after a public
company explicitly completes an acquisition or disposition, what happened to
its next filing-time owner-earnings margin? The compiler enumerates SEC Form 8-K
Item 2.01 events, retrieves the exact primary document, labels only explicit
transaction language with deterministic rules, and joins the event to exact
before/after accounting observations. Missing baseline, missing outcome,
unavailable document, and ambiguous document are preserved separately.

The current fixed search contains 162 events, of which 141 are mature and 67 have
usable accounting endpoints. All 67 filing documents have been hydrated with
zero provider errors. Rules plus source-quote semantic resolutions now compile 58 operating
episodes and exclude nine identity transitions, non-events, or financing-right transfers;
no document remains ambiguous. The episodes include 35 acquisitions, 20
divestitures, one partnership, one separated-company event, and one mixed
resource-reallocation bundle. Their raw one-year accounting deltas are useful
for killing a uniform rule, but not for estimating a move effect: the policy was
chosen after the sample and selection, timing, environment, and pretrends remain
uncontrolled.

The control-design compiler turns that residual into work. A typed four-moderator
grammar exhausts 16 projections over implementation mode, transaction form,
operating-object scope, and issuer role. Frontier closure uses only structural
support and timing—not observed effects—to retain the nondominated grains.
Twenty-one cells currently have at least four treated entities across two event
years, and 46 treated episodes have at least three pre-periods plus one
post-period. A bounded, market-catalog-prioritized SEC acquisition batch then
screens potential controls for full filing-index coverage, related Item 2.01
events, point-in-time Company Facts, and common pretrend support. Current
industry and market capitalization may prioritize source work; they cannot
admit a historical control or establish event-time comparability.

The primary implemented estimator remains group-time ATT with never- or
not-yet-treated controls. Untreated-observation imputation, synthetic DiD, and
parallel-trend-violation sensitivity are declared robustness targets rather
than silently substituted estimators. The periodic business clock recompiles
the replay and design, while law-promotion, paper-policy, and capital authority
remain false.

The 27-company replay is now a calibration sample, not the learning population.
The nightly SEC submissions archive is stored behind an exact source receipt and
compiled across every filer with an Item 2.01 event, independently of today's
ticker catalog. The current archive yields 26,142 events across 9,923 issuers
from 2010–2026; 7,939 events across 2,582 issuers also map to today's U.S.
common-equity catalog. This separation lets historical learning retain acquired,
delisted, and otherwise absent issuers while the deployment projection remains
limited to currently investable securities.

The first-family-adoption compiler identifies 489 industry/year cells with at
least four adopters and four later adopters, leaving 8,802 outcome-mature source

## The five owning categories

The product compounds only if each question stays with the object that can settle
it. These are the five pillars; symbolic regression, Lagrangian dynamics, factor
models, and qualitative agents are competing model families inside them rather than
separate products.

| Category | Question | Durable output | Settling evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| business state | How does this company create, defend, and lose economic value? | source-bound company and industry fingerprint | later filings, unit economics, and operating observations |
| strategic choice search | Which coherent move systems are feasible, locally attractive, or globally nondominated? | typed choices, incompatibility witnesses, local peaks, and scope-relative frontier | source-observed execution and omitted-choice residuals |
| strategy-law learning | Which granular executed moves work in which environments, and where do they fail? | phenotype law cards, moderators, cohorts, and counterexamples | comparison-group operating outcomes and transfer blocks |
| security underwriting | What earnings path and risk does the price imply, and how does it differ from the business distribution? | valuation programs, factor/state-price decomposition, thesis rivals, and forecast | future cash flows, prices, benchmarks, and costs |
| capital allocation | Which paper positions coexist under concentration, downside, turnover, liquidity, and mandate constraints? | watchlist, paper portfolio frontier, and frozen decision packet | prospective after-cost portfolio outcomes |

The institutional-memory graph connects the pillars:

```text
business state
  -> enumerated strategy systems
  -> executed move phenotype
  -> operating-outcome law
  -> earnings distribution versus price-implied expectations
  -> paper allocation
  -> settled return and counterexample
  -> revised business, strategy, and investment questions
```

The valuation grammar participates in the same learning loop. It derives a
conditional payoff grid, asks whether observed price and a declared numeraire admit
positive state prices, and turns failures or near-zero state bounds into rival
requests. Proposed changes—another state axis, wider support, a different numeraire,
or a different terminal mechanism—are compared one at a time on a later common
cohort. A revision cannot validate itself on the cases that suggested it.

Agents own open-world acquisition, interpretation, mechanism proposals, and rival
theses. Typed leaves own identities and evidence epochs. Deterministic programs own
arithmetic, enumeration, constraint satisfaction, causal eligibility, chronology,
and settlement. A model-family experiment may earn a place in underwriting only by
beating same-information controls on unseen blocks; evocative equations alone have
no policy role.

This also separates capability from delegation. An agent may generate options,
propose a causal graph, or transport an analogy beyond the observed cases. The
kernel can verify identities, arithmetic, chronology, and outcomes. Neither
ability implies discretion over capital: delegation rises only after the complete
decision policy demonstrates repeated prospective value within its authority
boundary.

## The AWS isomorphism

The useful transfer from service packaging is structural:

| Service reasoning | Investment institution |
|---|---|
| service fingerprint | company, fund, industry, or portfolio phenotype |
| customer or usage cohort | entity × horizon × industry × regime cohort |
| packaging program | strategic choice system, investment formula, or portfolio policy |
| conjecture | versioned investment-law candidate |
| regularity | bounded predictive or causal law |
| counterexample-guided refinement | preserve the subgroup that reverses the law, then create a future-only narrower scope |
| frontier closure | retain nondominated strategy, law, or portfolio programs and provide witnesses for the rest |
| experiment statistics | decide whether a pattern transfers beyond the cases that suggested it |

“Law” means a versioned regularity with a declared population, estimator,
outcome, evidence epoch, and failure domain. It does not mean a timeless market
theorem.

## The business-investor learning loop

Business understanding and investment judgment train each other, but they have
different outcomes. JaggedThoughts therefore learns two connected edges rather
than collapsing them into one score:

```text
company state + strategic move
  -> later operating consequence
  -> revised earnings-power distribution
  -> gap versus the expectations embedded in price
  -> later benchmark-relative return
```

The first edge asks whether a management choice improved durability, growth,
unit economics, returns on incremental capital, or downside resilience. The
second asks whether the security price already anticipated that consequence. A
strong move at an exuberant price can be a weak investment; an ordinary
business with expectations below durable earnings power can be attractive.

The strategy library needs three grains to learn this without erasing context:

| Grain | Identity | Use |
|---|---|---|
| move family | action × economic bridge | generate a broad transfer conjecture |
| mechanism phenotype | family × strategy form × addressed competitive pressures | select comparable treatment and control histories |
| company instance | exact object × conditions × break cases × dated sources | establish what happened and settle its outcome |

A family such as `expand_adjacent_scope -> growth` is deliberately broad. It
may connect acquisitions, new platforms, and internal capability expansion, but
those are not automatically comparable interventions. The phenotype is the
middle layer that decides which histories can enter the same causal diagnostic.
The exact instance remains the unit that can be sourced, falsified, and linked
to a security thesis.

An accepted qualified-equity dossier now activates that lowering automatically.
The durable queue freezes the dossier hash, company identity, evidence epoch,
candidate lineage, and the only admissible source ids. A subscription-authenticated
agent proposes the industry state, counterfactual options, mechanisms, and ordinal
scenario consequences with web access disabled. The existing compiler then rejects
identity drift, unknown evidence references, untyped mechanisms, non-ordinal generated
effects, invalid incompatibilities, or incomplete scenario coverage before Z3 closes
the declared option space. The choice-space certificate carries the typed constraint
catalog itself—cardinality bounds, pair exclusion, prerequisite implication, and
linear resource bounds—with exact Boolean lowerings and active counts; the workbench
does not maintain a second predicate list. This is the acquisition edge that lets the library grow
with every accepted company investigation instead of depending on handwritten
profiles.

This creates a two-way compounding path. Investment research supplies market
expectations, peer sets, and consequences that challenge strategy theories.

## Research memory as a compounding asset

Periodic discovery changes price, rank, valuation, and candidate identity more
often than a company's operating mechanism changes. Re-running a full research
agent on every numeric epoch would spend attention without adding proportional
information. JaggedThoughts now keeps those identities separate while allowing
bounded reuse:

```text
accepted dossier + material-source baselines
  -> later candidate for the same entity
  -> compare every configured material content hash
     | equal -> candidate-bound qualitative coverage bridge
     | changed -> local reassessment, then bridge or re-underwrite
     | unmonitored -> new underwriting
```

The bridge covers strategy, industry, and durable-earnings evidence only. The
new candidate keeps its own valuation, factor analysis, rank, screen, forecast,
and authority. This distinction turns prior research into a revocable asset
without smuggling an old conclusion into a new price.

That separation continues into proposal compilation. An accepted bridge lets
the prior dossier and its strategy frontier supply qualitative coordinates to
an inactive paper proposal, while discovery, valuation, underwriting, and the
modeled payoff grid must all bind the later candidate. A pending reassessment
is shown as pending and cannot impersonate accepted coverage.

Each dossier's strategy is also a bounded directed graph. The compiler requires
edge endpoints to use exact choice ids and canonicalizes graphs up to node
renaming. A repeated topology across companies creates a challenger question:
does the same graph shape carry a shared earnings mechanism, or is it only a
structural resemblance? Topology cannot establish semantic equivalence,
causality, or alpha. Later source-bound outcomes must do that work.

## Three connected frontiers

The system gets leverage from composing three searches rather than asking one
model for a ticker recommendation.

1. The company strategy frontier enumerates reinforcing and conflicting choice
   systems under rival industry worlds. Its output is a source-bound operating
   consequence proposal such as higher earnings durability or greater downside
   fragility.
2. The institutional law frontier recursively enumerates formulas over typed
   phenotype metrics. It compares programs on chronological market-date blocks,
   stability, coverage, and simplicity, then preserves a witnessed Pareto
   frontier. Selected formulas start a new future evidence epoch.
3. The portfolio frontier compares admissible opportunities on expected utility,
   uncertainty, factor exposure, drawdown, concentration, liquidity, and costs.
   It may keep cash.

The first implemented mechanism graph already has two composable routes:

```text
strategic choice adoption
  -> earnings durability
  -> value × quality or low-expectations × durability
  -> active return
  -> paper-portfolio utility after costs
```

The arrows before `active return` are conjectures until their required evidence
settles. The graph makes the missing links visible and lets the research planner
ask which observation would most change a strategy, law, or portfolio frontier.

The complete-policy tournament supplies the final consequence. It freezes one
qualified universe and compares cash, equal weight, discovery-priority,
law-adjusted priority, and reviewed operator weights on the same later returns.
Identical weight vectors share one representative, preventing a conceptual alias
from becoming another statistical trial. Settlement records total allocation
return versus SPY and security-selection contribution versus equal benchmark
exposure as separate outcomes. The cash hurdle is frozen from the newest
market-state receipt available at the block's opening epoch, never from a later
snapshot.

The same block freezes why each policy differs from its nearest declared
reference: cash and discovery compare with equal weight, while learned-law and
reviewed-operator policies compare with discovery. Each security's weight delta
retains its candidate hash, source receipts, research question, discovery score,
and eligible-law contributions. When prices arrive, `delta_weight ×
active_return` gives an exact contribution ledger whose sum reconciles to the
policy-score difference after incremental cost. Overlapping inputs are not
assigned causal credit until the engine has matched prospective policy
variation.

## The law object

An `InvestmentLawCandidate` owns:

- a stable `law_id@version` and creation epoch;
- entity kinds, horizons, cohort conditions, and evaluation environments;
- a signal AST or a source-bound causal-panel estimator;
- antecedent and consequence concepts for graph composition;
- an outcome metric, trial family, validation contract, and decision use;
- origin and generation receipts;
- diagnostic or shadow authority, never order authority.

The lifecycle is:

```text
observed -> phenotyped -> conjectured -> challenged
         -> prospectively tested -> transfer candidate -> policy review
```

Every stage is an object transition. A changed formula, cohort, mechanism, or
version creates a new identity.

## Recursive law search

[`law_search.py`](../../src/ztare/investment/law_search.py) reuses the general
JaggedThoughts typed-program enumerator and frontier compiler. For each entity
kind it:

1. reads predictor-role metrics from point-in-time phenotype episodes;
2. retains inputs already used by the current expertise catalog and fills the
   remaining grammar slots from observed coverage and variation;
3. enumerates identity, negation, spread, pairwise product, and recursive
   score-by-feature product programs to a declared depth and program budget;
4. scores each program only on complete cross-sections inside exact market-date
   inference blocks;
5. builds chronological training and holdout summaries;
6. compiles a Pareto frontier with dominance and local-peak witnesses;
7. creates at most three future-dated conjectures per entity kind after the
   declared block floor is met.

The current equity and fund searches each enumerate 305 programs to depth two
and exhaust their bounded grammars. The live projection reports its prospective
episode and inference-block counts. None has matured, so the engine has generated
zero new laws and names the first scoring date instead.

A short metamorphic learning test supplies the behavioral proof while those
market windows mature. Eight selection blocks contain a hidden
`price-implied excess return × earnings durability` relation. The engine
recovers that composition, mints one future-dated law, and excludes all 96
selection rows from evaluation. On eight later blocks the law becomes a
prospective transfer candidate with 0.9947 holdout rank correlation. Reversing
the relation in a new two-block industry changes the verdict to
`challenged_by_counterexample` and emits a future-domain refinement. The test
also verifies the causal-use membrane: an eligible law adds a bounded
cross-sectional research-priority adjustment, and the sign-reversal verdict
withdraws it. The test is
[`test_institutional_learning.py`](../../tests/investment/test_institutional_learning.py).

Grammar closure only states that every program in the declared search scope was
partitioned. Economic validity requires later outcomes.

## The first policy consequence

[`compile_law_policy_influence`](../../src/ztare/investment/institutional_learning.py)
joins settled law evaluations to the current discovery cross-section. Only an
association law that passes prospective-transfer and multiplicity gates can
contribute. The kernel re-executes its AST on current candidate metrics, converts
the direction-adjusted output to a cross-sectional percentile, caps each law at
±5 percentage points and the total at ±10 points, and writes the receipt into
the opportunity book and Golden Store lineage.

This changes research ordering only. It cannot change a candidate's screen,
active paper-decision weight, or capital authority. If a later evaluation is no longer eligible,
the next capital cycle emits zero influence from that law. Causal laws require a
separate target compiler because treatment effects are not candidate scores.
That compiler is now present. It binds one current discovery candidate to one
same-entity strategy move and requires an exact adoption event, causal
environment, operating metric, unit, direction, and declared horizon. It then
proves the target entity, move, event, and panel rows were absent from the law's
training support and that no target outcome was available at the decision
epoch. A passing law contributes one fixed capped research-priority increment,
not its effect magnitude. The opportunity book freezes that path beside the
baseline, and the existing paper-policy tournament later settles both on the
same return window.

## Predictive and causal laws

Rank-association laws answer whether a formula orders later return within a
comparable market-date cross-section. One cross-section counts as one market
history block; four tickers observed on the same day do not become four
independent histories. The evaluator uses chronological holdout, power-aware
verdicts, subgroup counterexamples, and Benjamini–Hochberg correction across the
compiled trial family.

The search-trial census now owns the final promotion membrane for that frozen
family. It verifies the exact pre-outcome candidate set and outcome boundary,
collapses multiple environments within each law conservatively, retains
unfinished laws in the denominator, and applies Benjamini–Hochberg across the
complete committed family. Its output can admit a law to paper-policy review;
it cannot change a screen, position size, or capital authority.

Causal laws use source-bound company-period panels. The first deterministic
adapter implements common-adoption difference-in-differences with minimum unit
and period floors, a parallel-trend diagnostic, and unit-cluster bootstrap. It
refuses staggered adoption until a group-time treatment-effect adapter exists.
An association may influence prediction after prospective settlement; it may
not receive causal language.

Strategy regularities add a stricter membrane. The law identity is the exact
move phenotype, while industry remains a transfer and counterexample
environment rather than creating duplicate laws. Seed cases can propose the
phenotype but cannot validate it. Only independently acquired post-freeze
treatment events, bounded non-adopters, later operating outcomes, and two or
more transfer environments enter the prospective panel. The frozen gate
requires at least four treated units, four controls, two pre-periods, one
post-period, a 5% meaningful-effect floor, 80% power, bootstrap uncertainty,
and cycle-wide multiplicity control. Reversals become future-only CEGAR scope
exclusions; they do not relabel the parent sample. The current workspace has
zero qualifying treated units, controls, or transfer environments and therefore
zero prospective regularities.

The law compiler also guards the conjecture boundary. Every law activates no
earlier than the latest implementation event, frontier evidence, cohort-search
availability, and compiler-release epoch. This prevents a conjecture discovered
later from being backdated to the business event it describes. The current MRVL
phenotype is therefore prospective only after `2026-08-13T13:40:01Z`; its
pre-outcome trial family cannot consume outcomes available before its frozen
boundary. Each transfer card hashes the complete chain from frontier and
conjecture through phenotype, cohort, outcomes, holdout/power/multiplicity, and
remaining transfer gaps. An association cannot consume or produce its
own outcome metric, and the outcome cannot define cohort membership,
evaluation environments, or counterexample partitions. A mechanism consequence
cannot reappear as an unlagged antecedent. These checks reject circular or
post-outcome constructions before a panel is scored.

## Goldilocks execution split

| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| LLM or research skill | translate an open question, find current primary sources, propose semantic types and mechanisms, challenge a cohort, explain a counterexample |
| typed leaf or program | carry the exact source epoch, revocable research coverage, choice topology, metric roles, formula AST, law identity, causal panel, forecast, or portfolio proposal |
| deterministic kernel | enforce identity and chronology, execute formulas, enumerate programs, compile frontiers, settle outcomes, calculate statistics, and enforce authority |

The LLM is allowed to be creative where semantic search matters. The kernel is
strict where arithmetic, time, comparison, and state transitions matter. A
fixed formula does not replace judgment; an unconstrained narrative does not
replace settlement.

## Why this could compound

The mechanism graph allows knowledge learned at one unit of analysis to become
a typed input elsewhere:

- cross-company laws can identify which strategic choices deserve deeper
  operating research;
- cross-industry counterexamples can reveal the boundary of a durability or
  expectations thesis;
- fund holdings can aggregate company phenotypes into look-through earnings and
  fragility estimates;
- public-company evidence can seed a private-company operating hypothesis while
  preserving the change of evidence and authority;
- every forecast settlement can update the value of a method, a law bundle, and
  the acquisition policy that chose to study it.

This is the strongest 100× direction: the institution learns which questions,
representations, mechanisms, and policies create economic information—not only
which historical formula had the highest score.

## Evidence and value standard

The integrated category is a build hypothesis: a source-bound strategy grammar,
recursive law search, causal panels, forecast tournaments, and portfolio
consequences share one append-only evidence graph. A defensible research claim
would require an explicit
comparison against automated factor mining, causal discovery, program
synthesis, quantitative model selection, and financial research agents.

Architecture does not earn portfolio influence. A law must improve later ranking or
paper-book utility after costs versus declared baselines, retain usefulness in
another compatible cohort, and survive the same selection corrections applied
to its rivals. No predictive edge has been established; the first prospective
outcomes are pending.

[OpenPM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09988) motivates the point-in-time field,
typed-constraint, role-separation, turnover, and cost-audit controls. [The
Statistical Limit of Arbitrage](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33070) makes weak,
rare signals and estimation error the default search boundary. [Large Language
Models: An Applied Econometric
Framework](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5094968) makes
historical LLM prediction ineligible for predictive credit unless the design
rules out training leakage. These are design constraints, not inherited
performance evidence.

The statistical boundaries follow multiple-period difference-in-differences
([Callaway and Sant’Anna](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09015)), modern DiD design
diagnostics ([Roth et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01194)), environment-based
invariance ([Peters, Bühlmann, and Meinshausen](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssb.12167)),
counterexample-guided refinement
([Clarke et al.](https://researchr.org/publication/ClarkeGJLV00)), model-set
uncertainty ([Hansen, Lunde, and Nason](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.3982/ecta5771)),
and backtest-selection correction
([Bailey and López de Prado](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/SSRN_ID2460551_code87814.pdf?abstractid=2460551)).

The granularity repair is also consistent with current strategy research.
[Interdependence and Performance: A Natural Experiment in Firm
Scope](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/stsc.2015.0004) finds that the
effect of changing firm scope depends on firm-specific interdependence rather
than transferring as a universal “focus” rule. [When Artificial Intelligence
Does Strategy](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/stsc.2025.0448)
shows why even a large business-model catalog can converge to a self-confirming
policy before exhausting its alternatives. JaggedThoughts answers both risks
with typed moderators, counterexample-preserving grammar versions, and explicit
representation residuals; whether that improves future decisions remains an
empirical question.

## Research that learns how to research

Each qualified company or fund receives a frozen typed question program before
the subscription agent runs. The program enumerates evidence atoms such as a
thesis premise, rival explanation, missing operating observation, or strategy
boundary. The dossier must return one outcome per exact atom and bind every
resolved outcome to admitted source receipts. A fluent narrative cannot stand
in for the contract.

Coverage-first and disagreement-first are competing research policies. Their
near-term outcomes are atom resolution, primary-source yield, rival evidence,
latency, and cost. Later security and portfolio outcomes are kept separate.
Only a future candidate-level randomized intent-to-treat census can show
whether one research policy improves economic decisions. This follows the warning in surrogate-index work that a
short-run measure predicts a long-run outcome only under additional surrogacy
conditions ([Athey et al.](https://www.nber.org/papers/w26463)) and the adaptive
policy-learning literature's separation of exploration data from policy value
([Zhan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03756)).

The scheduler consumes that later evidence without conditioning the census on
dossier or paper activation. The candidate leaf alone fixes a 50/50 arm. Each
request also freezes its typed rank-lane benchmark, a day-30 decision cutoff,
day-365 terminal, 5% shadow probe, and trading cost. At the cutoff the first
assignment-bound full-research closed-book forecast freezes either the probe or
abstention; every assignment opens the same price window. At the terminal the
probe contributes its after-cost active return and abstention contributes zero.
Missing prices for a positive probe block credit, while an unbound benchmark is
excluded before inference. Reviews use fixed 20-unit-per-arm increments, alpha
spending, and a one-percentage-point minimum useful effect. A supported result
changes research order only, while half of later candidates remain randomized
audits. It cannot rank securities, size positions, or authorize capital.

The scheduler also keeps one strategy-cohort dispatch in five outside the
world implied by its current conjectures. That law-blind probe is selected from
a frozen public catalog with source availability, a broad size band, and a
stable hash. Law support, observed adoption, cohort gaps, and learned bonuses
are forbidden inputs. This gives the institution a standing route to discover
counterexamples rather than repeatedly sampling its own preferred environments.

## The tradable-time membrane

A forecast or portfolio proposal is sealed before it can earn an outcome. Its
return window begins at the earliest synchronized company, benchmark, or full
portfolio price observation available after that seal. The exit is the earliest
synchronized observation available after the complete horizon. Therefore a
pre-seal price move cannot be credited to the engine.

Overlapping return intervals form one connected inference block. Adjacent
21-day or 90-day issues cannot inflate the effective sample size. Historical
LLM replays remain diagnostic because prompts cannot erase training-time market
memory. Prospective sealed runs and deterministic point-in-time replays are the
paths that can accumulate engine evidence. Operating outcomes and investment
returns run on two clocks: a nearer filing can test the business mechanism;
the later market window tests whether that information created after-cost value.

The source clock is now a content-addressed manifest rather than a timestamp
claim. Each new closed-book company or fund packet resolves the latest pre-issue
vault manifest that contains its required price and filing source IDs. The
packet hash and GoldenStore lineage bind the forecast to that information set;
missing coverage stays explicit. This controls source chronology while leaving
model-memory knowledge as a separate, unresolved authority boundary.

The scheduler therefore fills a current horizon cohort on its opening date and
waits for that cohort's latest protected exit before starting another dated
cohort. A legacy run without a prospective-return contract or persisted binding
does not consume cadence. This expands cross-sectional coverage inside one block
and replaces unscorable episodes without presenting either action as another
independent market history. Market-state issuance applies the same protected-exit
rule; a shorter polling cadence may detect maturity promptly but cannot issue an
overlapping horizon that its own tournament would exclude.

## The three compounding clocks

| business | Did a strategy move change the named earnings, durability, or competitive mediator in a compatible environment? | a narrower or transported strategy law |
| market | Did the frozen forecast or complete paper policy beat simple rivals after costs? | influence over later paper policy |

The research clock is now self-advancing. Terminal queue work settles the

## Market-scale strategy laws: current operation

The strategy-learning path is an evidence compiler, not an M&A score. It starts
from the full historical SEC Item 2.01 population, reads the exact filed document,
types the registrant's move, and completes earlier issuer histories before calling
anything a first acquisition, divestiture, partnership, separation, or resource
reallocation. Completion date determines the business event; filing time
determines when the event became knowable.

The financial side retains every as-filed annual revision. A compact coverage
index lets the acquisition policy avoid spending language-model judgment on
issuers that cannot supply the required owner-earnings history. Recursive
residuals then become acquisition work: a missing predecessor filing, a missing
compatible treated issuer, a missing later adopter, and a missing common post
year are different next actions.

The current population has 309 typed events. Of 262 eligible operating events,
154 have three pre-event and one post-event annual histories; 108 also have a
closed earlier filing prefix. Two industry × move × year cells reach marginal
4/4 support, and one reaches joint-calendar support. Its diagnostic does not
support the broad acquisition claim: parallel trends fail and the resampling
interval is extremely wide. That result prevents a broad move label from entering
portfolio logic and directs the next frozen rival toward a narrower strategy
phenotype, moderator, or more stable operating outcome.

The formal system makes that next step mechanical. It enumerates every bounded
combination of transaction form, operating scope, and issuer role, collapses
programs that produce the same company partition, and keeps the trade-off
between identification support and phenotype resolution. It never uses the
child outcome to choose the child. At the current evidence epoch, all eight

The same identity discipline applies before a valuation can become a learning
episode. A spot price and an accounting-period share count are not automatically
compatible. JaggedThoughts types stock splits, keeps annual and current diluted
shares distinct, and produces a share-basis receipt. An intervening split blocks

therefore removes an entire family of false opportunity signals from later
screens, forecasts, and portfolio experiments.

The paired outcome family keeps the question interpretable without allowing a
few tiny-revenue firms to determine the answer. Raw owner-earnings margin remains
the economic primary. `owner_earnings_balance = m/(1+|m|)` is a unit-invariant,
bounded stress score applied to every cell; the engine cannot choose between
them after seeing the estimates. An inverse-hyperbolic-sine transform was not
used because its results can depend materially on the units of measurement
([Aihounton and Henningsen, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/ectj/utaa032)). This
implements the broader event-study discipline that outcome restrictions and
effect targets need an economic justification fixed before the next evidence
epoch ([Borusyak, Jaravel, and Spiess, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdae007)).

### Two strategy comparisons, not one diluted control group

The strategy learner now keeps two causal questions apart. `P:0` asks whether
the exact focal strategy phenotype beats no related-family adoption. `P:Q` asks
whether it beats an actively chosen same-family alternative. Active comparators
can make adopters more comparable at the decision date, but only when starts,
eligibility, switching, and bundles are aligned; this is the logic of
[active-comparator new-user designs](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4778958/).

JaggedThoughts therefore does not pour family-only peers into its untreated
panel. A second frontier enumerates the existing phenotype projections and
checks whether P and Q firms share industry, calendar cohort, exact adoption
timing, prehistory, and uncontaminated follow-up. Any later estimator must allow
staggered and heterogeneous effects rather than pooled two-way fixed effects,
following [Callaway and Sant'Anna](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.12.001),
[Sun and Abraham](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.09.006), and
[Borusyak, Jaravel, and Spiess](https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdae007).
At the current epoch, no P:Q cell meets the declared firm floors, so the output
is an eligibility frontier rather than an estimate. The remaining `Q` history
gap survived a successful Company Facts fetch and is reported as insufficient
source coverage, not queued again as if no acquisition had run.

### What recursive strategy enumeration and Z3 do

The strategy term language has one terminal per source-bound option and one binary
operator:

```text
Program ::= Option | combine_reinforcing_choices(Program, Program)
```

`combine` is interpreted modulo associativity and commutativity, so reordered or
re-parenthesized copies are one program. Z3 does not invent the options or recurse
over syntax. It assigns one Boolean to every declared option, enumerates every model
that satisfies the typed predicate catalog, and blocks each model after emission.
The kernel then constructs one canonical balanced AST for that admitted option set.
The current predicates lower as `cardinality_ge`, `cardinality_le`,
`not_all_selected`, `implies_all_selected`, and `linear_sum_le`.

For the current five-option MRVL illustration, bundles contain one to three choices
and `accelerate_custom_xpu_platform` is incompatible with
`preserve_broad_communications_portfolio`. There are
`C(5,1) + C(5,2) + C(5,3) = 25` bounded nonempty subsets; the forbidden pair and
the three triples containing that pair remove four, leaving 21 solver-admitted
programs. One emitted program is:

```text
combine(
  reserve_advanced_capacity,
  combine(expand_scaleup_switching, build_photonic_scaleup_fabric)
)
```

The compiler derives 39 exact one-choice edges over those 21 programs. On the edge
that adds `reserve_advanced_capacity` to switching plus photonics, the authored
scenario model predicts `+0.07` earnings durability, `0.00` growth, `-0.15` capital
efficiency, `+0.22` downside resilience, and `+0.40` industry-pressure coverage.
That target is both Pareto-frontier and a one-choice local peak in the authored
model. Every delta is explicitly marked `requires_observed_transition_and_contrast`.

The certificate proves exhaustive feasibility only inside the declared finite model.
It does not prove that the option vocabulary is complete, that the incompatibility is
economically correct, that the scenario effects will occur, or that any program
creates security returns. Those are separate source, measurement, causal, valuation,
and portfolio questions. The useful next step is to prospectively classify whether a
company adopted `P` or `P + option`, put both on the same operating clock and
environment stratum, settle the readout, and feed the observed contrast back into a
successor frontier.

Recursive strategy attribution is deliberately narrower. Today the observable

A new source-typed strategy event can change what the institution investigates
without changing what it is allowed to buy. The frozen event queue orders attention
by disagreement between typed and untyped operating models. Public-source acquisition
then runs through ordinary discovery. A qualified company enters capital-candidate

supports the move-to-durable-earnings bridge, the rival explanation, both, or neither,
and must separately state the valuation implication. Exact source-support tokens make

alter security rank, create a paper proposal, or allocate capital.

Each point-in-time strategy shadow is also stored by content hash. When the event

## One event, one visible learning clock

The strategy-event learning unit is the smallest end-to-end institutional memory.
It answers three plain questions for each observed company move: where is the case,
what exact artifact owns it, and what must happen next? Its stages run from discovery
closure or research request through dossier, formal option frontier, operating
settlement, return settlement, and joined scoring readiness.

Candidate refreshes cannot silently strand the clock. If a frozen request no longer
matches the current discovery leaf, the runtime records `superseded`; the event may
open a successor request only by recompiling the current candidate while preserving
the original move identity and time boundary. Nothing is learned merely because an
agent wrote a thesis. The company mechanism, subsequent operating result, and
factor-controlled security result must meet again at their frozen identities before
the scoring layer can compare models.
The unit is a clock and lineage join, not a new evaluator. Operating and return losses
remain owned by their frozen aggregate tournaments, while candidate-bound dual-outcome
episodes remain the separate bridge from an underwritten thesis to investable results.
The join has no anonymous fallback: a dossier or frontier without the exact selected
request hash is unrelated, even when its company or display order appears compatible.

## Keep evidence deep and clocks small

The institutional clock is an index over evidence, not another copy of the evidence.
A factor analysis owns its point-in-time observation identities. Underwriting keeps
that analysis hash plus the count and hash of the observation vector. A capital cycle
keeps the underwriting and learning-state hashes plus immutable paths. This lets the
periodic engine traverse the same lineage without serializing tens of megabytes of
repeated identifiers into every cycle and golden-store leaf.

While a cycle is calling blinded forecast arms or settling due episodes, its heartbeat
says `running_capital_cycle`. The distinction matters operationally: `checking_due_work`
means the scheduler is deciding whether to act; `running_capital_cycle` means a frozen
prospective protocol is in flight.

## What makes the loop consequential

The institution does not learn when an agent writes an attractive explanation. It
learns only when a frozen method predicts an observation that later becomes public,
settles under the same identity, and beats its declared rivals across independent
blocks. Search artifacts remain below that boundary.

For company strategy, the agent may author two to twelve typed candidate predicates.
The kernel evaluates each predicate over the parent's enumerated feasible bundles.
Predicates that reject nothing, reject everything, or reject the same bundles as a
differently named predicate are refused. Independent acquisition receives the option
vocabulary but not the candidate predicates. Deterministic replay then enumerates every
candidate subset and accepts only one inclusion-minimal explanation of admitted,
excluded, and implication examples. A solver may certify feasibility or reserve
disjoint holdout identities; it does not decide whether the business claim is true.

For security selection, every selected company can enter a six-arm closed-book episode:
zero-edge, momentum, valuation, typed quantitative underwriting, quantitative plus
business fingerprint, and the full research packet. This decomposition asks whether
additional business understanding improves later return forecasts rather than merely
whether one final model happened to be right.

For the probability-current lane, the cross-sectional state is a dated market object.
The compiler searches backward from the latest visible date for the latest session with
the declared minimum breadth and history, records every skipped incomplete session, and
refuses a complete session older than the declared staleness limit. The discovery clock
refreshes one adjusted-price source for every member of the registered prospective
cohort before the next episode can open.

Source clocks remain atomic across the whole loop. If institutional learning or another
worker advances the public-source epoch while a capital cycle is compiling, the cycle
returns to discovery before downstream publication. Forecast episodes already sealed
against the earlier packet remain immutable, but no mixed-epoch capital bundle is
published.

The first breadth-complete market-flow episode demonstrates the intended learning
unit. Thirty-seven declared equities and ETFs were refreshed at one retrieval epoch;
the latest complete cross-section was sealed before outcomes against persistence,
empirical Markov, monotone odd-current calibration, and a Lagrangian probability-current
challenger. The challenger disagreed directionally with one control and materially in
density with all three, but the episode remains `prospective_pending`. Disagreement buys
measurement attention; only later settlement can buy predictive credit.

Operational speed is part of the learning contract because a slow projection starves
experiments. The completed 100-candidate / 29-qualified epoch originally spent 259.069
seconds rebuilding the workbench. Profiling found repeated household-world enumeration,
source-manifest parsing, paper-watch verification, factor-price scans, and graph-shape
canonicalization. Typed-input and filesystem-epoch caches plus exact graph-shape
memoization reduced the measured full projection to 47.868 seconds without removing a
section or changing authority. A 611-case metamorphic check rejected a faster graph
refinement that would have changed historical phenotype identities.

Market repricing and business evidence move on different clocks. A monitored dossier
may therefore cover a later candidate epoch without being copied or relabelled. The
strategy scheduler represents that relation as `monitored_dossier_coverage`: a new
frontier request binds the prior source request and dossier, the current routing
candidate, and the exact Golden Store coverage leaf that joins them. The request is
admissible only while that coverage leaf still resolves to the same admissible dossier.
This lets recursive strategy experiments survive price-only churn while a changed
filing, revoked dossier, or different coverage head stops the lineage.
