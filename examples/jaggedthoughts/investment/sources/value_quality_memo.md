# Fictional value-quality decision memo

This reference packet is synthetic. It exists to demonstrate a point-in-time
investment workflow and does not describe a listed issuer.

At the decision timestamp, Alpha Components is modeled with an eight-percent
free-cash-flow yield, eighteen-percent return on invested capital, moderate
leverage, and meaningful customer concentration. The base mechanism assumes
customer concentration declines without margin damage. The stress mechanism
assumes concentration persists and reduces the expected compounding path.

The market-state committee deliberately preserves disagreement among a
historical estimate, an implied estimate, and a valuation-conditioned estimate.
The paper play permits a zero, five-percent, or twelve-percent weight, charges
fifteen basis points on traded notional, and settles later against both a total
market benchmark and the frozen no-action book.

For the reference underwriting challenge, the outside-view comparison is a
concentrated component supplier attempting to diversify a dominant customer
within one investment horizon. The illustrative base rate is 35 percent. The
next-best alternative is the existing total-market and cash sleeves, and the
declared hurdle is six percent expected excess return. The rival view treats
the reported cash yield as temporarily elevated. Two consecutive reporting
periods of lower concentration with stable margin and cash conversion are the
decisive observation.
