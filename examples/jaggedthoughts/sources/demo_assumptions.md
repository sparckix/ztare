# Integrated service-packaging decision inputs

Decision epoch: 2026-08-08. These inputs demonstrate the source-binding and
factor-evaluation workflow. Replace them with dated interview notes, market

The focused segment has sufficient willingness to pay for a specialized offer.

The broad segment can be served without destructive dilution of the offer.

The organization can operate a direct channel at the required scale.

Partner delivery preserves the required customer and pricing control.

Subscription retention persists after a modeled competitor response.

Usage pricing remains attractive under modeled demand and cost volatility.

In the base case, arena, channel, and economic-logic contributions are additive except for the two explicitly declared complementarity bundles.

In the distribution-disruption case, partner control weakens while direct-channel control becomes more valuable.
