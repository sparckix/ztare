# Autonomous service strategy reference evidence

The decision concerns whether to expand through a partner pilot or build a direct channel.

The tracked state coordinates are contribution margin, channel control, market reach, competitor pressure, and partner support.

In the low-pressure observation, the partner pilot reduced margin by 2, increased reach by 10, and increased partner support by 5.

A live mechanism hypothesis predicts that high competitor pressure triggers a stronger response against a partner pilot than against a direct channel.

The decision objectives are to preserve contribution margin, channel control, and market reach across surviving mechanisms.

A bounded pilot readout can distinguish the two current mechanism candidates before the larger channel commitment.
