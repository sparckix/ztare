# Neural Hunt — Public Claim Summary (Closed Negative Campaign)

> Public-evidence surface for the out-of-loop neural-scaling audit
> campaign. Working directory private (charter + ~670 workspace
> artifacts); this summary is the canonical public artifact cited by
> [`docs/public_claim_register.md`](../../../docs/public_claim_register.md)
> under *Neural Scaling And Mechanistic Audits*.

## One-line claim (a sealed negative)

The campaign that re-audited the neural-scaling material behind the
broader neural-law work **closed negative** on every contemporary
raw-train-loss law candidate tested (H-01 through H-12). The durable
public evidence is the failure record and the corresponding catalogue
of retired hypotheses.

## What was tested

Closed-artifact re-audit, no live ZTARE loop, of the prospective
neural-law candidates against modern LLM training/eval telemetry. The
target was *endpoint-free predictive evidence* — a stronger criterion
than full-curve normalized morphology — on exact OLMo2 1B raw
history and on downstream-eval W&B packets for OLMo2 7B / 13B.

## What closed negative

- **H-05.** The slope-anchored extrapolation rule was killed on
  exact OLMo2 1B raw history.
- **H-06.** The exchange-rate reframe (predict local contraction
  rates rather than future tail levels) also failed on the same
  no-overlap rows.
- **H-07.** A real downstream-eval observation site (W&B telemetry)
  was identified, but simple optimizer telemetry was rejected as the
  missing state variable: gradient norm wins `0/6` required 20%
  margins on the 7B packet; train CE also wins `0/6`.
- **H-08, H-09.** Remaining same-packet residuals failed: eval-vector
  manifold prediction and matched stage-2 branch response both fail
  promotion.
- **H-11.** The void was reframed as a *measurement-channel
  boundary* — accuracy eval targets carry finite denominator
  structure; CE targets are smoother but too persistence-dominated at
  the logged cadence.
- **H-12.** Converted the H-11 diagnosis into an *observability gate*:
  `0/12` H-07/H-08 same-packet candidates clear
  `max(20% baseline MAE, two accuracy quanta)`.

## What is on the durable retired-or-suspect list

- `β = 1/φ` as a named-constant law.
- Raw local-α as an externally admissible production observable.
- Optimizer-control phase flow as a production OLMo law.
- Any success criterion that depends on knowing the held-out curve's
  future minimum or amplitude.
- Online or cross-family law language from the current `mlfoundations`
  packet.
- Scalar exchange-rate rescue on the same raw train-loss packet.
- Simple gradient-norm / learning-rate / clipping eval rescue on the
  same public OLMo2 packet.

## The first gate (for any future neural work)

Do not run another raw train-loss curve-fitting audit unless it
introduces a *genuinely new observable or source class*. The next
admissible eigenquestion must name cleaner eval/validation telemetry,
explicit restart or dataloader-state markers, controlled
production-style logs, or a non-raw-train-loss observable. It should
not be another transformation of the same OLMo raw train CE tail.

## Honest framing — this is a discipline win, not a law

The campaign produced *no* neural law. It produced (a) a clean
scientific object — "evaluator-response transfer under a typed
measurement contract" — and (b) a hardened measurement pipeline. The
public-claim contribution is the catalogue of demotions, not the
discovery of a law. The next gate is staged but not executed.

## Retest tag

*Negative result on retest (across H-01 through H-12).* Each demotion
is durable evidence — re-running the same candidates under the same
protocol would not promote them. Only a different observable / source
class can move the verdict; that is the standing open question for
future work.
