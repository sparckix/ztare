# GLP-1 Adoption Economics — Web Research
Compiled: 2026-04-13 | Sources: BCBS, Mercer, KFF, EBRI, CMS, UHC

## Adoption Rate Data
- 2023: 6.9% prescription claim share (GLP-1 as % of all Rx claims)
- 2025: 10.5% prescription claim share
- 2022: Only 3% of non-elderly adults with employer insurance had a GLP-1 claim
- 2024 consumer survey: 1 in 8 respondents had EVER taken a GLP-1
- 57 million+ privately insured adults clinically qualify for GLP-1 drugs

## Employer Coverage Trends (Critical Payer Coverage Data)
- 2024: 44% of employers with 500+ employees covered weight loss GLP-1s
- 2025: 19% of firms with 200+ workers; 43% of firms with 5,000+ workers
- Year-over-year growth in large employer coverage: 28% (2024) → 43% (2025) for firms >5,000
- Source: KFF employer health benefits survey, Mercer annual survey

## Premium Impact Range (More Nuanced Than the 14% Headline)
- EBRI simulation analysis: Premium increase ranges from 5.3% to 13.8%
  — Depends on adherence assumptions, cost-sharing design, and eligibility criteria
- Hypothetical lower-price scenario ($200/month GLP-1): premium increase only 1% to 3.9%
- Current GLP-1 net cost: up to $766/month (net of discounts and rebates — not list price)
- Source: EBRI "GLP-1 Coverage and Its Impact on Employment-Based Health Plan Premiums" 
  https://www.ebri.org/content/glp-1-coverage-and-its-impact-on-employment-based-health-plan-premiums--a-simulation-based-analysis

## Medicare Coverage Status
- Medicare GLP-1 Bridge program: CMS has an interim bridge program
  https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/prescription-drug-coverage/medicare-glp-1-bridge
- Full Medicare Part D obesity coverage: January 2027 (this is the Step Function inflection point)
- Currently: Medicare covers GLP-1 for T2D and cardiovascular indication, NOT obesity alone

## Access Gap Data
- GLP-1 Access Gap: affordability varies significantly by state
- Source: Health Management Academy state-level access gap analysis
  https://hmacademy.com/blog/glp1-access-gap-affordability-by-state
- Medicaid coverage: only 13 states cover for obesity (FFS) as of Jan 2026

## Key Thesis Calibration Points
- Premium threshold: EBRI narrows the 14% BCBS headline to 5.3%-13.8% range
  - Model should use this RANGE not a point estimate
  - The lower bound (5.3%) would fire at much lower penetration than 14% implies
- Price elasticity: $200/month → 1-4% premium impact vs $766/month → 5-14%
  - The 70% Novo price cut matters ONLY IF payers pass through to patient out-of-pocket
  - Net cost to insured patient depends on formulary placement, not list price
- Large employer leading indicator: 43% of >5K firms covering in 2025 = adoption already accelerating
  in the payer-coverage channel, not just clinical demand channel
