## Novo Nordisk GLP-1 U.S. Price Cuts: Factual Summary

### Pre-Cut Baseline Prices


Wegovy's list price was approximately $1,350/month, while Ozempic and Rybelsus ran at approximately $1,000/month.

 

Health economists at ICER estimated that current net prices of about $7,000–$9,000 per year (roughly $600–$750/month) already sat within the usual U.S. cost-effectiveness range for obesity treatment — roughly half to two-thirds of original list prices.



---

### February 2026: Major WAC List Price Cuts (Effective Jan. 1, 2027)


Novo announced plans to cut the U.S. wholesale acquisition cost (WAC) of its semaglutide products to **$675/month**, effective January 1, 2027, applying to the full suite including both injectable and oral Wegovy, injectable Ozempic, and oral Rybelsus.

 

For Wegovy, this represents a **50% reduction** from its current list price; for Ozempic, it is a **35% reduction**.





Notably, the new list prices do not affect direct-to-patient, self-pay prescriptions. Citi analyst Geoffrey Meacham noted the move will likely benefit only a small portion of new prescriptions, since cash-pay channels now hold an "overwhelming share."



---

### November 2025: Trump/TrumpRx Deal & Medicare/Medicaid Pricing


President Trump announced deals with Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk to lower GLP-1 drug prices on Medicare and Medicaid, and to offer treatments directly to consumers at a discount on a new platform called **TrumpRx.gov**.



Key price points from the deal:
- 

Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly agreed to cut the price Medicare pays for GLP-1s to **$245/month**, extending that government price across all 50 Medicaid programs for all covered uses.


- 

Certain Medicare patients will pay a copay of **$50/month** for approved uses of injectable and oral GLP-1 drugs.


- 

Starting doses of upcoming obesity pills will be **$149/month** for everyone getting them through Medicare, Medicaid, or TrumpRx.


- 

Consumers buying injectable GLP-1 medications directly from the companies will pay an average of **$350/month** to start, with drugmakers committed to reducing that price to about **$250** over the next two years.



---

### November 2025: Self-Pay Channel Price Cuts


Novo offered the first two monthly doses of Wegovy and Ozempic for **$199** (expiring end of March), and reduced the self-pay price for other patients to **$349/month**, down from $499/month.

 

For comparison, compounding pharmacy Hims & Hers charges as little as $199/month for compounded semaglutide.
