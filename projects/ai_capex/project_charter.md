# Project Charter: AI CapEx

## Research Question

Can the AI infrastructure capital cycle destroy shareholder value for major
hyperscalers by 2028, and what observable accounting or unit-economics signal
would prove the thesis wrong?

## Scope

- Horizon: 2026-2028.
- Companies in scope: hyperscalers with material AI infrastructure CapEx and
  public reporting lines.
- Evidence in scope: filings, CapEx guidance, depreciation policy, utilization
  assumptions, pricing pressure, demand indicators, and source-backed unit
  economics.
- Out of scope: generic AI-bubble commentary, NVIDIA-only demand narratives,
  and claims that cannot be tied to a filing, pricing, utilization, or cash-flow
  metric.

## Success Criteria

1. The thesis states a concrete value-destruction mechanism rather than a broad
   bearish narrative.
2. The unit-economics case can be checked from independently cited inputs:
   utilization, depreciation life, pricing, margin, and cash payback period.
3. The capital-cycle timing is tied to a specific filing window or guidance
   update.
4. The thesis names kill criteria that would force revision, including demand
   absorption, pricing resilience, or utilization remaining above the stated
   threshold.

## Known Evidence Gaps

- Realized AI accelerator utilization by hyperscaler.
- Segment-level AI infrastructure pricing and realized discounting.
- Depreciation schedules and useful-life changes specific to AI equipment.
- Evidence separating strategic overbuild from structurally bad unit economics.
- Company-specific reporting triggers that would make impairment or guidance
  cuts visible to outside reviewers.
