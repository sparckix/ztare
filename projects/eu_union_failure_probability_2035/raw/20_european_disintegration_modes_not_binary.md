Title: The Causes and Modes of European Disintegration
URL: https://www.cogitatiopress.com/politicsandgovernance/article/view/7163
Date: 2023-07-05

Claim / relevance:
- This source directly targets the champion gap on event ontology robustness.
- It is relevant because it argues that European disintegration has multiple actual and potential modes, not just a simple intact/failure binary.

Key facts / excerpts:
- The editorial frames two questions: the causes of potential European disintegration and the actual/potential modes of European disintegration.
- It explicitly says the second question has been largely overlooked.
- It says the literature should go beyond Brexit, differentiated integration, and non-compliance alone.
- It highlights issues such as temporary opt-outs from Schengen as modes of disintegration that are not equivalent to total EU collapse.
- The piece presents disintegration as a family of modes rather than a single terminal event.

Why this matters for probability:
- This is strong external support for the firing-squad objection that there may be legitimate intermediate states between full intactness and full failure.
- It suggests the current probability project should not assume complementarity is trivial; it must explain why its binary event boundary is still acceptable despite a wider mode-space.
- In practice, this source pushes the ontology toward either:
  - a stricter, narrower definition of `material_union_failure`, or
  - an explicit explanation for why intermediate disintegrative modes still map to `formal_intactness` for this horizon.
