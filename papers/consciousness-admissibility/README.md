# Consciousness Admissibility

Public mirror for:

**When a Consciousness Verdict Cannot Be Earned: A Descent-Theoretic Admissibility Criterion**

The manuscript treats consciousness attribution as an identification problem: a verdict is scientifically licensed only when the target property is recoverable from a declared observation site. It makes precise a limitative criterion — *revision-stable non-effective descent* — for cases where that recovery fails necessarily, surviving every admissible revision of the measurement language. The finite factorization is proved and its key components machine-checked in Lean 4; the general reduction is isolated as an explicit open obligation. The paper is primarily philosophy of science and philosophy of mind, with category-theoretic / formal-methods machinery used to make the criterion precise.
