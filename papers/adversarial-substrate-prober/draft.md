## Abstract

A substrate prober is an instrument that tells a researcher what a body of data or a decision process can and cannot answer — before that researcher commits to a theoretical framework. This paper reports what happens when a single LLM-driven adversarial symbolic-regression apparatus, Zero-Trust Adversarial Recursive Evaluation (hereafter "the apparatus"), is applied as a substrate prober across four independent scientific and governance domains: neural scaling-law exponents, Navier-Stokes singularity diagnostics, modified-gravity field response, and consciousness-ascription governance.

The operating principal is not a domain expert in any of the four fields. The language models generated the domain synthesis — the physical interpretations, the candidate formalisms, the mathematical derivations. The principal's role was strictly automated epistemic falsification discipline: gate design, pre-registration, inversion logic, and explicit self-demotion of wrong causal stories. This division of labor is the paper's structural premise and is made explicit throughout.

In the **neural** domain, gauge-removed training-loss trajectories from OLMo2 7B and 13B production telemetry collapse onto a power-family shape (point-MAE 0.054, integrated-segment MAE 0.252, six-of-six holdout splits beating baseline at 2.4×). That positive does not promote to a cross-architecture law. The optimizer-control phase-flow law derived from toy-transformer grids anti-transfers to production training; adding optimizer-control features to a working trajectory-only model degrades every holdout split by +18% to +23,684% MAE. A later exact OLMo2 1B acquisition closes the strongest endpoint-free slope-anchor promotion path negatively, a direct local exchange-rate reframe also fails, and downstream-eval audits find usable eval telemetry but reject simple optimizer telemetry, eval-vector manifold prediction, and a late stage-2 branch-response hint as promotable state variables. The residual void is an eval-instrument boundary: several accuracy metrics expose finite denominator structure, while CE metrics are smoother but still persistence-dominated at the logged cadence. An observability-gate audit then finds that `0/12` same-packet candidate rows clear `max(20% baseline MAE, two accuracy quanta)`. A public aggregate OLMES fork then shifts the next question from curve fitting to targeted measurement: high-motion tasks can be selected, but a law claim still needs per-instance checkpoint evidence. Across 14 (study, modality, scaling-variable) groups, the empirical Hölder regularity exponent β is consistent with β = 1/2 (MAE 0.050), with pooled β = 0.486 ± 0.083 SEM, but direct measurement falsifies strict universality. A golden-ratio candidate (β = 1/φ) generated by the apparatus via PSLQ on a small sample is explicitly refuted by cross-modality testing.

In the **Navier-Stokes** domain, a published-paper-grade methodology error is surfaced: the bounded near-miss in fixed-resolution spectral simulations is consistent with Galerkin-truncation artifact (one-cell-limited terminal geometry near the spectral edge) rather than physical singular-core survival. A signed-alignment discriminator survives matched-geometry stress through N = 128. A later theorem-compression pass isolates a material-frame metric-degeneracy frontier: exponential strain-aligned metric rescue requires hnorm >= R − 1 and lambdaMin <= exp(1 − R) at material-frame transport ratio R. Subsequent exact Fourier audits compress the finite-mode pressure route to a sharper boundary: pressure-blind scalar/radial stencils are tail-dominated, while Leray-aware matrix intertwiners create only a signed local coordinate unless a pointwise same-ledger block inequality fails after damping and residual-defect costs are paired.

In the **modified-gravity** domain, a row-wise RAR campaign returns a substrate-bounded null: exhaustive symbolic-regression across 17 candidate functional families on SPARC + cluster + wide-binary data does not unify the regimes at MAE < 0.5, and the diagnosis is within-class feature collapse at the cross-class joints. In a repaired 3D AQUAL-style field-slice sandbox, a diffuse UDG-like source exhibits a tensor-orientation susceptibility well compressed by χ(θ) = χ₀ + χ₄ cos(4θ), while a compact binary-like control is essentially flat. A later local invariant search sharpens the live science object: not raw phase law, but a representation-stable diffuse susceptibility amplitude in an internal-share observable family. The result remains an instrument-audit / numerical-methods finding — not a MOND orientation law.

In the **consciousness-ascription governance** domain, the apparatus first recovered a high-scoring pluralism thesis diagnosed as training-corpus overdetermination, then retired it under an alien-substrate/no-citation reframe. The surviving result is the Adversarial Identification Certificate for the Measurement-Channel Veto Protocol (AID-MCVP): a low-concern verdict on a substrate of unknown consciousness is forbidden unless intervention access, independent replication, an invertible predicate bridge, and an adversarial completeness certificate make the target property identifiable from admissible measurements.

The contribution is not domain-expert-grade discovery in any single field, nor a methodology-without-substance demonstration. It is the third thing: defensible cross-domain results produced by disciplined non-expert operation of an adversarial falsification apparatus, with the apparatus's own wrong causal stories documented and retired in the same paper.

## 1. Introduction

Four substrates, one apparatus, one operating principal, one paper. This is a cross-domain capability paper. It reports what the same LLM-driven adversarial symbolic-regression apparatus produced when applied to three structurally unrelated quantitative science substrates and one qualitative governance substrate by a principal whose domain depth in fluid dynamics, modified gravity, neural-scaling-law theory, and consciousness studies is bounded. The argument is not that the apparatus replaces domain experts. It is that a non-expert principal operating an apparatus under a specific automated epistemic falsification discipline produces defensible results that catch real methodology errors, surface real empirical regularities, and self-demote the apparatus's own wrong causal stories.

The four case studies are not equally mature, nor do they make the same kind of claim. The neural case (§3.1) produces a bounded empirical regularity inside OLMo2 7B/13B production data, four clean negative results (optimizer-control phase-flow does not transfer from toy-substrate fits to production, exact OLMo2 1B falsifies the endpoint-free slope-anchor promotion path, a scalar local exchange-rate reframe fails, and downstream-eval telemetry does not promote the tested state-variable candidates), plus a measurement-channel diagnosis and a cross-modality empirical anchor (β ≈ 0.486 ± 0.083 SEM consistent with the Brownian/Matérn-1 GP regularity class via the Havrilla-Liao 2024 form, but not universal). The Navier-Stokes case (§3.2) produces a structural diagnostic (the bounded near-miss in finite-resolution spectral simulations is Galerkin-truncation-limited), a frontier discriminator (signed-alignment surviving matched-geometry stress through N = 128), a theorem-facing material-frame metric-degeneracy frontier, and a finite-mode pressure-channel pruning result whose remaining live object is a pointwise same-ledger block inequality rather than more packet discovery. The modified-gravity case (§3.3) produces two results at different layers: a row-wise RAR substrate null with a within-class feature-collapse diagnosis, and a later repaired-PDE-sandbox susceptibility object bounded as an instrument-audit result, with the live observable now narrowed to an internal-share family rather than raw phase law. The consciousness-ascription case (§3.4) produces a governance/identification result: behavioral or disclosed-record equivalence is insufficient for a low-concern verdict unless the target property is identifiable through an adversarially certified measurement channel. We report each domain's contribution at the maturity it has actually earned, not above.

The unifying claim is methodological. We frame the apparatus as a **substrate prober**: an instrument that tells a researcher what a body of data or decision substrate can and cannot answer, before that researcher commits to a theoretical framework. In each case study, the apparatus answered a "what is this substrate's binding constraint" question. In the neural domain, the binding constraint is now fivefold: toy-substrate optimizer correlations do not transfer to production training, raw public train-loss telemetry supports bounded trajectory morphology better than endpoint-free law promotion, the current public eval packet is too persistence-dominated to support a state-variable claim without richer lineage or independent checkpoint evidence, the eval targets themselves impose a resolution/cadence boundary, and public aggregate eval is useful for targeting but insufficient for law promotion without per-instance checkpoint evidence. In the Navier-Stokes domain, the binding constraint on the bounded-near-miss interpretation is the spectral-edge proximity at fixed resolution. In the modified-gravity domain, the row-wise binding constraint is within-class feature collapse on the aggregated public data, and the later PDE-sandbox binding constraint is representation invariance together with the need for a declared internal-share observable rather than a raw phase statistic. In the consciousness-ascription domain, the binding constraint is identification: behavior and disclosed audit records do not identify the target property under gatekeeper-controlled selection unless the measurement channel is intervention-accessible, independently replicated, bridge-calibrated, and adversarially complete. These constraints differ, but the apparatus's discipline surfaced each one; the methodology contribution is showing this is reproducible across domains under the same operating regime.

This paper has the following contributions, ordered by tier of confidence:

1. **Tier 1 — empirical, externally stress-tested:** the neural trajectory-shape regularity on OLMo2 7B/13B production telemetry, validated by two independent gauge-removal metrics and bounded by exact OLMo2 1B failure of the slope-anchor promotion path, a direct scalar exchange-rate failure, downstream-eval source-packet audits that reject the tested same-packet state-variable rescues, an eval-resolution/cadence diagnosis, an observability-gate audit with `0/12` same-packet candidate rows clearing the floor, and a public aggregate OLMES fork that identifies a targeted per-instance successor packet without promoting a law; the cleanly retired optimizer-control phase-flow as a non-transferring inference; the cross-modality empirical anchor β ≈ 1/2 across 14 groups under the Havrilla-Liao 2024 form, with universality explicitly denied.

## 2. The Apparatus

The apparatus is a mutator-judge loop. Each iteration runs four stages. Stage one is mutator-side proposal: a frontier language model is given the substrate description and the rubric, and is asked to produce a closed-form parametric law along with a thesis prose. Stage two is fitting: scipy.optimize fits the free parameters of the law against the visible training rows under a Bayesian information criterion budget. Stage three is gate evaluation: a deterministic harness runs the fitted law against the held-out set and a stack of structural anti-pattern gates. Stage four is judge scoring: a separate language model from a different family scores the thesis against the rubric, with access to the form, the thesis, and the gate outcome but not the mutator's chain-of-thought.

### 2.1 Division of labor: what the principal does, what the LLM does

The non-expert paradox requires an explicit resolution. The paper contains references to Čech cohomology, Galerkin truncation, Bekenstein-Milgrom nonlinearities, and Hölder regularity — this is not the work of a domain expert. The language models generated all domain synthesis: the physical interpretations, the candidate formalisms, the algebraic derivations, and the identification of candidate Lagrangians. Every piece of domain-specific physics named in this paper — the chameleon scalar field with quartic self-interaction, the AQUAL interpolating function, the Banik-Zhao multiplicative external-field effect, the Euler-Lagrange derivations — came from language models and cited literature, not from the principal.

The principal's role was restricted to four functions:

**Gate design.** The principal decides what deterministic checks run on every iteration — which structural properties a candidate law must satisfy, and what the acceptance thresholds are. Gate design is epistemic engineering, not domain knowledge.

**Pre-registration.** Every substantive claim requires a pre-registered prediction or acceptance threshold authored before the result that confirms or refutes it is seen. Pre-registration prevents post-hoc re-interpretation of fitted constants as earned results.

**Inversion logic.** When the apparatus proposes a result, the principal asks: what would need to be true for this to be wrong? That inversion question becomes the next falsification probe. The apparatus's own enthusiasm is the primary target of inversion. This move requires no domain knowledge; it requires willingness to generate hostile tests against the apparatus's own outputs.

**Authorization of self-demotion events.** When the apparatus produces a result that does not survive its own subsequent audit, the principal authorizes the demotion and ensures it is documented in the same paper as the original result. Demotion without documentation is the failure mode this discipline exists to prevent.

This division of labor is the "third thing" framing: neither domain-expert discovery nor raw LLM output, but disciplined non-expert operation of an adversarial falsification apparatus. The LLMs supply the domain content; the principal supplies the falsification architecture.

Stated as an operating loop, the division of labor has four roles. First, the generator produces a candidate object: a law, theorem packet, bridge, discriminator, or failure explanation. Second, deterministic and algebraic machinery close the cheap formal vulnerabilities around that object: fitting, exact checks, symbolic identities, typed gates, and held-out tests. Third, a compression or classification pass asks what class of object has actually survived, so that a local win is not mispromoted into a stronger category than it earned. Fourth, the principal acts as capital allocator and promotion gate, advancing only objects that materially change the live belief graph and demoting them when the next hostile probe breaks them. That four-role loop is the compact operational rendering of the "third thing" claim in this paper.

### 2.2 The falsification gate stack

Gates are deterministic checks that run on every iteration against a candidate law. A gate takes a parametric form, the fitted parameters, and the substrate, and produces a binary verdict (pass/fail) plus a structured diagnostic. Gates prevent the LLM from confirming whatever it was asked to confirm by enforcing structural constraints the LLM cannot evade through prose.

Structural anti-pattern gates (R20-R24) flag persona-leaked anchors, hardcoded class-label hacks, parameter laundering against absent classes, and forms whose branches reduce to one another at default parameter values (hidden universality). These were tightened during the 2026-04-26 cycle following an incident in which numeric values from the rubric prose appeared in the candidate form as fitted constants.

The **feature-collapse gate** (falsification gate R26) is the key example of substrate-legibility gating. It flags any candidate law whose input features include axes with near-zero within-class variance on any extrapolation class. Concretely, R26 inspects the parametric form, identifies the axes the form depends on, and queries the substrate for the per-class within-class range of each. If a feature is depended-on by the form and is degenerate on a class for which the form is asked to predict, R26 fires. The gate's output is not a yes-or-no acceptance but a structured diagnostic naming the (feature, class) pair and its implication. This gate makes substrate-data ceilings legible: it tells the apparatus when the substrate's data structure — not the apparatus's search — is the binding constraint.

The gate stack runs in milliseconds. Its controlling property is determinism: the same form on the same substrate always produces the same gate verdicts, regardless of which LLM proposed the form or how the thesis was framed. This determinism is what makes gates falsification instruments rather than scored preferences.

### 2.3 Corpus-gradient suppression and cold-shot diversity

The apparatus bans domain vocabulary in cold-shot seeds. When an LLM is given a domain description and asked to propose forms, it retrieves training-corpus consensus rather than performing structural reasoning. Forbidding domain vocabulary forces the LLM to import structure from orthogonal fields. This is the primary defense against the apparatus recapitulating training-corpus mainstream at high score.

Cold-shot seeds use different model families (Gemini, GPT, Claude) at different points in the search. Parametric-memory retrieval from one model family is not shared with another; structural diversity across model families is the cold-shot panel's purpose. In the neural domain, the cold-shot's forbidden-domain list explicitly excluded "machine learning, deep learning, neural-network scaling, AI, scaling laws, Bahri, Sharma-Kaplan" to prevent retrieval of the published α = 4/d prediction. In the consciousness domain, cold shots were forbidden from referencing consciousness philosophy, philosophy of mind, formal epistemology, identification theory, and moral philosophy — forcing the apparatus into algebraic topology, gauge theory, and statistical physics as structural source fields.

Multi-model cold-shot diversity is distinct from iterating the same model. The five-model convergence on PMOND v5 in the gravity domain (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, and a second Gemini session converging on the same Hill-saturated MOND + multiplicative EFE structure) is evidence of a structural attractor in the substrate, not a single model's parametric memory. Convergence across model families is stronger evidence than convergence within a single model across iterations.

### 2.4 Pre-registration and self-demotion

Every substantive claim must be pre-registered before the data that confirms or refutes it is seen. Pre-registration in this apparatus takes two forms: (1) acceptance thresholds written into the gate harness before a run launches, and (2) written predictions about what a result would look like if the hypothesis is true, authored before the test that checks it.

Self-demotion is the discipline of documenting, in the same paper as the original result, any apparatus output that does not survive its own subsequent audit. The 1/φ golden-ratio recovery is the canonical example in this paper: the apparatus generated a candidate β = 1/φ via PSLQ on a small sample, partially supported it within controlled image data, and then refuted it under cross-modality testing with a theoretically-anchored alternative. This sequence — generate, partially support, refute — is documented explicitly rather than silently replaced with the surviving result. A reader who sees only the β = 1/2 finding does not see the apparatus's self-correction at work.

Self-demotion is not a concession. It is the evidence that the apparatus is operating under discipline rather than optimizing for compelling-sounding outputs.

### 2.5 The two improvement loops

The apparatus has two improvement loops, not one.

The **algorithmic layer** (apparatus self-improvement) covers the gate stack, AST-distance enforcement, structural anti-pattern gates, and meta-gates that audit the gate stack rather than the candidate forms. When a run caps for an apparatus reason — a missing structural check, a scope-narrowed gate, a calibration anchor leaking from prompt to form — the algorithmic layer is the right place to act.

The **knowledge substrate** loop (substrate self-improvement) covers data enrichment, feature engineering, and coverage expansion. When a run caps because the substrate lacks the variance the search needs — as the feature-collapse gate makes explicit — the knowledge substrate loop is the right place to act.

The recurring meta-failure mode is conflating the two. The gravity v2 stuck-at-50 was initially read as an apparatus failure when it was a substrate failure: the substrate did not contain the variance any joint interpolation form needed. The post-run audit function is the routing decision: it mines run logs for the pattern of a gate engaging without ever flagging, and diagnoses whether the binding constraint is the apparatus or the substrate.

## 3. Four Stress Tests

### 3.1 Neural Scaling Exponents

#### 3.1.1 The substrate

We use OLMo2 7B and 13B production training-loss telemetry, exported from public W&B logs by the AllenAI OLMo project. Each row is a (training step, training-cross-entropy) pair across the full Stage-1 pretraining of the corresponding model, spanning approximately 9 × 10⁵ steps for 7B and 6 × 10⁵ steps for 13B, covering roughly 3.9 × 10¹² and 5.0 × 10¹² training tokens respectively.

A gauge-removal protocol normalizes the loss to z = (L − L_min) / (L_max − L_min) per curve and the axis to a normalized progress coordinate r ∈ [0, 1]. The resulting (z, r) pairs live on a comparable surface across runs, and the question becomes whether the shape of one trajectory predicts the shape of another after removing per-curve floor-and-amplitude gauges. This is a structurally different question from scaling-law fits: it substitutes a curve-comparison problem for a final-loss-comparison problem.

The binding constraint on the optimizer-control story turned out to be that the toy-substrate correlations encode toy-substrate-specific structure absent from production training — not a missing form family, and not a gate failure.

#### 3.1.2 The apparatus failure and correction

The trajectory-shape collapse held within OLMo2 7B/13B: at the point-level, the gauge-removed trajectory admits a power-family shape (all-row MAE 0.0535, p90 0.1242). At the integrated-segment level, six holdout splits all beat a train-mean baseline at MAE 0.252 versus 0.597, a 2.4× margin. Two independent metrics agree on the same bounded evidence.

The apparatus then required the optimizer-control phase-flow law — learned on controlled toy-transformer grids where learning rate and batch size are crossed in a 5×5 grid — to transfer to production OLMo2 telemetry. The result was not merely failure but anti-transfer. When optimizer-control features are added to a working trajectory-only model, the prediction worsens across every holdout split, by margins ranging from +18% to +23,684% MAE relative to the trajectory-only model. The full feature set passes only 2 of 6 splits; the optimizer-only set passes 0 of 6. Cross-corpus stratification fails in both directions. The apparatus's interpretation: the optimizer-control law learned on the toy substrate is anti-informative on production data, not merely uninformative. The law is retired from production-law claims.

The later exact-1B audit was the stricter test. The public W&B report context exposed exact sampled-history pages for OLMo2 1B Stage 1, yielding 2,356,360 train-cross-entropy rows across 14 internally consecutive run segments. The predeclared H-05 test trained the external shape template on OLMo2 7B/13B and asked whether the boundary-slope coordinate predicted exact 1B future loss without seeing the future endpoint. It failed. Segment-level fixed-count online scored 0/28 all-baseline wins; known-horizon progress scored 1/28 all-baseline wins. A median-stitched diagnostic over the 1,907,359 unique global steps also failed. A no-overlap rescue test, dropping every globally duplicated step, still failed: 2/24 fixed-count online all-baseline wins and 3/24 known-horizon all-baseline wins.

The restart structure then became the residual-void audit rather than a rescue. Exact 1B contains 448,207 duplicate global steps across restart overlaps. Long same-step overlaps are effectively single-valued after smoothing: the two largest overlap pairs have branch/local ratios near 1.0. The final short restart seam is noisy in raw train CE, but auxiliary metrics show no learning-rate jump, similar gradient norms, a throughput change, sign-changing CE deltas, and no validation CE rows in the tested report context. Ordinary duplicate-step noise is far smaller than the H-05 error scale: median same-step raw range is 0.0001886 and p99 is 0.000894, while H-05 errors live at roughly 0.026 to 0.045. The conclusion is not that restarts explain the law. It is that raw train-loss restart seams must be treated as source-contamination objects unless eval loss or dataloader-state evidence says otherwise.

We then tested whether the failure came from looking at the wrong observation
site. A W&B source inventory found downstream-eval telemetry on OLMo2 Stage-1
runs. Targeted acquisition yielded 1,850 rows across four 7B runs, or 925
unique run-step rows after deduplication, plus a sparse 13B packet. This passed
the source gate but not the scientific gate. In chronological next-eval

zero of six required 20% wins over persistence and local-linear baselines.
Train CE also achieved zero of six. Downstream eval at this resolution is
mostly persistence/local-trend dominated. The audit leaves two negative facts:
raw train loss was the wrong target for promotion, and simple optimizer
telemetry did not supply the missing state.

Two residual checks probed the remaining obvious source-state interpretations.
First, the eval-vector check asked whether sibling eval metrics could forecast
a withheld next eval metric under chronological holdout. Sibling eval, sibling
eval plus support telemetry, and full eval plus support all achieved zero of six
required 20% wins. Second, the late stage-2 branch check tested three matched

The last audits mined the measurement channel itself. ARC Easy accuracy exposes

Those accuracy metrics do move across 1,000-step intervals, but their changes

H-07/H-08 candidate rows cleared the per-metric floor
`max(20% baseline MAE, two accuracy quanta)`. A later public aggregate OLMES

dataset contains late-stage aggregate OLMo2 1B eval movement across `225`

#### 3.1.3 The invariant threshold

The trajectory-shape collapse stands as a bounded OLMo2-family empirical regularity, not as a promoted scaling law. The gauge-removed OLMo2 7B/13B result is consistent across two independent metrics and across model sizes within that family. Exact 1B closes the strongest endpoint-free slope-anchor extension negatively. A direct local exchange-rate objective also fails on the same exact 1B source after duplicate-step overlaps are removed. The downstream-eval audits broaden the site, then close the tested same-packet state-variable rescues and expose the eval-instrument boundary.

A successor source-design pass does not change that claim. It ranks independent
checkpoint evaluation and controlled lineage perturbation as the next admissible
source classes, then seals a 34-job OLMo2 1B checkpoint-eval manifest with
CE/logprob primary and accuracy secondary. A later execution packet resolves
the selected coordinates to exact Hugging Face revisions and defines an ingest
gate that accepts only aggregate metrics plus per-instance prediction/logprob
rows. The post-measurement law gate is also fixed in advance against
chronological baselines and lineage-variance diagnostics. After the public
aggregate fork, a second packet selects `24` high-motion, diagnostic, and
control tasks over `20` checkpoints. This targeted packet is explicitly
post-public-data and cannot be merged into the sealed broad packet. Its post-run
gate is fixed before measurement: accepted jobs require aggregate metrics plus
per-instance prediction/logprob rows, and the targeting-lift test compares
high-motion tasks to matched controls. A later META-DARWIN audit tightened the
interpretation again: because the targeted tasks were selected from public
late-stage aggregate motion, H-24 now requires survival on earlier and mid-stage
checkpoints outside the public selector window before the packet can be treated
as more than leakage-aware targeting. A local execution preflight then confirms
that this is now a runtime question, not a design question: the packet has the
needed heldout stage-1 coverage, but the next step is an OLMES/GPU smoke test
for task aliases and output schema. A synthetic ingest fixture then fixed a
leakage bug in the post-run table path: rejected metrics-only or no-logprob
jobs can no longer enter downstream scoring. These packets are future evidence, not
current evidence; their role here is to show that the negative packet has been
converted into cleaner observation sites rather than into another same-packet
curve family.

A second empirical regularity extends across 14 (study, modality, scaling-variable) groups spanning image classification, language modeling, translation, math text, speech, and video. Under literature-anchored intrinsic-dimensionality estimates, β = 1/2 ranks first among principled candidates {1/2, 1/φ, 1, 2} by a 27% MAE margin over 1/φ, at MAE 0.050 across 14 groups; the pooled empirical β = 0.486 ± 0.083 SEM has 95% CI [0.32, 0.65] containing β = 1/2. This is consistent with natural data manifolds carrying Hölder regularity in the Brownian / Matérn-1 Gaussian-process class (Havrilla & Liao 2024). A symmetric Mode-1 direct measurement falsified the strict universality of β = 1/2 across modalities (speech gives β ≈ 2.5 under direct hidden-state measurement, a 5× discrepancy from the β = 1/2 zero-fit prediction), but the modality-conditional structure is itself informative: β values cluster by task class (sequence-aligned outputs are smoother; categorical-token outputs are rougher), consistent with task-class smoothness differences rather than noise.

**What §3.1 claims:** a bounded trajectory-shape empirical regularity within OLMo2 7B/13B, validated by two independent gauge-removal metrics; cleanly retired optimizer-control phase-flow as a non-transferring inference; exact OLMo2 1B falsification of the strongest endpoint-free slope-anchor promotion path; direct rejection of a scalar local exchange-rate rescue; a restart-overlap source-contamination rule for raw train-loss telemetry; source-gated downstream-eval evidence that rejects simple optimizer telemetry, eval-vector forecasting, and the tested stage-2 branch hint as promoted state variables; an eval-resolution/cadence boundary for this packet; a deterministic observability gate under which `0/12` tested same-packet candidates clear the floor; a sealed successor checkpoint-eval manifest plus execution, ingest, and law gates as future work, not current evidence; a separate public-aggregate targeted packet as an observability-acquisition design rather than a law result; and a cross-modality β ≈ 1/2 empirical anchor consistent with the Brownian/Matérn-1 GP class, with strict universality refuted by direct measurement and modality-conditional structure documented.

**What §3.1 does not claim:** universal cross-architecture generalization; first-principles derivation of the trajectory shape; an endpoint-free law surviving exact 1B; a universal β across modalities; a closed cross-modality scaling law.

**Falsification probes.** Each domain admitted explicit falsification surfaces beyond the rubric's primary gates.
