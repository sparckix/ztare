## Eigenquestion

Can JaggedThoughts repeatedly search broad public markets, spend bounded public
data and research attention on the candidates with the highest inspectable
acquisition priority, and convert useful evidence into governed research and
paper-decision objects without laundering a routing score into an investment
claim?

## Decision

Add a periodic opportunity-funnel layer to `ztare.investment`. It compiles
saved scout queues into one content-addressed acquisition cycle, selects a
diverse budget-feasible batch, enrolls the necessary public-source bundles,
runs the existing deep analyzers, and emits immutable agent research requests.
An enabled leaf subscription converts those requests into separately leased
web-research jobs. The agent may author a dossier; kernel validation owns
admission.

Subscription workers use the operator's ChatGPT/Codex subscription rather than
an API key. On a sandboxed Mac host, the runtime copies only the subscription
auth file into a mode-0600 process-private writable Codex home and ignores
ambient user configuration. This prevents the nested worker from failing while
initializing its state database without weakening the existing tool, model,
schema, or evidence seals.

The kernel owns identity, budgets, source epochs, leases, verification,
candidate screening, lineage, authority, and lifecycle transitions. It keeps
deterministic reference algorithms without granting them permanent production
privilege: typed numeric tasks may be offered to current-epoch model executors
and model-authored programs under the same verifier. The JaggedThoughts
research skill owns open-language translation, current primary-source research,
choice-system construction, and dossier authorship. The operator retains paper
activation. Brokerage authority is absent.

The first score is named `uncalibrated_acquisition_priority_proxy`. It orders
research acquisitions. It has no expected-return, target-probability, or
portfolio-weight interpretation.

## Product architecture

The Capital OS has five owning categories. Business state owns sourced economic
structure. Strategic-choice search owns typed option systems, incompatibilities,
local peaks, and scope-relative closure. Strategy-law learning owns dated execution,
moderators, operating outcomes, cohorts, and counterexamples. Security underwriting
owns earnings distributions, market-implied expectations, factors, state prices,
and future-return forecasts. Capital allocation owns paper portfolios, constraints,
costs, settlement, and prospective policy evidence. The institutional-memory graph
connects these outputs without merging their authority.

Formal grammar, Z3, symbolic regression, probability current, Lagrangian action,
Newton/Kepler search, and qualitative agents are tools within these categories.
None is a sixth pillar. Agents acquire open-world evidence and propose mechanisms;
typed leaves own identities and epochs; deterministic programs own arithmetic,
enumeration, constraint proofs, chronology, and settlement.

The OS also separates the capital owner from the investment subject. A
`CapitalMandate` may represent a household now and an ETA fund-of-funds vehicle
later. Eligible subjects are distinct `PublicSecurity`, `FundInterest`, and
`PrivateCompany` identities. They share evidence epochs, business fingerprints,
strategy mechanisms, prospective tickets, outcome episodes, and institutional
memory. They do not share market-price, liquidity, redemption, governance,
valuation, or settlement semantics. A `PositionPolicy` is the explicit join of
one mandate and one eligible subject. This is the extension point for private
markets; it prevents a public ticker adapter from becoming the accidental
universal investment model.

## Problem

The broad market catalog makes recurring searches cheap, but a queue of 911
companies or 17 funds is not actionable. Enriching every match would multiply
network calls, research time, duplicated sectors, and repeated attempts.
Selecting by catalog rank would also overstate coarse identity data.

The prior deep-discovery loop started only after manual enrollment. It could
periodically recompute an enrolled universe, but it could not decide which new
catalog identities deserved evidence acquisition, issue research-agent work,
recover a previously blocked job after a better source epoch, or bind a
monitor-candidate dossier into immutable lineage.

### Household planning authority contract

The implemented budget compiler MUST read the configured private source without
modifying it, hash the source bytes, restrict extraction to the declared
worksheet and component coordinates, preserve formula-defect diagnostics, and
hash the normalized receipt. It MUST NOT emit the private source path. Workbook
aggregate totals that fail the declared overlap checks MUST remain inadmissible;
only the component recomputation may seed a scenario default, with operator
confirmation false.

The implemented scenario compiler MUST require its exact input schema, bind the
goal-surface, public-basis, and planning-purpose mandate hashes, validate bounded
fractions and the complete sleeve-haircut map, enumerate the declared weight
grid, and emit its own content hash. It MUST expose assumption provenance,
operator-policy blockers, excluded liabilities whose rates are unresolved, and
false policy, capital, and brokerage authority.

The mandate-frontier compiler MUST vary only finite domains already declared by
the private goal surface or supplied explicitly by the operator. Every
completion MUST reuse one common-random-number identity, compile through the
same household allocation kernel, and close by exact selected sleeve weights.
It MUST expose fixed sleeve coordinates and ranges, retain every completion,
and rank unresolved inputs by reduction in decision entropy using the shared
information-yield primitive. Uniform completion weights describe the finite
design only; they MUST NOT be labeled beliefs, likelihoods, return forecasts,
or expected value. Inputs without a declared bounded domain remain unpriced.
The compiler MUST NOT infer an operator preference or grant policy, capital,
brokerage, or order authority.

The selected planning scenario MUST publish annual median and tenth-percentile
wealth paths from the same source-bound return, covariance, tax-haircut, and
simulation-draw identity used to select its allocation. The primary workbench
chart MUST consume those paths. A separate user-entered constant-return
calculator MUST NOT be presented as the selected policy's trajectory.

Promotion beyond planning additionally requires a versioned extractor/range
manifest, an explicit excluded-sheet manifest, source chronology, confirmation
or override provenance, and any currency/FX transformation. These are residual
controls in the current receipt. An operator paper policy MUST be a separate
reviewed object bound to an operator-purpose household mandate. Account
implementation MUST be another object bound to a current account snapshot,
positions, tax lots, restrictions, and estimated costs. Neither transition may
be inferred from a scenario's selected policy, and neither grants capital,
brokerage, or order authority.

Strategy evidence grades are monotone in claim strength, not necessarily in
time: `option_only`, `implementation_observed`,
`descriptive_outcome_support`, `comparator_adjusted_outcome_support`, then
`causal_transport_support`. Contradicting outcomes move a strategy to
`challenged_by_outcome`. No agent may jump a grade; the corresponding event,
outcome, comparator, or causal-panel receipt must exist.

## Lifecycle

```text
proposed -> queued -> leased -> enriching
                               |-> evidence_ready -> researched -> drafted
                               |                 |              -> paper_active
                               |                 |              -> settled -> learned
                               |                 -> covered_by_prior_dossier
                               |                 -> awaiting_source_reassessment
                               |                 -> superseded
                               |-> blocked -> repair_evidence -> evidence_ready
                                           -> retry -> queued
```

`researched` does not imply `qualified`. A monitor equity or fund can receive a
dossier and stays at its screen status. `drafted` is available only to a
qualified public-equity leaf. `paper_active` requires exact operator
confirmation.

## Acquisition policy

For candidate `x`, the kernel computes normalized components:

```text
base(x) = weighted_mean(
  measurement_value_proxy,
  request_specificity,
  identity_coverage,
  liquidity,
  source_efficiency
)

priority(x | S) = base(x) + diversity_weight *
                  min_{s in S} JaccardDistance(features(x), features(s))
```

The greedy loop repeatedly considers the highest current marginal priority and
admits it only if all remaining budgets permit:

- maximum equities and funds;
- maximum equities per sector;
- incremental and total public-source calls;
- estimated research minutes;
- enrollment, retry-attempt, and cooldown constraints.

The selection receipt records every component, diversity increment, cost,
admission or rejection reason, policy hash, source scouts, and total budget
usage. Jaccard diversity is a transparent first policy. It is not presented as
a proven optimal active-search rule.

## Source transaction

```text
due recurring intents
  -> broad catalog scans
  -> enrichment cycle
  -> leased jobs
  -> one batched SEC registry resolution for selected equities
  -> fund source enrollment
  -> issuer characteristics + available full-holdings source
  -> refresh core sources + selected entities + active profiles
  -> spend remaining source-call budget on unresolved request entities
  -> derive maximal valid signal subgraph
  -> deep equity/fund discovery
  -> candidate leaves
  -> agent research requests or typed blocks
```

Optional missing inputs cannot erase independent derived signals. A failed
candidate dependency is marked `stale_evidence`. A source outside the bounded
refresh retains its last point-in-time observation only while the type-specific
input-age gate admits it. The source
run records each blocked signal separately and distinguishes configured,
selected, attempted, cached, and newly retrieved sources. Exact provider bytes
remain cached for replay; cached-byte counts are diagnostics rather than
opportunity scores.

One workspace owns one serialized source transaction at a time. The transaction
holds the source-epoch lock through provider capture, normalized observation
merge, standard-metric derivation, evidence-vault receipt, and publication of
the latest source-run head. A slower earlier refresh therefore cannot overwrite
the head of a later completed refresh. Compilers consume only observations
whose `available_at` is admitted by their declared cutoff. A semantic compiler
change that alters this admission rule must advance the compiler identity and
availability epoch; it cannot silently reinterpret an existing same-epoch
Golden Store head.

Research-request supersession and evidence maintenance are separate lifecycles.
The request worker must reject an obsolete candidate hash before invoking a
provider or admitting a dossier. The source policy may still refresh that
entity when the current discovery leaf is `qualified`, `monitor`,
`stale_evidence`, or `needs_valuation_evidence`. Maintenance ranks the current
candidate state and consumes whole configured source bundles under the total
call ceiling. It cannot restore authority to the superseded request.

Research coverage is a third lifecycle. For a qualified candidate, the compiler
locates the newest monitored dossier for the same entity and compares every
configured material-source identity with the subscription baseline. An equal
content hash covers that source. A different hash covers it only when an exact
reassessment for that digest ended in `monitor` with a non-invalidating thesis
status. Missing baselines or unresolved changes defer the full dossier while the
reassessment or source refresh runs; an unmonitored material source requests new
underwriting. A covered bridge settles duplicate queued work without invoking
the subscription runtime.

The strategy phenotype compiler admits at most eight typed choices, requires
reinforcing-edge endpoints to equal declared choice ids, and computes the
lexicographically minimal directed adjacency under all node permutations.
Matching topology emits a cross-entity challenger question only. Mechanism
semantics, earnings effects, and returns still require source evidence and later
settlement.

Numeric acquisition does not depend on a general web-search provider. Company
facts, prices, macro series, ERP inputs, and fund characteristics come from
their configured issuer or regulatory adapters. Search may locate qualitative
industry evidence, but a search result cannot become a metric observation
without a source adapter and typed receipt. Current fund adapters cover
iShares, Vanguard, Harbor, Avantis, and First Trust; unsupported issuers remain
blocked by provider identity.

## Leaf-subscription transaction

Every evidence-ready request is deterministically subscribed into a separate
`jaggedthoughts_subscription_research` queue identity. One consumer:

1. exposes a candidate job to the claim membrane only when its exact parent is
   `done/evidence_ready`;
2. claims the highest-priority admissible request under a compare-and-set lease;
3. checks the daily dispatch budget and exact request hash;
4. reads the exact candidate leaf;
5. rechecks that leaf against the current discovery identity immediately before
   any provider call;
6. invokes the operator-authenticated Codex or Claude subscription CLI;
7. exposes web research while sealing shell, repo reads, JS, and remote MCP;
8. requires a strict dossier schema, two opened sources, and a primary source;
9. rejects unknown strategy evidence references;
10. submits the dossier through the kernel.

Before ranking or claim, the queue MUST settle both ordinary candidate research
and activation-transport research as `covered_by_prior_dossier` when the exact
current request is already covered. A covered activation job MUST consume no
provider call and MUST NOT remain claimable merely because its immutable request
artifact still exists.
The activation request object identity MUST include its source-epoch digest. Two
requests compiled at one timestamp against different source bytes MUST be
different Golden Store objects, never rival heads of one object.

Large Golden Store bodies may use a transparent compressed physical encoding.
Logical equality, payload hashes, leaf hashes, and lineage are computed over
canonical uncompressed JSON; readers MUST accept both legacy text and compressed
rows. Verification MUST stream the graph, and physical compaction MUST NOT
rewrite append-only history as part of an ordinary workspace build.

`workspace submit-dossier` then:

1. verifies request ID and content hash;
2. verifies candidate leaf, candidate hash, ticker, and evidence epoch;
3. verifies the exact parent is still `done/evidence_ready` before any golden
   research write;
4. validates required research sections and primary-source metadata;
5. adds a dossier content hash;
6. writes a `candidate_research_dossier` golden leaf with edges to the request
   and candidate;
7. projects the queue item to `researched`;
8. preserves `capital_authority=false`.

Submission also atomizes cited documents and strategy choices into shared
evidence and mechanism leaves, then creates a monitor subscription. The current
material adapters are SEC Company Facts for companies and configured issuer
fundamentals for funds; daily price sources stay in the quantitative compiler.
When a material source content digest changes after the subscription baseline:

1. the source receipt becomes a `public_source_change_event` leaf;
2. reverse edges enumerate the prior dossier and its contained mechanism claims;
3. a content-addressed `research_reopen_request` and queue job are created;
4. the leased consumer gathers current primary evidence under a strict schema;
5. the kernel records a `research_reassessment` with thesis, rival, choice, and
   falsifier deltas;
6. duplicate checks of the same source receipt cannot create another job;
7. the reassessment has no draft, portfolio, order, or capital authority.

Provider stdout, stderr, prompt, response schema, result bytes, process receipt,
and warm-session state are durable artifacts. Expired SQLite leases are
reclaimable. The current policy allows 40 subscription research dispatches per
UTC day.

Before the next claim, `InstitutionalLearningSchedule` MUST reprioritize only
admissible queued jobs. Its inspectable score combines query-by-committee
separation, description-length compression, new entity context, outstanding
cohort-block deficit, and declared decision closure. Repeated work for one
entity/action receives a bounded diminishing-return penalty. An under-served
action class receives a bounded service bonus; a job waiting seven days receives
an explicit starvation guard. Existing queue priority MUST NOT feed back into the
next schedule. The components are upper-bound learning-leverage proxies rather
than observed information gain. The schedule
MUST persist its components and exact next work id, MUST NOT create or remove a
job, and has research-queue authority only.

A frozen measurement or outcome successor MAY carry an immutable
`frozen_chain_priority` in its job payload. The scheduler MUST consume that
coordinate instead of mutable queue priority so an already-open
evidence-to-settlement chain can close before widening acquisition without a
feedback loop. The coordinate cannot change eligibility, security rank, paper
weight, or capital authority. Eligible frozen measurement/outcome successors
form a claim tier ahead of numeric learning-leverage scores and candidate- or
activation-lane fairness reservations. Normal score ordering and fairness
strides resume when the chain is drained. Encoding this only as a large numeric
score is forbidden: widening scores can legitimately be larger and would bypass
the successor.

Current fund-implementation work is a separate bounded-service lane. Before
enqueue, the queue owner MUST retire any queued fund-gap job whose exact
request × prior-evidence identity is absent from the current comparison epoch;
the provider MUST NOT be invoked for that obsolete work. After exact frozen
successors, the live arbiter MUST claim a current fund-gap request after at most
three provider calls outside the fund lane while fund work waits. Activation,
candidate, and fund cadence debt are measured from durable provider-call
receipts, exposed in status, and cannot change a fund's comparison rank,
eligibility, paper posture, or capital authority.

The discovery checker, research consumer, and capital-cycle checker are
process-local threads started by the local port-8080 server, or foreground CLI
services. Their queues and immutable artifacts survive a restart; their timers
stop when the starter process stops. No launchd, cron, or systemd unit is
installed by this spec.

The capital-cycle status MUST expose one point-in-time settlement-readiness
projection across closed-book forecasts, complete-policy tournaments, and
strategy-dual outcomes. It distinguishes an open horizon, a matured contract
awaiting an admissible later observation, and a contract that has not yet been
issued. For strategy-dual issuance it also reports exact-adoption move count
and declared operating-outcome-contract count. The projection cannot create an
outcome, move a maturity date, or grant paper or capital authority.

An exact-adoption operating contract MUST bind a metric already admitted by the
point-in-time observation system, a unit, direction, minimum effect, horizon,
baseline rule, and evidence refs. A company-level metric is a bundle-level
falsifier unless a separately identified control supports narrower attribution.
The measurement successor MUST preserve the validated primary-source catalog
and metric locator inside the operating contract hash. When the horizon matures,
the due outcome request MUST carry that exact catalog and locator inside its own
hash so the subscription worker retrieves later documents from the frozen source
identity instead of silently rediscovering one. Every catalog entry binds source
ID, HTTPS URL, source class, availability chronology, and declared metric/clock
support. The worker may return a typed later-document gap; it may not substitute
an unbound source or backfill an observation.
Adding a contract MUST NOT modify or backfill an already issued closed-book run.

The discovery heartbeat MUST expose a single periodic-activation projection:
last discovery and completed research identities, the next due discovery or
leased research job, any blocker and retry time, the current discovery hash and
candidate-leaf count, subscription transport, and paper-only authority. A
source-change event identity includes source, prior content, current content,
and receipt. The same fetched receipt may be compared with several monitor
baselines; differing transitions MUST produce versioned events and MUST NOT be
silently collapsed or written under one immutable identity.

Qualified public equities may subsequently enter `draft-candidate`. Monitor
candidates stop after dossier submission. A qualified fund dossier, or an accepted
source reassessment that makes its candidate-bound coverage current, immediately
recompiles the inactive fund-proposal audit; activation still requires the separate

information gain without a source-bound hypothesis committee.
The learning surface is an append-only intent-to-treat census. Request issue
freezes the typed rank-lane benchmark, a 30-day research-decision cutoff, the
365-day terminal, a 5% shadow probe, adjusted-price identity, and round-trip
cost. Every eligible assignment opens the same candidate/benchmark window at
the cutoff. The first exact assignment-bound full-research closed-book forecast
available by then freezes the probe when its expected active return is positive;
absence or a nonpositive forecast freezes abstention. At the terminal the probe
scores `0.05 × (candidate gross return − benchmark gross return) − candidate
trading cost`; abstention scores zero. A positive probe with missing price or
lineage remains censored and blocks routing credit. An unbound fund benchmark
excludes the unit instead of silently substituting a broad index. Process
observations cannot select an arm. Review
occurs only in fixed increments of at least 20 settled units per arm, uses an
independent-arm randomization test with alpha spending, and requires the
confidence interval to clear a one-percentage-point minimum useful effect.
The resulting content-addressed decision may alter research-question order
only. Half of later assignments remain randomized audits; the rest may consume
the supported arm only through exact learning-credit lineage. The decision has
no security-ranking, position-sizing, order, or capital authority.

Closed-book forecast learning uses a separate unit. It groups settled episodes
by exact mechanism bundle and forecast subject kind. Per-run disagreement
identifies the entity and horizon where comparable forecasts differ most and can
route the next evidence request. A bundle observed on multiple entities becomes
a descriptive transfer candidate. Individual mechanisms receive no attribution
unless a later controlled comparison varies that mechanism while holding the
rest of the bundle fixed.

Institutional law learning uses a separate lifecycle:

```text
closed-book packet -> phenotype episode -> cohort -> law evaluation
                    -> counterexample or future transfer candidate

typed metric vocabulary -> recursive program enumeration -> law frontier
                        -> frozen training selection -> sealed holdout veto
                        -> future-dated conjecture -> later settlement
```

One market-date cross-section is one inference block. Predictor metrics are
frozen before outcome values are joined. A generated law has
`not_before=generated_at`, so the sample that selected its program can create a
conjecture but cannot validate it. The first search grammar admits five
data-supported features per entity kind, depth two, and 400 programs; the
current equity instance exhausts at 305 programs. At least eight settled blocks
are required before it may mint up to three new conjectures per entity kind.
Any changed formula, mechanism, cohort, or semantic meaning requires a new law
version.

Compilation rejects four structural leakage routes before evaluation: a law
cannot activate before its creation epoch; its outcome metric cannot be a
predictor input or predictor output; that outcome cannot select or partition a
cohort; and a mechanism consequence cannot be its own antecedent unless a
future grammar adds an explicit typed lag. Omitted `not_before` is compiled to
`created_at`, never to an unbounded historical sample.

Historical grammar replay separates search from test. Feature selection,
recursive enumeration, objective values, frontier closure, direction, and the
bounded candidate list consume only the chronological training blocks. Their
program IDs and evidence hashes form an immutable frozen-selection receipt.
The later historical partition can veto those programs but cannot choose or
backfill a replacement. Reversing every holdout outcome leaves the selection
receipt unchanged and rejects the previously supported proposal. This tests
the typed program and deterministic kernel without LLM-memory leakage; repeated
human exposure to the same partition still limits it to diagnostic authority.

Every empirical search family also enters a shared `SearchTrialFamily` registry
before its first outcome boundary. The record binds purpose, model family,
selection unit, exact sorted candidate-set hash, generator receipts, and timing.
`SearchTrialCensus` reconciles tournament, experiment, and law-search artifacts
to those immutable records. An unregistered or candidate-set-mismatched surface
is diagnostic-only. Registration permits later multiplicity accounting; it does
not establish an effect, predictive edge, policy weight, or capital authority.
Opening a closed-book model family or complete-policy family registers its exact
candidate set at first use; later episodes reuse that identity, and any changed
candidate set requires a new family.
Recursive strategy enumeration remains visible in the census as decision search
but is excluded from the empirical-coverage denominator until a later outcome
contract turns its candidate family into an empirical test.

The census must also group every closed-book run by horizon and exact candidate
trial-family set, and every portfolio-policy run by its deterministic policy
family. Equivalent policy aliases remain visible but do not increase the
candidate count. Pending episodes count as observed empirical search and cannot
be omitted merely because no outcome exists yet; without a common
`SearchTrialFamily` registration they remain explicitly uncovered.

For a world-model tournament with an exact prospective family,
`TrialCountSelectionGate` compares the point-estimate economic winner only with
the declared baseline, reuses the paired block-permutation p-value, and applies
a Bonferroni family-wise adjustment using the committed trial count. It fails
closed on a missing or mismatched family, late registration, unreached outcome
boundary, insufficient independent blocks, ineligible temporal receipt, missing
baseline, or missing paired result. A pass is candidate evidence only; it cannot
set `alpha_claim_eligible`, paper-policy authority, or capital authority.
Deflated Sharpe and probability-of-backtest-overfitting names may appear only as
uncomputed methods with typed missing inputs.

A promotion-eligible association law may enter
`compile_law_policy_influence`. The compiler re-executes its typed predictor on
the current compatible discovery cross-section, converts the expected-direction
score to a percentile, caps each law at ±5 percentage points and aggregate
influence at ±10 points, and writes the receipt into the opportunity book. It
cannot change screening, paper weight, or capital authority. Loss of promotion
eligibility yields zero influence on the next cycle. Causal laws are excluded
until a separate treatment-effect-to-policy contract exists.

The complete-policy layer freezes cash, equal-weight, discovery-priority,
law-adjusted-priority, and reviewed operator policies on one opportunity-book
epoch. Identical weight vectors are quotient-equivalent and only one
representative enters the trial family. Settlement separates total portfolio
return versus SPY from security-selection contribution versus equal benchmark
exposure and charges frozen turnover cost. At least eight independent blocks
are required before policy comparison is enabled.

Each exact policy family also compiles a `jaggedthoughts-portfolio-policy-review-v1`
artifact from settled blocks. The family identity binds policy versions,
horizon, benchmark, score contract, and transaction-cost contract. Policy
scores become paired losses for total excess return and selection contribution;
the substrate-general survivor compiler applies paired permutation tests and
Benjamini–Hochberg correction. Only one statistically surviving policy can be
named `eligible_for_paper_policy_review`, and that status grants no automatic
weight or capital mutation.

Each policy also freezes an attribution contract against its nearest declared
reference. Cash and discovery compare with equal weight; learned-law and
reviewed-operator policies compare with discovery. For every security the
contract binds the weight delta to the exact candidate digest, source receipts,
research question, discovery score, and any eligible law contributions.
Settlement multiplies that frozen delta by later active return and verifies the
sum against the observed policy-score difference after incremental cost. This
is accounting provenance. It cannot divide causal credit among shared sources
or research questions without separately varied prospective policies.

Cross-layer learning MUST compile one read-only credit projection from these
existing contracts. An underwriting settlement updates the exact model bundle
that ran. One law earns credit only when a non-equivalent prospective policy
varies exactly that law identity. One fund implementation earns credit only when
the target and reference program ids are frozen in an isolated one-for-one policy
comparison. A complete policy earns review credit only through the existing
paired, after-cost survivor gate. Lineage, activity, and process quality cannot
substitute for later independently settled variation. This projection MUST NOT
create a second outcome ledger or grant paper-weight or capital authority.

Association laws are ranked within compatible cross-sections and carry no
causal status. The causal adapter supports common adoption and an unadjusted
group-time ATT diagnostic with never- or not-yet-treated controls, minimum
treated/control and pre/post floors, pretrend diagnostics, and deterministic
unit resampling. Every treated v2 panel row binds an exact source-observed
implementation event; interval-censored adoption remains ineligible. The
control adapter MAY admit a bounded non-adopter only when every in-scope source
event through its control year is classified, no typed target-mode completion
exists, point-in-time industry identity matches, individual 3+1 outcome history
is complete, and the corpus, classification, and negative event proofs are hash
bound. A missing or ambiguous earlier event excludes the control. The
group-time result cannot promote a law or change policy. A subgroup reversal is preserved
as a counterexample; any narrower successor starts after that evidence epoch.

## SOTA grounding and current boundary

- [OpenPM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09988) requires every agent-visible
  record to be available at decision time, compiles risk mandates into typed
  constraints, separates analysts from the constructor and deterministic
  critic, and reports turnover-aware cost sensitivity. Its single-window
  results are upper bounds rather than validated predictive performance.
  JaggedThoughts adopts the point-in-time field, typed-admission,
  role-separation, and deterministic-cost controls without importing that
  study's result.
- [The Statistical Limit of Arbitrage](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33070)
  shows why high-dimensional search does not remove the estimation problem when
  pricing signals are weak and rare. More enumerated programs therefore
  increase the need for independent blocks, explicit uncertainty, and failed-
  program retention; they do not earn return credit by breadth alone.
- [Large Language Models: An Applied Econometric
  Framework](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5094968)
  requires a no-training-leakage design for prediction from text and validation
  for LLM-generated measurements. Historical LLM market forecasts remain
  diagnostic unless the outcome could not have entered model training;
  prospectively sealed episodes are the primary predictive evidence path.
- [Machine Learning and the Implementable Efficient Frontier](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/advance-article/doi/10.1093/rfs/hhag022/8524346)
  makes the net-of-cost portfolio frontier, rather than a cost-agnostic return
  forecast, the economic target. JaggedThoughts therefore keeps research rank,
  instrument implementation, and portfolio sizing as separate contracts and
  scores the complete policy after turnover and trading frictions.
- [Can AI Do Strategy?](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/stsc.2026.intro.v11.n1)
  separates predictive, interventional, transported-counterfactual, and
  frame-building strategy tasks, and separates those capabilities from delegated
  discretion. The strategy grammar and agent may search across all four levels;
  causal and capital authority remain attached to independently settleable
  transitions.
- [Structural Causal Modeling of Managerial Interventions](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/stsc.2022.0169)
  motivates explicit intervention, mediator, outcome, and counterfactual
  identities. A strategy phenotype is therefore a treatment definition and
  hypothesis, never causal evidence by itself.
- The options-led process in [Teaching Students How to Make Strategic
  Choices](https://d30i16bbj53pdg.cloudfront.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Teaching-Students-How-to-Make-Strategic-Choices.pdf)
  motivates bundle-level search on rugged, interdependent choice landscapes.
  Typed recursive enumeration and frontier closure mechanize only the declared
  option space; omitted-choice residuals and later outcomes remain explicit.

- [Adaptive submodularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.3967) gives greedy
  guarantees when its diminishing-returns property holds. This funnel has not
  established that property, so it claims no such guarantee.
- [Callaway and Sant'Anna](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09015) identify
  group-time effects under multiple periods and treatment timing; [Sun and
  Abraham](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.09.006) show how conventional
  event-study coefficients can be contaminated under heterogeneous staggered
  effects. They motivate the group-time diagnostic and its explicit limits.
- [Information-directed sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.5556) balances
  squared expected regret against information gain under a posterior. The
  current acquisition score has neither quantity and stays explicitly
  uncalibrated.
- [Efficient nonmyopic active search](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/jiang17d.html)
  establishes the hardness of approximating the optimal search policy and
  motivates budget-aware lookahead. The current compiler uses inspectable
  marginal greedy selection as an operating baseline.
- [Diverse active search with diminishing returns](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/nguyen23d.html)
  motivates diversity as part of discovery utility rather than a cosmetic
  tie-breaker. The current feature/Jaccard term is a deterministic surrogate.
- [Multifidelity active search](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/nguyen21f.html)
  motivates cheap broad observations followed by costlier evidence. The
  catalog/deep-source split has this shape, but no Bayesian fidelity model is
  claimed.
- [Finance Agent Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00828) and
  [FinanceQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18062) are capability snapshots from
  particular model/tool configurations. They establish an initial failure prior
  and motivate independent settlement; they do not define a permanent
  agent-versus-program boundary.
- [PAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10435) and
  [LEVER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08468) show the useful split between model
  search or program composition and execution-backed verification.
- [RouteLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18665) motivates task-conditional
  routing from observed evidence, while
  [Large Language Monkeys](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21787) demonstrates that
  repeated sampling becomes useful when answers have an automatic verifier.
- [FinToolBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08262) and
  [Fin-RATE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07294) further motivate typed tool-use,
  causal-trace, and failure-class evaluation for finance agents. The local
  execution receipts remain the routing authority for this workbench.

The synthesis is useful because it combines those search concerns with
point-in-time financial identities, separate equity/fund contracts, recursive
company choice frontiers, immutable research handoffs, and paper settlement.
Novelty remains an empirical research question. Usefulness is judged by
research yield and later economic consequences.
