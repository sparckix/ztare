# AxiomPack compositional theory-invention successor

Frozen before navigator inference. This campaign tests the unresolved case
left by the contrastive run: interaction among two or more future authored
coordinates. The preceding exhaustive scan covered the single authored
coordinate against all 210 seed formulas and found no residual pair; it did not
cover authored-coordinate interactions.

## Hypotheses

1. Host-isolated lineages produce at least two distinct typed coordinates whose
   exact seed-stratum profiles are absent from the seed panel.
2. After batch admission into a fresh epoch, at least two frozen theory
   programs consume those coordinates and make explicit predictions.
3. The rebuilt chart witnesses a hold/refute disagreement on a common target;
   silence by another program does not count as refutation.
4. An information-priced holding program survives a larger-size countermodel
   search and formal attribution.
5. Post-freeze source review classifies the surviving mechanism as more than a
   direct recovery of a familiar named law or definitional rewrite.

Each hypothesis may fail independently. Hypotheses 1–3 are the apparatus test;
4–5 are the mathematical-interest bar.

## Frozen inputs

- campaign: `campaign_compositional.md`;
- reviewed executable blueprint: `typed_blueprint_compositional.json`;
- anonymous exact context and published enumeration checksums from the prior
  cycle-structure campaign, at the stratum owned by the executable blueprint;
- three host-isolated Sol 5.6 medium navigator lineages plus one late
  synthesizer;
- one shared 1,800-second wall clock, subscription runtime only, no API
  fallback or metered API spend;
- the blueprint-owned withheld countermodel strata, then Isabelle/Lean only
  for a selected lift.

## Provider-free launch gate

Before dispatch, Hetzner must reproduce the frozen signature, base theory,
seed-stratum census, seed formula count, semantic profile count, object partition,
blueprint ID, runtime route, and budget digest with zero provider calls. It must
also confirm:

- `selection_mode: theory_program`;
- three host-isolated lineages;
- presentation width is not fixed to two;
- formula and theory-language proposal capabilities are visible;
- prediction-disagreement policy is importable and provider-free;
- the previous completed attempts are not selected as resume targets.

Any mismatch blocks launch.

## Discriminating outcomes

1. no authored coordinate: the seed representation remained the effective
   ceiling;
2. one new profile only: representation lift without composition;
3. two profiles admitted, no program consumes both collectively: synthesis or
   navigation failure;
4. programs freeze, no actual hold/refute disagreement: plural theories without
   a discriminating query;
5. disagreement selected, larger-size countermodel found: bounded overfit;
6. boundary survives, source review finds direct recovery: recovery rather than
   discovery;
7. boundary survives and source review finds no direct antecedent: novelty
   candidate requiring expert interpretation;
8. receipted refusal/exhaustion after tested routes: informative null.

## Anti-gaming and iatrogenesis checks

- The host supplies no candidate law, target identity, or desired abstraction.
- Multiple authored coordinates are a success criterion, not a forced action
  sequence; the leaf may refuse or request a different language.
- Only host-evaluated semantic profile novelty earns coordinate credit.
- Disagreement requires positive support in one program and a concrete finite
  counterexample in another. Missing nomination is `unknown`, never `false`.
- Query ranking is proposal-only and preserves each program's complete frozen
  prediction tuple for ordinary boundary replay.
- Exact finite support, formal proof, and literature novelty remain separate
  authorities.

## Stop and claim boundary

Stop at the frozen wall clock, a receipted null, or completion of the selected
boundary and interpretation. This campaign can establish an agent-origin
theory-program candidate with bounded and formal evidence. It cannot establish
unrestricted novelty, importance, or a general theory-invention rate from one
run.
