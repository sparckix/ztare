# AxiomPack contrastive language-refinement successor

Frozen before navigator inference. This successor tests the representation
repair exposed by the first cycle-structure campaign. It reuses the same typed
substrate and exact size-five declaration while changing the cold workbench
surface and campaign prompt.

## Hypotheses

1. The 210-formula seed language leaves at least one non-singleton
   same-stratum observational object class.
2. Given anonymous structures from such a class, Sol 5.6 high can author at
   least one typed equation that exact host evaluation shows separates the
   displayed pair.
3. The stronger scientific outcome requires that admitted coordinate to enter
   an exact-two presentation with positive residual information after the v5
   equational baseline, finite-structure baseline, and singleton ablation.

The first hypothesis is a provider-free viability gate. The second measures
active representation refinement. The third is the only outcome that warrants
larger-model or formal boundary spend.

## Discriminating outcomes

1. no contrast request: the new affordance did not alter search behavior;
2. contrast requested, no formula proposed: representation evidence alone was
   insufficient for authorship;
3. formula proposed but pair not separated: failed coordinate invention;
4. pair separated but full finite profile duplicates an existing profile:
   host inconsistency, because the pair agreed on every existing formula;
5. pair separated and a new profile admitted, with no residual exact-two use:
   representation lift without theory-selection lift;
6. admitted profile enters a residual pair, then fails at larger size:
   size-five overfit;
7. admitted profile enters a residual pair and survives formal attribution:
   source-review candidate;
8. receipted reject-all after tested contrasts: informative null.

## Iatrogenesis checks

- The pair is selected by current truth-vector equivalence and stratum, without
  theory names, source labels, or a desired law.
- The leaf chooses the equation. The host owns typing, exact evaluation,
  context identity, and replay.
- Separation certifies only a new coordinate in the finite panel.
- Existing residual, collapse, premise-necessity, boundary, and knowledge
  gates remain unchanged.
- `research_isomorphism` is unavailable to the cold navigator and supplies no
  destination prior in this campaign.

## Stop and escalation

Stop after the frozen budget, a receipted null, or a finalist. Two consecutive
failed contrast proposals count as low-yield pressure; they do not trigger a
host-authored formula. Larger-model, Isabelle, Lean, and literature work remain
lazy. Approve those stages only if an agent-authored profile contributes to a
positive-residual exact-two presentation with both singleton countermodels.

## Claim boundary

This campaign can measure whether contrastive examples improve typed formula
invention and whether an invented finite coordinate enters a useful local
theory presentation. It cannot establish theorem novelty, broad conjecturer
superiority, or cross-domain transfer.
