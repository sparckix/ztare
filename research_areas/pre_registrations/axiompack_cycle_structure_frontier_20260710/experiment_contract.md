# AxiomPack anonymous cycle-structure frontier campaign

Frozen before navigator inference. The campaign tests axiom invention inside a
semantics-rich finite algebraic domain. It does not target the already-published
enumeration problem.

## Hypothesis

Given an exact size-five model geometry and a deliberately shallow seed chart,
a cold Sol navigator can author at least one typed equation with a new semantic
profile and use it in an exact-two presentation whose joint consequences retain
positive residual information after host deduction and singleton ablation.

## Eigenquestion

Can the navigator create the next useful coordinate of the theory landscape,
or does it remain confined to the host-enumerated order-two chart?

## Why this substrate

The frozen equational base is a definitional expansion of finite
nondegenerate cycle sets: one binary operation, its rowwise inverse operation,
and the inverse of its diagonal map. The 2025 SAT-enumeration paper reports
that finite databases have already generated mathematical conjectures and that
size eleven materially expands the available structure data. The domain is
therefore active, executable, and rich enough for structural conjectures, while
its short equational subregions are not supplied to the navigator.

The host encoding has an external checksum. Exact solver enumeration must
reproduce the published isomorphism counts `2, 5, 23, 88` at carrier sizes
`2, 3, 4, 5`. This checksum validates the compiled substrate; reproducing it
earns no discovery credit.

## Frozen inputs

- signature and five anonymous base equations: `typed_blueprint.json`;
- exact initial stratum: carrier size five;
- formula seed: all canonical universal equations through total operation
  order two;
- presentation arity: exactly two;
- navigator: Codex subscription runtime, `sol`, normalized effort `medium`;
- hard campaign wall: 1,800 seconds;
- no API fallback or metered API spend.

The navigator sees anonymous operation shapes, anonymous base equations,
finite profiles, and typed workbench receipts. It does not see the domain name,
published counts, named subclasses, source text, or post-freeze interpretation.

## Provider-free launch gate

Before dispatch, the Hetzner preflight must establish all of the following:

- final-UNSAT exact census receipt at size five;
- 88 canonical models and 2,640 accepted labeled models;
- 210 seed formulas, 11 semantic profiles, and 15 generated theory nodes;
- model-generation policy `smt_isomorphism_class_enumeration.v1`;
- zero provider calls;
- runtime resolution `codex / gpt-5.6-sol / medium`.

## Discriminating outcomes

1. no formula proposal and seed-only rejection: formula authorship was not
   exercised;
2. proposed formula has an existing finite profile: the conjecture did not add
   a coordinate at this resolution;
3. a new profile is admitted but participates in no residual pair: measurable
   distinction without useful joint yield;
4. a pair's consequence follows from one premise: compound-signal failure;
5. the bounded equational baseline derives every joint consequence: routine
   recovery;
6. size-six or size-seven SMT finds a countermodel: size-five overfit;
7. a proof-attributed implication maps to a named published law or subclass:
   recovery with a useful apparatus receipt;
8. a proof-attributed implication remains source-unmapped after independent
   post-freeze review: novelty candidate for a wider campaign.

## Harness iatrogenesis checks

- The prompt legitimizes formula expansion and reject-all; it does not name a
  desired law, subclass, or application.
- The order-two chart is explicitly a seed. Failure to leave it is measured,
  not converted into a claim about the model.
- The host supplies exact model geometry and receipts but does not choose the
  next formula or presentation.
- Formula IDs, finite extents, and residual coordinates cannot establish
  mathematical interest alone.
- Source search begins only after freeze and records discovery versus recovery
  separately from proof status.

## Success, escalation, and kill conditions

Implementation succeeds when the exact SMT census compiles through the normal
campaign inlet, freezes, snapshots, and replays with no family-specific runtime
adapter. The scientific campaign earns escalation only if an agent-authored
profile contributes to a residual exact-two pack, both singleton ablations have
countermodels, and at least one larger-size or proof boundary survives.

Kill or downgrade on context drift, pre-freeze source leakage, incomplete
census, semantic duplicate credit, direct base-rewrite credit, primitive
collapse, empty/singleton extent, missing singleton witnesses, or promotion of
a bounded absence into an unrestricted statement. Reject-all with complete
host receipts is an informative null.

## Claim boundary

The run can produce candidate axioms and proof-attributed implications. It
cannot establish literature novelty, an asymptotic classification, a finite
basis theorem, or superiority over other conjecture systems. Those require a
post-freeze source screen, independent proof review, and a wider model or
construction campaign.
