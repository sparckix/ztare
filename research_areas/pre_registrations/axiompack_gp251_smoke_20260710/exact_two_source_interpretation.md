## Key recombination

The proof has a short three-step mechanism:

1. instantiate E8 at `x ◇ x`;
2. rewrite its inner `(x ◇ x) ◇ (x ◇ x)` to `x` using E151, deriving
   E359: `x ◇ x = (x ◇ x) ◇ x`;
3. use that derived bridge inside E8 to obtain E99.

E151 is therefore a contraction bridge for the self-product appearing after
self-instantiation of E8. E8 supplies both the outer target skeleton and the
self-instantiated equation from which E359 is derived. The second residual
target selected by the finite context was E359 itself; the checked E99 proof
contains it as an intermediate, but the campaign cap stopped before a separate
boundary receipt for that target.
