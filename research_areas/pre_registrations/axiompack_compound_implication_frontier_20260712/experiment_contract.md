# Compound-implication theory invention

Frozen before navigator inference.

## Eigenquestion

Can an anonymous theory-program search discover an irreducible compound magma
implication whose target survives the exact seed chart and larger-carrier
countermodel search, without receiving equation identities or a candidate law?

## Prior and discriminator

The Equational Theories Project completed the single-equation implication graph
and identifies compound implications `E₁ ∧ E₂ ⇒ T` as future work. AxiomPack's
finite incidence quotient can search presentations rather than enumerate raw
syntactic triples.

The discriminating outcome requires all of:

1. two to four hypotheses support a target on the complete seed context;
2. removing each hypothesis exposes a concrete countermodel;
3. the frozen single-premise oracle supplies no recorded singleton route;
4. the target survives the blueprint-owned larger-carrier search;
5. post-freeze formal and source review does not classify the result as a
   direct rewrite or catalogued recovery.

Representation lift, a finite semantic profile, or a seed-refuted target alone
does not pass this contract.

## Kill conditions

- every candidate has a redundant premise;
- every nominated target is already refuted or vacuous in the seed context;
- the single-premise oracle explains the implication;
- a larger carrier supplies a countermodel;
- formal attribution fails;
- source review identifies a routine antecedent.

## Claim boundary

A surviving run is a compound-implication candidate with bounded and formal
receipts. Knowledge-frontier status remains post-freeze and advisory.
