# Content-bound matched-control single-execution probe

## Identity boundary

The matched negative control is one of the finite final-ratification
authorities. Its producer may execute the positive/perturbed pair once, but the
closed-artifact boundary must verify that the receipt belongs to the exact
qualified target, posed source, closed source, normalized signature, and
deterministically rebuilt probe pair before reusing it.

## Hypothesis

Provider-free carried-theorem ratification currently executes the same
source-aware conclusion perturbation in the solver contract and again in the
post-close margin battery. The first receipt already records the target
identity, target-signature hash, posed/closed source hashes, both probe hashes,
and compile outcomes. A pure binding validator can eliminate the second
execution without reducing the authority set or accepting an unbound receipt.

## Discriminating test

1. Factor the perturbation builder from its compiler calls.
2. Validate a positive producer receipt against independently rebuilt probe
   hashes and exact posed/closed source identity.
3. Inject target, source, signature, positive-probe, and negative-probe drift;
   every mutation must reject reuse.
4. Supply a valid bound receipt to the margin battery and assert that the
   compiler-backed conclusion-control function is not called.
5. For an unbound producer receipt, rerun the exact outer control and replace
   the provisional receipt before finalization; unavailable replay withholds
   credit.
6. Replay the target-scope, ratification, and closed-artifact controls.

## Success criterion

- a valid bound receipt executes the matched control only once;
- every identity mutation rejects reuse;
- fallback replay produces the same complete receipt shape before finalization;
- the fourteen-authority roster and accepted/rejected mathematical verdicts do
  not change;
- the uniform frozen-corpus replay has no governance regressions.

## Kill conditions

- the positive and negative probes cannot be rebuilt without compiler state;
- a composite or legacy receipt can pass binding validation without all exact
  identities;
- reuse changes a normal verdict or converts runtime unavailability into
  counterevidence.

If killed, retain the duplicate execution and record it as authority cost.
