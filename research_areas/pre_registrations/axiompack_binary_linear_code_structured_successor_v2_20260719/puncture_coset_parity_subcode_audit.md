# Puncture--coset--parity route audit

Date: 2026-07-20

Status: pencil audit of a campaign-authored route; no search or priority claim.

## Verdict and exact hypotheses

The construction is valid.  Let `L` be the frozen even binary
`[51,20,14]` parity extension, puncture any chosen active coordinate, and
relabel that deleted coordinate as the parity coordinate.  Write

\[
C\leq\mathbf F_2^{50},\qquad L=\{(c,p(c)):c\in C\},\qquad
p(c)=\operatorname{wt}(c)\bmod 2.
\]

The existing exact oracles give `dim C = 20`, `d(C)=13`, and show that
`p|C` is nonzero.  Suppose that

\[
\delta_C(v):=\min_{c\in C}\operatorname{wt}(v+c)\geq 13.
\]

Then `v` is outside `C`, so `D=C+<v>` has dimension 21, and

\[
d(D)=\min(d(C),\delta_C(v))\geq13.
\]

The parity functional is nonzero on `D` because it is already nonzero on
`C`.  Hence

\[
E=\ker(p|D)
\]

has dimension 20.  Every word of `E` has even weight, while every nonzero
word of `D` has weight at least 13.  Consequently `d(E)>=14`, and `E` is a
binary `[50,20,>=14]` code.

These are the conditions that must be checked rather than inferred:

1. `C` has rank 20 and distance at least 13;
2. the complete coset minimum `delta_C(v)` is at least 13;
3. `p|D` has rank one, so the parity kernel drops dimension exactly once;
4. an exact-distance claim also needs a weight-14 word in `E`.

Condition 2 implies the row-independence of `v`; it need not be checked as a
separate mathematical assumption.  For the frozen control, conditions 1 and
3 are already certified.  Its even subcode

\[
C_0=\ker(p|C)
\]

is the `[50,19,14]` shortening of `L` at the selected coordinate and contains
a certified weight-14 word.  Since `C_0` is contained in `E`, the
frozen-source conclusion is the exact parameter set `[50,20,14]`.

## Relation to `H-AXIOMPACK-BLC-20260720-02`

Choose an odd word `a` in `C`.  Choose any `b` in `C` with `p(b)=p(v)` and
put `w=v+b`, so `w` is even.  Although `w` itself depends on this choice,
the coset `w+C_0` does not.
Then

\[
C=C_0\sqcup(a+C_0),\qquad
v+C=(w+C_0)\sqcup(w+a+C_0),
\]

and parity sharpens the coset condition to

\[
\delta_C(v)\geq13
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
d(w,C_0)\geq14\ \text{and}\ d(w+a,C_0)\geq13.
\]

The first conjunct has exactly the positive construction form sought by
`H-AXIOMPACK-BLC-20260720-02`; the second is an additional companion-coset
condition.  If the puncture coordinate is the hypothesis's byte-frozen
coordinate zero, this is a subcone of that exact instance.  A different
coordinate gives a sibling shortening, and no negative result transfers
between the two without a certified coordinate automorphism.  Success at
either coordinate produces the target code and answers the hypothesis's
broad existential eigenquestion positively.  Exhausting this route excludes
only its paired subcone and leaves the larger hypothesis open: an even coset
`w+C_0` may have distance 14 while the odd companion `w+a+C_0` has distance
below 13.

Equivalently, put `v_hat=(v,p(v))`.  Then

\[
d(v_{\rm hat},L)
=\min_{c\in C}\bigl(\operatorname{wt}(v+c)+p(v+c)\bigr)\geq14
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \delta_C(v)\geq13.
\]

Thus the route searches for an **even** `[51,21,14]` one-dimensional
supercode of the frozen `L`; shortening that supercode at the parity
coordinate gives `E`.  This identity is useful for verification and does not
assert that such a supercode exists.

## Deterministic exact formulation

Put `C` in systematic gauge and select one even representative of each of
the `2^30` cosets of `C`.  The complete Boolean instance is

\[
\Phi_C(r)=\bigwedge_{c\in C}
  [\operatorname{wt}(r+c)\geq13].
\]

It has 30 quotient variables and exactly `2^20` canonically ordered
cardinality constraints.  The equivalent split form is

\[
\bigwedge_{c_0\in C_0}
 [\operatorname{wt}(w+c_0)\geq14]\ \wedge\
 [\operatorname{wt}(w+a+c_0)\geq13].
\]

A positive certificate is the representative plus exhaustive replay of both
`2^19`-word sectors, followed by rank/parity-kernel and full minimum-distance
verification of `E`.  A negative certificate for this frozen puncture is a
canonical CNF together with a checked LRAT/DRAT refutation; it proves
`rho(C)<=12`, equivalently that this frozen even-supercode cone is empty.
Incremental CEGIS is admissible when every rejected representative carries
its exact reducing codeword and the final learned-subset CNF is independently
proof-checked.  Timeout, stochastic failure, or solver `unknown` has the typed
outcome `certificate_unavailable` and supports no cone-exhaustion statement.

The syndrome version uses a `30 x 50` parity-check matrix for `C`: a positive
syndrome has no leader of weight at most 12, while a negative certificate
proves that every syndrome is generated by at most 12 columns.  This is the
same finite decision problem, with a compact BDD or proof-producing SAT trace
preferred over an explicit `2^30` leader table.
