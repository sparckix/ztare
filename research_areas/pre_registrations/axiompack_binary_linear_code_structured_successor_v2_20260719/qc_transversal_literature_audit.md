# Cyclic-transversal obstruction literature audit

Date: 2026-07-19

## Verdict

The repeated-difference core of `qc_transversal_obstruction_pencil.md` is
known mathematics. It is the failure of a `(5,5,5,1)` relative difference set
inside the cyclic group of order 25.

Dominic T. Elvira, *On Semi-Regular Relative Difference Sets in Non-Abelian
Groups of Order p^4*, Kyushu Journal of Mathematics 56 (2002), records as
Result 2.1 the low-order classification: a group of order `p^2` containing a
`(p,p,p,1)` relative difference set is cyclic only for `p=2`; for odd `p` it
is the elementary abelian group. The article attributes the result to Ma and
Pott. Primary source:

https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kyushujm/56/2/56_2_419/_pdf

The modern cyclic-RDS literature also treats these objects as liftings and
does not suggest a new `Z/25` exception; see Daniel M. Gordon, *Cyclic relative
difference sets and circulant weighing matrices* (2025):

https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14924
