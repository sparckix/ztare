## Scope boundary

Each receipt concludes only that one exact Lean target signature was ratified.
It does not adopt the claim identifier or conclusion of a broader filtered
premise.  Thus the arithmetic receipts do not silently certify:

- the algebraic Puiseux expansion or Julia/two-flow identities;
- infinite critical or moving-backbone support;
- maximal-face or adapter completeness;
- polynomial radial division or the full Magnus group; or
- the component lower and upper theorems consumed by the global comparison.

The enclosing branch evidence remains `adapter_exact` or
`filtered_obstruction_compiler`.  `enclosing_authority_upgraded` is `false` in
the replay envelope.
