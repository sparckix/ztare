# Kernel-ratified authority bridge for filtered evidence

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** supported; governed replay, transfer, and rejection tests pass

## Eigenquestion

Can a filtered-obstruction evidence receipt acquire formal-kernel authority
only by replaying LeanMill's existing governed carried-theorem record, while
preserving the established mathematical certificate identities and keeping
the scope of each formal claim exactly equal to the Lean theorem signature?

## Governing identities and lifecycle

LeanMill's `leanmill.governed_closure.v2` record remains the authority owner.
It binds an exact theorem target and normalized signature to posed-source,
proof, goal, toolchain, axiom-audit, matched-negative-control, governance, and
persisted kernel-parity identities.  The common filtered-evidence layer owns
only the immutable downstream projection of that already-ratified identity.

The only admissible positive lifecycle is:

1. open one already-proved Lean declaration with the canonical Lean source
   parser;
2. ratify those exact bytes through the existing governed carried-theorem
   executor;
3. resolve one append-only record by its canonical content hash;
4. replay the record against the exact target, signature, posed source, proof,
   goal, complete toolchain identity, and persisted parity record; and
5. construct a content-bound evidence receipt whose `formal_kernel` authority
   carries that immutable ratification binding.

The generic evidence factory must reject `formal_kernel`.  A caller cannot
obtain this authority from an enum label, digest-shaped string, or locally
asserted success field.

## Candidate theorem

If a governed record passes exact replay and its toolchain identity and
kernel-parity record are complete and content-bound, then LeanMill may project
one theorem-scoped `formal_kernel` evidence receipt.  Replaying that receipt
checks its binding content and digest without importing LeanMill into the
common layer.  Ordinary adapter/compiler receipts retain their current bytes
and hashes.

The first Jacobian support promotions are deliberately narrower than their
branch certificates:

- the moving-backbone arithmetic terminal theorem supports the
  least-positive induction;
- a new Puiseux arithmetic theorem supports the exact nonzero fractional
  coefficient and the impossibility of integral multiplicity `5/2` at the
  critical terminal;
- the radial-staircase arithmetic terminal theorem supports the admissible
  upper construction; and
- one unrelated arithmetic theorem exercises transfer outside the Jacobian
  vocabulary.

None of these receipts alone proves the adapter-owned algebraic decomposition,
all-order support theorem, formal-flow identity, polynomial radial division,
or Magnus construction.  Consequently the enclosing Jacobian branch receipts
remain at `adapter_exact` or `filtered_obstruction_compiler` authority until
their complete propositions are formalized.

## Proof skeleton

1. Extend the closed evidence-authority category with `formal_kernel` while
   making the ordinary factory fail closed for that value.
2. Define a canonical ratified-kernel binding containing the governed-record,
   theorem target/signature, source, proof, goal, toolchain, and parity
   identities.
3. Preserve every ordinary receipt byte by omitting the optional binding when
   authority is not formal-kernel.
4. Put the only public positive adapter in `ztare.leanmill`; call
   `validate_governed_ratification_record` rather than reproducing its rules.
5. Require the certificate ledger record hash to replay, the toolchain
   identity to be complete and self-hashing, and the parity record to be
   persisted and content-addressed.
6. Ratify the theorem-scoped carriers, issue support receipts, and attach them
   outside the established semantic payloads.
7. Re-run the full Jacobian certificate and compare every pinned semantic
   digest byte-for-byte.

## Attack vectors and counterattacks

- **Label minting:** a caller passes `EvidenceAuthority.FORMAL_KERNEL` to the
  generic factory. Reject before constructing a receipt.
- **Partial-theorem inflation:** an arithmetic carrier is presented as proof
  of a whole branch. Bind claim and subject to the exact theorem signature and
  expose it only as theorem-scoped support.
- **Cross-theorem graft:** reuse a valid record under another claim or target.
  Require exact target, normalized signature, source, proof, and goal replay.
- **Stale-toolchain graft:** retain a valid theorem record while replacing its
  toolchain object or identity. Recompute and require the toolchain content
  identity.
- **Parity laundering:** accept a claimed parity digest without durable
  persistence. Require the governed record's persisted bit and a valid parity
  digest.
- **Failed-kernel laundering:** alter a rejected or incomplete record into a
  binding. Require the existing governed validator and every positive receipt.
- **Parallel receipt dialect:** reimplement LeanMill governance in common.
  Keep common limited to immutable binding replay and call the sole LeanMill
  validator at the adapter boundary.
- **Semantic drift:** adding support changes a mathematical certificate hash.
  Keep support in proof envelopes or report metadata outside semantic payloads
  and assert all established hashes.

## Exact kill conditions

The bridge is rejected if a generic caller can mint formal-kernel authority;
if a partial arithmetic theorem silently upgrades a broader claim; if any
cross-target, cross-source, cross-proof, cross-goal, stale-toolchain,
unpersisted-parity, malformed-digest, or failed-governance fixture passes; if
common imports LeanMill; if governance checks are duplicated in a new dialect;
or if the global Jacobian semantic digest changes from
`24bee337068d65d8d81d1fa4ac584cec1130e3b160bef765f5afbd131acc1108`.

## Recurrence and amnesia check

`primitive_amnesia` surfaced the shared content-identity and filtered-evidence
primitives.  The semantic backend was unavailable, so repository identity
inspection was decisive: `carried_theorem_ratification.py` owns production of
governed records and `governed_ratification.py` already owns exact replay and
content-addressed resolution.  This campaign extends those owners rather than
creating a second kernel-certificate lifecycle.

## Intended formal surface

Existing Lean arithmetic terminal theorems remain unchanged.  The only new
Lean statement is the critical Puiseux arithmetic endpoint: the exact rational
coefficient is nonzero and no integer multiplicity equals `5/2`.  Python owns
record resolution, immutable authority projection, content replay, adversarial
identity checks, and attachment of theorem-scoped support receipts.

## Outcome

The bridge projects six governed theorem records into theorem-scoped
`formal_kernel` receipts: pure polar-tensor arithmetic, least-positive moving-
backbone arithmetic, critical Puiseux arithmetic, radial-staircase arithmetic,
global two-branch comparison logic, and an alien valuation/support fixture.
The ordinary factory and common replay path cannot authorize these receipts;
only the LeanMill ledger-backed replay succeeds.
