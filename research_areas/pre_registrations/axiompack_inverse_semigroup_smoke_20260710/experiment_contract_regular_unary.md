# AxiomPack regular-unary-semigroup frontier smoke

Prepared 2026-07-10 after the fully conditioned inverse-semigroup preflight was
killed and before this successor context or any provider dispatch.

## Hypothesis

Moving one level outward—from inverse semigroups to regular unary semigroups—
will preserve meaningful multiplication/inverse interaction while producing a
finite landscape large enough for discriminating anonymous navigation. The
host should either freeze an exact-two residual candidate with concrete
singleton countermodels or return a receipted null; any finalist then faces
larger-model and conditional Lean checks.

## Eigenquestion

Can AxiomPack identify a compound short identity mechanism inside regular
unary semigroups without being seeded with the inverse-semigroup
characterization, or does the short language still collapse to cheap
equational structure?

## Frozen substrate

The campaign uses the generic unary/binary signature. Its base states:

- associativity of multiplication;
- `x = (x x') x`;
- `x' = (x' x) x'`.

Thus the unary operation selects one semigroup inverse for each element. No
involution, product-reversal, commuting-idempotent, or inverse-semigroup axiom
is seeded. Candidate formulas are the complete canonical universal-equation
band through total operation order two. Size-two/three models are quotiented by
sort-preserving isomorphism with labeled multiplicity retained.

## Provider-free viability gate

Before launch, the named Hetzner preflight must report:

- 71 formulas;
- at least 25 canonical models;
- provider calls equal to zero.

Failure kills the campaign without navigation. The threshold is a minimum
geometry check, not evidence that any candidate will be interesting.

## Discriminating test

If the viability gate passes, run `campaign_regular_unary.md` through the
Hetzner `leanmill-campaign` action with Codex gpt-5.5 low and the frozen
20-minute subscription budget. For a finalist, execute only the selected
size-four/five SMT and one conditional Lean boundary. Source/literature review
begins after freeze.

Classify the first binding constraint:

1. no residual candidate: short-language/domain null;
2. missing singleton witness: false compound signal;
3. larger countermodel: small-context overfit;
4. Lean unresolved: formal-lift bottleneck;
5. proof unattributed: causal failure;
6. logically and proof-attributed but catalogued: positive control;
7. attributed and source-unmapped: candidate for a larger, independently
   reviewed campaign.

## Success criterion

The apparatus criterion requires no theory-named adapter, positive residual
coordinates, a host-replayed countermodel for every singleton implication, and
at least one concrete larger-model or governed Lean result. Scientific
escalation additionally requires a mechanism that changes the next experiment
after bounded source review.

## Kill conditions

- viability threshold fails;
- a relabeling duplicate changes selection credit;
- a base consequence or direct rewrite receives residual credit;
- a finalist has no concrete singleton countermodels;
- failed finite search is promoted into proof;
- inverse-semigroup literature enters the cold navigator;
- a standard characterization is presented as a new axiom;
- application rhetoric substitutes for mathematical evidence.

## Claim boundary

This campaign probes a finite short-equation frontier around inverse
semigroups. It cannot establish novelty, a finite basis result, practical
distributed-systems value, or comparative advantage over Prover9/Mace4-style
automated deduction.
