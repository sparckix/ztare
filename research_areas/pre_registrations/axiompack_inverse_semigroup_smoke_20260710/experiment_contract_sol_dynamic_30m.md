## Discriminating outcomes

1. no formula proposal and only seed rejection: the navigator did not use the
   new agency surface;
2. proposed syntax has an existing finite profile: conjecture quality or panel
   resolution is binding;
3. a new profile is admitted but yields no residual pair: the proposed
   distinction is measurable but scientifically empty in this context;
4. a residual pair fails singleton ablation: false compound signal;
5. a pair receives a larger countermodel: size-two/three overfit;
6. a pair survives finite checks but Lean is unresolved: formal-lift frontier;
7. a pair is proof-attributed but source-catalogued: positive-control
   rediscovery;
8. a proof-attributed, source-unmapped mechanism: candidate for an independent
   wider campaign, not a novelty claim.

## Success and kill conditions

Implementation succeeds if an agent-origin typed equation can be admitted,
replayed under a new context hash, and navigated without identity or budget
drift. Scientific escalation additionally requires positive residual
coordinates after the named structural baseline, concrete singleton
countermodels, and at least one larger-model or governed Lean result.

Kill the run on a runtime/model/effort mismatch, context digest drift,
unreceipted formula admission, syntactic-duplicate credit, primitive-collapse
credit, a finalist without singleton witnesses, or any promotion of bounded
absence into unrestricted truth. A receipted null remains an informative
outcome.

## Claim boundary

This 30-minute campaign tests whether agent-authored typed context expansion
changes the accessible search frontier. It cannot establish mathematical
novelty, a finite basis theorem, or general superiority over automated
deduction systems.
