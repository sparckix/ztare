# Full seed-cusp prefix quotient

**Status:** exact structural pencil theorem with deterministic symbolic replay;
the moving-family and BCH cost remain open

## Governing quotient

Put

\[
A=\mathbb Q[r],\qquad
f(r)=(-3r^2,-2r^3),\qquad
C=4P^3+27Q^2.
\]

The exact target lift ideals are

\[
\dot P\in(P,Q),\qquad
\dot Q\in(Q,P^2).
\]

For a Hamiltonian field

\[
X_K=(K_Q,-K_P)
\]

they are equivalent to

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H_{\rm lift}
=\mathbb Q\oplus(P^3,PQ,Q^2).
}
\]

Indeed, \(K_Q\in(P,Q)\) removes the \(Q\) term, while
\(-K_P\in(Q,P^2)\) removes the \(P\) and \(P^2\) terms.  Every other
nonconstant monomial belongs to \((P^3,PQ,Q^2)\).

Write a polynomial vector along the cusp in the basis

\[
T=(1,r),\qquad N=(0,1):
\qquad
h=(a,b)=aT+nN,\quad n=b-ra.
\]

Since

\[
f'=-6rT,
\]

\(n\) is the normal coordinate and

\[
\det(f',h)=-6rn.
\]

The compatibility relation is

\[
\boxed{
h\sim h+X_K(f)+f'u,
\qquad K\in\mathcal H_{\rm lift},
}
\]

where \(u\) must have a polynomial weighted-volume source lift and, for the
strict contact category, satisfy the original source lift ideals.  The normal
part is independent of \(u\).

## Potential image and normal image

The restriction map

\[
\operatorname{res}:\mathbb Q[P,Q]\longrightarrow A,
\qquad
P\mapsto-3r^2,\quad Q\mapsto-2r^3
\]

has kernel \((C)\).  Its image on the liftable Hamiltonians is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{res}(\mathcal H_{\rm lift})
=\mathbb Q\oplus r^5A.
}
\]

The exponent \(5\) is supplied by \(PQ\), exponent \(6\) by \(P^3,Q^2\),
and every exponent at least five belongs to the semigroup generated by two
and three with none of the three excluded low monomials.

If \(k=K(f)\), then

\[
n(X_K(f))
=-K_P(f)-rK_Q(f)
=\frac{k'}{6r}.
\]

Characteristic zero therefore gives the exact normal sequence

\[
\boxed{
0\longrightarrow
\bigl(\mathbb Q+(C)\bigr)
\longrightarrow
\mathcal H_{\rm lift}
\xrightarrow{\ K\mapsto n(X_K(f))\ }
r^3A
\longrightarrow0.
}
\]

The kernel is precisely

\[
\boxed{K=c+CL.}
\]

This separates two often conflated statements:

- target potentials restrict to \(\mathbb Q\oplus r^5A\);
- their Hamiltonian normal vectors fill \(r^3A\).

For a general polynomial vector along the cusp, the normal transfer
obstruction is exactly

\[
\boxed{n(h)\bmod r^3.}
\]

The exact lift-compatible motion space has

\[
a\in r^2A,\qquad b\in r^3A,
\]

because it is the restriction of
\(\dot P\in(P,Q)\), \(\dot Q\in(Q,P^2)\).  Hence

\[
n=b-ra\in r^3A,
\]

so every lift-compatible normal class transfers.  The three low normal
jets are an obstruction only outside the declared contact category.

## Normal transfer and the paired source obstruction

After choosing \(K\) with \(n(X_K(f))=n(h)\), put

\[
q=a-K_Q(f).
\]

Then \(q\in r^2A\) and

\[
h-X_K(f)=qT=f'u,\qquad
u=-\frac{q}{6r}\in rA.
\]

Every polynomial \(u\in rA\) has a polynomial weighted-volume lift in the
relaxed boundary category.  In the boundary source coordinates

\[
G=T-\frac32V,\qquad r=VG,
\]

the monomial \(u=r^m\), \(m\ge1\), is lifted by the potential

\[
L_m=\frac12V^mG^{m+2}
\]

and the vector field

\[
\boxed{
U_m=
\left(
\frac{m+2}{2}V^mG^{m-1},
-\frac m2V^{m-1}G^m
\right)_{(V,G)}.
}
\]

It obeys

\[
\operatorname{div}(G^2U_m)=0,\qquad U_m(r)=r^m.
\]

Conversely, if \(U\) is polynomial,
\(\operatorname{div}(G^2U)=0\), and \(U(r)\) is a polynomial in \(r\),
then \(U(r)\in rA\).  To see this, write

\[
U=G^{-2}(L_G,-L_V).
\]

Polynomiality of both components forces

\[
L=c+G^3M(V,G).
\]

A monomial \(V^\alpha G^\beta\) contributes

\[
(\beta-\alpha)V^\alpha G^{\beta-2}
\]

to \(U(r)\).  A nonzero fiber-constant contribution requires
\(\alpha=\beta-2=m\), hence \(m\ge1\).  Thus \(rA\) is the complete
polynomial weighted-volume tangent image before imposing the source lift
ideals.

The original source lift ideals sharpen this statement.  In \((V,T)\)
coordinates they are

\[
U_V\in(V,T),\qquad U_T\in(T,V^2).
\]

For \(m=1\),

\[
U_1=\left(\frac32V,-\frac12G\right)_{(V,G)}
=\left(\frac32V,3V-\frac12T\right)_{(V,T)}.
\]

Its second component has a forbidden \(V\)-linear term.  For every \(m\ge2\),
both components of \(U_m\) are homogeneous of degree \(2m-1\ge3\), so the
two source lift ideals hold.  The uniqueness argument above shows that the
\(r\) coefficient cannot be repaired by another polynomial weighted-volume
field without changing \(U(r)\).  Hence

\[
\boxed{
\{U(r):U\text{ satisfies weighted volume and both source lift ideals}\}
=r^2A.
}
\]

Before the source ideals are imposed, the paired quotient is

\[
\boxed{
A^2/
\bigl(\text{weighted source tangents}
      +X_{\mathcal H_{\rm lift}}(f)\bigr)
\simeq A/(r^2)\oplus A/(r^3),
}
\]

represented by

\[
\left(a\bmod r^2,\ (b-ra)\bmod r^3\right).
\]

The target lift ideals set both coordinates to zero in this relaxed
quotient, but the forbidden \(U_1\) leaves one further scalar obstruction in
the strict source category.  The combined strict image is generated by

\[
r^3A\,T,\qquad r^4A\,N,\qquad
-3r^2T+5r^3N,
\]

where the last vector is the restriction of \(X_{PQ}\).  Thus, on the
target-lift-compatible space \(r^2A\,T\oplus r^3A\,N\), the complete paired
obstruction is

\[
\boxed{
\omega(h)
=5[r^2]a+3[r^3](b-ra)
=2[r^2]a+3[r^3]b.
}
\]

Every normal class still transfers.  The full vector has a paired source
completion precisely when \(\omega(h)=0\).

## Stabilizer-reduced tangent normal form

The normal representative is unique only modulo \(K\mapsto K+CL\).
Along the cusp,

\[
X_C(f)=-108r^3T.
\]

Therefore the stabilizer changes \(q\) by a multiple of

\[
r^3\operatorname{res}\mathbb Q[P,Q]
=r^3\mathbb Q[r^2,r^3].
\]

Since

\[
r^2A/
r^3\mathbb Q[r^2,r^3]
=\langle r^2,r^4\rangle_{\mathbb Q},
\]

one can choose \(L\) so that

\[
\boxed{q=\alpha r^2+\beta r^4.}
\]

The relaxed boundary source coefficient is then

\[
u=-\frac{\alpha}{6}r-\frac{\beta}{6}r^3,
\]

and its weighted-volume lift is a combination of \(U_1,U_3\), of ordinary
source degrees one and five.  The strict source lift condition is exactly
\(\alpha=0\), equivalently \(\omega(h)=0\).  For a compatible prefix the
remaining source field is a multiple of \(U_3\), so it has degree five.

The relaxed pair is BCH-stable as well.  Direct calculation gives

\[
[U_m,U_n]=(n-m)U_{m+n-1},
\]

and in particular

\[
[U_1,U_3]=2U_3.
\]

Thus \(\langle U_1,U_3\rangle\) is a two-dimensional affine Lie algebra.
In the strict category the \(U_1\) coefficient vanishes, leaving the
one-dimensional abelian \(U_3\) remainder.

For a normal monomial \(r^dN\), integration gives a target potential
proportional to \(r^{d+2}\).  Choosing a monomial
\(P^\alpha Q^\beta\) with

\[
2\alpha+3\beta=d+2
\]

and maximal \(\beta\) gives target polynomial degree

\[
\left\lceil\frac{d+2}{3}\right\rceil.
\]

The extra stabilizer used for the two-term tangent normal form changes this
by only an additive constant.  Seed-cusp normalization therefore moves
unbounded normal degree to target degree with asymptotic factor \(1/3\),
while keeping the source tangent representative bounded by degree five.

## Critical Rees prefixes

At parameter order \(j\), a source potential on the pole-six critical Rees
face has original ordinary degree \(2j+12\).  If its boundary normal class
is fiber-constant, homogeneity forces

\[
\ell_j=c_jr^{j+6}.
\]

Its normal vector has degree \(j+4\) in \(r\), hence belongs to \(r^3A\).
Every finite critical normal source prefix therefore transfers
coefficientwise to a liftable target Hamiltonian.

For the campaign's polar witness

\[
h=(r^3,\tfrac34r^4),
\qquad n=-\tfrac14r^4,
\]

the minimal-degree normal lift begins with \(-Q^2/16\).  Stabilizer reduction
selects

\[
-\frac{Q^2}{16}-\frac C{144}
=-\frac{P^3+9Q^2}{36}=B,
\]

for which the paired source remainder vanishes exactly.  Thus the polar
counterexample is the first instance of the general quotient theorem.

## Consequence and remaining boundary

The seed cusp has no surviving normal-transfer obstruction inside the exact
target lift category.  Full paired completion has the single scalar
\(\omega\); every actual strict contact prefix must make it vanish.  After
that check, the source tangent normal form is degree five and abelian.
Therefore high normal degree at the seed cannot prove a lower bound for the
symmetric logarithmic tail statistic \(\sigma_{\rm ct}\).

It does not construct a low-slope full contact.  A finite coefficientwise
normalization must still be compatible with:

1. the positive transverse layers of the moving family;
2. polynomial lift through the full inverse cubic;
3. the source and target logarithms rather than only instantaneous fields;
4. BCH terms created by the chosen \(C\)-stabilizers.

The known \(C\)-stabilizer calculation shows that a finite choice can create
an infinite critical BCH tail.  The remaining problem is therefore a
costed moving-family normal-form theorem, not another seed normal-image
search.
