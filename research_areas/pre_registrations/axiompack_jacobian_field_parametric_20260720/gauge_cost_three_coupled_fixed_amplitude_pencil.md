# Coupled cost-three quotient after the exact positive-contact repair

**Status:** active successor pencil; no tail conclusion

## Eigenquestion

The contact-zero associated grade leaves a leading-amplitude terminal, but
the full lift algebra contains the exact cost-two repair

\[
R_2=-\frac12Q^2C,
\qquad
8R_2(P_0,Q_0)=-L_2.
\]

After applying this repair, the cost-three source Hamiltonian \(L_3\)
survives every lift-compatible target monomial.  Does the complete coupled
higher-contact recursion force infinitely many source or target logarithmic
responses of rate at least two at each fixed nonzero rational amplitude, or
can later rows cancel both sides below rate two?

This question is about the paired moving family.  A fixed target cokernel at
cost three is only the entry object.

## Governing identities

Write the cusp equation as

\[
C=4P^3-P^2-18PQ+27Q^2+4Q.
\]

The repair has positive \(C\)-adic valuation.  A contact-zero derivation need
not preserve a fixed power of \((C)\): for a Hamiltonian \(H\),

\[
\{H,C^mf\}
=mC^{m-1}\{H,C\}f+C^m\{H,f\}.
\]

Thus the coupled object is a cross-grade Rees or moving-contact complex.  A
fixed contact-grade coinvariant would discard the first leakage term.

On the source, density-\(z^2\) Hamiltonian monomials satisfy

\[
[u^az^b,u^cz^d]=(bc-ad)u^{a+c-1}z^{b+d-3}.
\]

Both the source-excess filtration and the target ordinary degree must be
retained.  A cancellation is acceptable only if it removes the chosen
terminal without creating a source surplus or a target row whose rate is at
least two.

## First post-repair module

The immediate exact tasks are:

1. insert \(R_2\) into the complete target logarithm rather than subtracting
   its source pullback only;
2. recompute the paired source row at cost three, including every BCH and
   forward-`dexp` contribution caused by \(R_2\);
3. quotient the resulting row by every same-cost lift-compatible target
   column;
4. compute the first later contact row that acts nontrivially on that
   quotient; and
5. retain amplitude as an indeterminate until the complete same-cost source

whose exact witness reads only source \(z\)-exponents seven through nine.

the cost-three representative or its annihilator.

## Attack vectors and counterattacks

### A. Full paired recomputation

Reuse the exact radial staircase and the side-typed formal Lie-series engine
to carry a prescribed cost-two target logarithm.  Compare three objects:

- the original coupled connection;
- the static subtraction \(L_2+8R_2(P_0,Q_0)=0\); and
- the fully recomputed target/source logarithms.

**Counterattack:** if the available prefix hook inserts an instantaneous
velocity rather than a logarithmic row, using it directly changes the BCH
problem.  The adapter must state which equation and coefficient type it
accepts and pass a forward-`dexp` round trip.

### B. Cross-contact transition

Compile adjacent \(C\)-grades and the source axis together.  The first
transition generated by a contact-zero or positive-contact row must either
remain in the target/source image or pair nontrivially with a shifted
annihilator.

**Counterattack:** projection to one chosen \(C\)-normal complement is
representative-dependent.  Relation transport and the negative filtration
order must be explicit; otherwise a finite rank is inadmissible.

### C. Fixed-amplitude collision graph

At each parameter cost, retain the polynomial in the amplitude \(\lambda\):

\[
\Omega_n(\lambda)=\sum_d\lambda^d\Omega_{n,d}.
\]

Build edges only between terms with the same cost and source pivot (or the
same target monomial).  Coefficientwise separation proves a formal-amplitude
result.  The fixed-amplitude result additionally requires that the scalar
polynomial paired with the terminal have no infinite compatible zero path.

**Counterattack:** distinct amplitude degrees can cancel after
\(\lambda=\lambda_0\in\mathbb Q^\times\).  A leading-amplitude terminal is
therefore insufficient unless another filtration separates the degrees or
the resulting exceptional polynomials are classified uniformly.

### D. Surplus-or-target payment

For every current control, form

\[
S=(\text{higher source pivots},\text{high-rate target pivots}),
\qquad T=(\text{selected terminal}).
\]

Use `compile_filtered_surplus_projection` to decide \(T(\ker S)\) on exact
finite base windows.  Any finite negative certificate must then be lifted by
an all-weight support, valuation, or transition argument.

**Counterattack:** combinations of individually expensive controls can
cancel their complete surplus.  The compiler must use the full kernel, and
later contact depth may add new columns outside the current window.

## Candidate result or obstruction

The first post-repair cost-three class either generates a covariant
cross-contact module with a finite exceptional amplitude divisor, or the
first exact coupled recomputation produces a cancellation schedule.  In the
former case, every escape from the exceptional module must pay rate at least
two on the source or target.  In the latter, the schedule must be iterated
and replayed on both group sides before it bears on the tail statistic.

## Kill conditions

- The full paired recomputation changes or cancels the purported \(L_3\)
  quotient.
- The first transition fails relation descent across adjacent contact
  grades.
- A fixed rational amplitude creates an infinite zero-payment path.
- A later contact row cancels the terminal inside the complete surplus
  kernel with target degree rate below two.
- Any asserted recurrence depends on a finite weight, cost, contact-depth,
  or amplitude-degree cap.
- Source and target forward-`dexp` replays disagree with the proposed rows.
