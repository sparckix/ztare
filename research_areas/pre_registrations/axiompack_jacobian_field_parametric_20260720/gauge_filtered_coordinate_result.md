# Gauge-minimal second source jet in the normalized Jacobian family

**Status:** exact theorem proved; terminal certificate kernel-ratified;
historical priority unconfirmed

## Theorem

Let

\[
F_0=(P,Q):\mathbb A^2_{v,t}\longrightarrow\mathbb A^2_{P,Q}
\]

be the normalized quotient seed, and let \(R_2\) be the second parameter
residual after subtracting the square of the fixed first target flow. Allow:

- a polynomial source field \(Y=(U,V)\) satisfying the full equivariant lift
  ideals
  \[
  U\in(v,t),\qquad V\in(t,v^2);
  \]
- an arbitrary polynomial Hamiltonian target field
  \[
  X_K=(K_Q,-K_P).
  \]

Among all solutions of

\[
R_2=DF_0Y+X_K(F_0),
\]

the minimum possible source component degree is exactly five:

\[
\min \max(\deg U,\deg V)=5.
\]

Thus the earlier degree-eleven coefficient belongs to its fixed target gauge;
it is not minimal under polynomial Hamiltonian target changes.

## Canonical target coordinate

Put

\[
\gamma=1-\frac32v+t,\qquad w=(1+v)\gamma.
\]

Then

\[
P=\gamma+2w-3w^2,\qquad
Q=w\gamma+w^2-2w^3.
\]

The cancellation discovered by the campaign is

\[
C=4P^3-P^2-18PQ+27Q^2+4Q,
\]

with exact pullback

\[
C(F_0)=\gamma^2(3P+\gamma-1)
      =4\gamma^3-9\gamma^2w^2+6\gamma^2w-\gamma^2.
\]

It also satisfies

\[
27Q^2=C+(18P-4)Q-4P^3+P^2.
\]

Consequently every target polynomial reduces to

\[
A(P,C)+QB(P,C).
\]

Under weights \(\operatorname{wt}(\gamma,w)=(1,2)\), the normal monomials
\(P^aC^c\) and \(QP^aC^c\) have distinct leading signatures and filtered
degrees

\[
4a+6c,\qquad 6+4a+6c.
\]

The scalar windows relevant to a source field of degree at most four are
therefore

\[
\mathcal F_8=\langle1,P,P^2,Q,C\rangle
\]

and

\[
\mathcal F_{10}
=\langle1,P,P^2,Q,PQ,C,PC\rangle.
\]

Coefficient comparison with the divergence-free condition removes the
\(C\) and \(PC\) directions. Every admissible target field in the component
window is generated by the Hamiltonians

\[
P,\ Q,\ P^2,\ PQ,\ Q^2,\ P^3,\ P^2Q.
\]

This explains the exact rank-seven stabilization observed at every tested
Hamiltonian degree from three through twelve.

composition space is the nearest conceptual overlap, and may contain related
directions implicitly, but its published claim and artifacts do not state
this minimization theorem.

The mathematical statement is therefore a credible narrow priority candidate
inside a newly active frontier. Historical novelty still requires specialist
comparison or acknowledgement from the groups working on the counterexample.
It is not another counterexample, an all-order gauge minimum, or a resolution
of the two-dimensional Jacobian problem.
