# Fixed-support local rigidity at the cubic weighted lift

**Status:** exact characteristic-zero calculation; novelty and a proof-assistant
translation remain open.

## Canonical-coordinate calculation

Use invariant coordinates `v=xy`, `t=x^2z` and write

\[
\gamma=1+av+t,\qquad P=\beta\gamma,\qquad Q=\alpha\gamma^2.
\]

The three-variable Keller condition becomes the two-variable mechanism

\[
\operatorname{Jac}_{v,t}(P,Q)=-\gamma^2.
\]

At the public cubic seed `p(w)=w-2w^3`, the normalized value is
`a=-4/3`.  Freeze the monomial supports generated by that seed:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{supp}(\beta)=\{&(5,0),(4,1),(4,0),(3,2),(3,1),(3,0),
(2,2),(2,1),(2,0),\\
&(1,2),(1,1),(1,0),(0,2),(0,1)\},\\
\operatorname{supp}(\alpha)=\{&(6,0),(5,1),(5,0),(4,2),(4,1),(4,0),
(3,2),(3,1),(3,0),\\
&(2,2),(2,1),(2,0),(1,2),(1,1),(0,2),(0,1)\}.
\end{aligned}
\]

To remove the visible family and coordinate motions, fix

\[
a=-4/3,\qquad b_{1,0}=1/3,\qquad b_{0,1}=-4.
\]

These three coordinates are transverse to target scaling, source `v`
scaling, and the cubic seed parameter: the exact transversality determinant
is `4/9`.

Expanding the Keller identity on this slice gives 69 coefficient equations
in 28 variables.  Their linearization at the weighted lift has rank 26, so
the apparent transverse tangent space is two-dimensional.  For the canonical
tangent coordinates `(S,R)`, second-order compatibility is equivalent to

\[
(2R+S)(5R+3S)=0.
\]

Thus only two infinitesimal directions survive the quadratic attack.  Both
fail one order later:

- on `S=-2R`, a third-order compatibility functional equals `16/81`;
- on `S=-5R/3`, the second-order correction parameters would have to satisfy

  \[
  12u_0+20u_1=7,\qquad 33u_0+55u_1=19,
  \]

  whose left sides differ by the factor `11/4` while the right sides do not
  (constant contradiction `-1/4`).

Therefore there is no nonconstant formal arc through this normalized point
inside the frozen support slice.  Equivalently, the public cubic weighted
lift is locally rigid in this coefficient chart after quotienting the two
scaling motions and the known seed-family direction.

## Claim boundary

This is a bounded local classification, not a classification of all
equivariant Keller maps and not another Jacobian counterexample.  It excludes
nearby off-locus families only for the displayed supports and normalizations.
Larger supports, different equivariant coordinate changes, and other points
of the weighted-lift family remain live.

The calculation is replayed exactly by
[`equivariant_sparse_local_rigidity.py`](equivariant_sparse_local_rigidity.py).
It uses rational-domain linear algebra and symbolic coefficient identities;
there are no floating-point rank or truncation decisions.
