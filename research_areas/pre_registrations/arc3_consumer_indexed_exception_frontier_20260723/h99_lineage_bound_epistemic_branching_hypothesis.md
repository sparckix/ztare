# H99: lineage-bound epistemic branching across two generations

Date: 2026-08-04

Status: pre-registered, offline mechanism test not yet run

## Inherited evidence boundary

H97 remains unobserved because the first live call stopped at the API billing
boundary before model, controller, or environment contact. H98 passed a
synthetic one-generation discriminator: four raw residual candidates compressed
to rank two, both independent children settled, `R_k = 2`, `R_e = 0`, and
shared trajectory cost was half the separate-test cost. H98 explicitly cannot
support a takeoff claim.

## Eigenquestion

Can promoted residual children be made into the exact causal parents of a
second settlement generation, with no authority drift or evidence recycling,
while validated knowledge remains supercritical and false-edge reproduction
remains subcritical after rank quotienting and full primitive-cost accounting?

## Hypothesis

One-generation offspring count can be manufactured by renaming correlated
branches. A two-generation capability-growth discriminator therefore requires
an identity-bearing lineage operator:

1. every residual candidate names the child identities from which it was
   derived;
2. every promoted child receives a content identity bound to its fission,
   settlement, evidence, authority, and lineage parents;
3. the next generation consumes the exact promoted-child set, with neither
   survivor cherry-picking nor an unrelated parent;
4. task, context, controller, choice set, action vocabulary, catalog,
   intervention revision, and primitive cost unit remain identical;
5. the next source program is the canonical quotient family of the promoted
   children, and its response derivative must be new;
6. trajectory, trial, and settlement-observation identities cannot be reused
   across generations;
7. each generation separately satisfies `R_k > 1` and `R_e < 1`.

For generation growth factors `R_k,g`, the finite-lineage growth statistic is
the geometric mean

`G_k = exp(mean_g(log(R_k,g)))`.

The analogous error statistic is `G_e`, with zero retained as an absorbing
value. A two-generation synthetic pass may earn only
`multigeneration_mechanism_candidate`. External settlement authority and
cross-task transport remain required before any capability-takeoff claim.

## Discriminating offline test

Generation one begins from one frozen root identity and uses H98's four raw,
rank-two fixture. It must promote two independent children with `R_k,1 = 2`
and `R_e,1 = 0`.

Generation two must:

- use those two promoted identities as its exact parent set;
- preserve the invariant authority coordinates;
- use the canonical descendant-family program identity and a new derivative;
- compile three independent residual directions over three measurement axes;
- promote all three under a full eight-trajectory factorial schedule;
- report `R_k,2 = 3/2` and `R_e,2 = 0`;
- use fresh trajectory, trial, and observation identities.

The lineage compiler must report `G_k = sqrt(3) > 1`, `G_e = 0`, final
validated descendants `3` from one root, and
`multigeneration_mechanism_candidate`, with `takeoff_supported = false`.

## Negative fixtures

- Replacing even one second-generation parent identity is rejected.
- Reusing the first-generation source program instead of compiling the exact
  promoted-child family is rejected.
- Reusing a derivative identity is rejected.
- Reusing a trajectory, trial, or settlement-observation identity is rejected.
- A second generation with only two promoted children from two parents has
  `R_k,2 = 1` and cannot pass through the geometric mean.
- Any generation with `R_e >= 1` blocks the lineage.
- Synthetic evidence can never set `takeoff_supported = true`.

## Success criterion

The positive fixture is accepted only when both generations are individually
supercritical for validated knowledge, subcritical for false edges, exactly
causally chained, authority-compatible, evidence-disjoint, and fully costed.
Every negative fixture fails at its declared boundary.

## Kill conditions

Kill the lineage mechanism if a generation can consume unpromoted or unrelated
parents, if child identities omit settlement evidence, if authority changes are
hidden as fields, if evidence can pay twice, if arithmetic averaging masks a
critical generation, or if synthetic generations are described as demonstrated
capability takeoff.

## Nearest-prior-art boundary

The components have established antecedents: multi-type branching and spectral
criteria; neural criticality; three-factor eligibility traces; micro-randomized
trials; active experimental design; Bayesian-surprise discovery; and recursive
skill evolution. H99 tests a specific compilation of those components for
externally settled agent judgment. It does not establish literature novelty.
