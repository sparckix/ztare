# H103 projected live protocol observation

Date: 2026-08-07

Hypothesis:
`H-GPSA-PROJECTED-LIVE-PROTOCOL-OBSERVATION-20260807-103`

Status: pre-registered; offline transition fixtures

## Eigenquestion

Can a concrete executed transition be projected into the exact abstract
source/operation/successor/effect observation required by H102 under frame,
action-history, and operation-effect-history identities?

## Hypothesis

The frozen mechanism-acquisition problem owns the projection and optional
history lift. For a concrete transition and its pre-transition action and
operation-effect prefixes:

1. the abstract source is `observed_start_key` at the pre-transition prefixes;
2. an ordinary successor uses prefixes advanced by exactly that transition;
3. the effect is the compiled-fiber mechanism effect between source and
   successor factors; and
4. an adapter-owned boundary carries no abstract successor or ordinary effect.

The resulting observation remains bound to the frozen projection, action
system, mechanism problem, history kind, and explicit live evidence reference.

## Discriminating test

Construct frame-only, action-history, and operation-effect-history mechanism
fixtures. Project the same ordinary transition with nonempty prefixes and
verify source, successor, and effect against direct calls to the frozen
problem. Then project an authoritative epoch boundary and verify partiality.

Pass the ordinary projection to H102 with a selected protocol target/probe.
Change the pre-transition history, projection identity, selected source, or
operation and verify refusal or a nonmatching identity.

## Success criterion

1. Frame-only source, successor, and effect equal direct projection.
2. Action-history source uses the old prefix and successor uses the exactly
   once-advanced prefix.
3. Operation-effect-history source and successor use the corresponding exact
   effect-token prefixes.
4. An authoritative boundary has no successor or ordinary effect.
5. The receipt binds projection, action system, problem, history kind, and
   evidence reference.
6. H102 accepts the exact projected target/probe observation.
7. History, source, operation, or projection drift cannot silently attach to
   the frozen forecast.
8. No task status or level count is read.

## Kill conditions

- source and successor use the same unadvanced history when a lift is active;
- history advances more than once;
- an ordinary effect crosses projection identity;
- a boundary receives an ordinary successor/effect;
- H102 accepts a source or probe mismatch; or
- task status enters projection.

## Claim boundary

Passing establishes concrete-transition projection into an H102 observation
under frozen fixtures. It does not establish that the ARC play loop attaches
the receipt at the correct selected-probe step, automatic episode assembly,
sealed live replay arms, H97 support, or score gain.
