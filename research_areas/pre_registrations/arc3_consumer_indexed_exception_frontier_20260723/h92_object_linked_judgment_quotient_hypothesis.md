# H92 object-linked judgment quotient hypothesis

## Eigenquestion

Did the H91 target intervention improve decisions by correcting the
controller's object/role plan, or did the apparent proposal response depend on
words that fail to identify the controller's operative object assignment?

## Hypothesis

A controller proposal becomes an inspectable policy state when it binds its
planned control and waypoint sequence to the same content-addressed object
catalog as the world model. At the frozen H91 observation, blind controllers
frequently assign control to the small `0/1` occurrence. The causal memory
should move at least three of four target proposals to the recorded `9/12`
controlled occurrence with the `0/1` occurrence as a waypoint. The
exact-byte-matched redundant-true placebo should produce that transport in at
most one of four arms.

The randomized target offer remains the instrument. Object-linked supported
transport is the endogenous response, and the fixed-cost external task score
is the settlement. Predicted first-stage transport difference is at least
`+0.50`. Target task score must be nonnegative relative to placebo and the
mean paired composite effect must be positive.

## Discriminating test

Compile the recorded initial grid into a frozen object catalog without task
labels. Field-like palette values are derived by component area; the remaining
multi-palette four-connected components receive content-addressed type and
same-observation occurrence identities. The grid adapter resolves two
pre-registered selectors into opaque refs:

- the 25-cell `9/12` occurrence is the required controlled object;
- the five-cell `0/1` occurrence is the required waypoint and a forbidden
  controlled object.

Every fresh Sol `xhigh` controller receives the same catalog and must emit the
controlled occurrence ref and ordered waypoint refs in both its blind and
revised proposal. Free text remains in the receipt but cannot determine
transport. Four alternating target/placebo pairs reuse H91's two-proposal-call,
3,849-byte, and 20-action controls.

The common kernel checks scope, controller, observation, catalog, proposal
ancestry, intervention consumption, reference resolution, plan displacement,
and contract satisfaction. It emits the first stage and reduced-form external
effect separately and refuses a complier estimate below the frozen first-stage
threshold.

## Kill conditions

Kill the construction if either selector is ambiguous; any proposal cites an
unknown or cross-observation object; typed output needs a lexical fallback;
target supported transport is below `3/4`; placebo spontaneous transport
exceeds `1/4`; the first stage is below `0.50`; target task score is lower; or
the paired composite mean is nonpositive.

## Algorithmic contribution under test

The candidate contribution is an **internal controllability operator**:

```text
(typed proposal basin, evidence intervention, cost)
    -> typed object/role-path displacement
    -> externally settled consequence
```

It extends the existing abstraction-functor discipline across the controller
boundary. `alpha_world` maps observations to content-addressed objects;
`alpha_judgment` maps proposals to paths over those exact objects. Response
credit attaches to a commuting displacement between the two views. Existing
memory-retrieval, attribution, and causal-replay papers motivate adjacent
pieces; H92 tests the narrower controller/worldmodel identity bridge and makes
no literature-novelty claim.

## Pre-contact apparatus correction

The first launch attempt on 2026-07-30 stopped during bundle construction,
before controller inference or any charged action, because the H92 adapter
spec had omitted the H88/H91 bundle constructor's producer-prior metadata.
The unchanged frozen values (`0.96` target, `0.99` placebo) were copied from
H91. They enter provenance receipts only; object transport, assignment,
costs, predictions, success criteria, and kill conditions were unchanged.

The corrected launch then reached the Responses request boundary but was
rejected before generation because that API's structured-output subset does
not admit JSON Schema's `uniqueItems` keyword. The keyword was removed from
the actor schema; duplicate waypoint refs still fail in
`ObjectLinkedControllerProposal`. No proposal, charged action, or outcome
preceded this compatibility correction.
