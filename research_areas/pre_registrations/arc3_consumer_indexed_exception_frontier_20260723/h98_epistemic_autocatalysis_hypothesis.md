## Eigenquestion

Can one settled parent response be inverted into several residual questions,
compressed to the rank of their independently distinguishable causal
signatures, and settled through a minimum-cost shared measurement basis so
that validated response reproduction is supercritical while false-edge
reproduction remains subcritical?

## Hypothesis

Raw descendant count is the wrong order parameter. Correlated copies can make
that count exceed one without adding a distinguishable judgment. The effective
offspring count is the exact rank of pre-outcome residual response signatures
under one frozen authority tuple. A minimum-cost observable basis can bind each
independent residual to a distinct pivot measurement while allowing one
successor observation to settle several pivots.

Define three separate rates:

- proposal reproduction `R_p`: offline candidate branches per parent;
- validated knowledge reproduction `R_k`: independently settled promoted
  children per parent;
- false-edge reproduction `R_e`: contradicted or spontaneous children per
  parent.

The candidate takeoff regime requires `R_p > 1`, `R_k > 1`, `R_e < 1`, full
settlement rank, bounded predicted-versus-observed information-yield error,
and lower shared live cost than separate child tests. One generation may earn
only `supercritical_mechanism_candidate`; evidence across at least two
successive generations is required for `takeoff_supported`.

## Discriminating offline test

Use one parent and four residual candidates over three observation axes:

1. two linearly independent signatures;
2. one positive scalar duplicate of the first;
3. one linear combination of the first two.

The compiler must:

- preserve the exact task/controller/context/choice-set/action-vocabulary,
  catalog, source-program, derivative, and intervention identities;
- quotient the scalar duplicate;
- count exact independent rank `2`, never raw count `4`;
- choose the minimum-cost two-axis measurement basis;
- accept two randomized offer/withhold settlements per basis child when
  causal uptake is `2/2`, spontaneous uptake is `0/2`, net external value is
  positive, and information-yield calibration is within tolerance;
- report `R_k = 2`, `R_e = 0`, and a supercritical mechanism candidate after
  one generation;
- refuse a takeoff claim because only one generation was observed.

## Negative fixtures

- Proportional or linearly dependent descendants do not increase effective
  offspring beyond matrix rank.
- A scalar settlement surface cannot promote two independent children.
- A candidate crossing any authority coordinate is rejected.
- Spontaneous or contradicted children that make `R_e >= 1` block the
  supercritical gate even if `R_k > 1`.
- Information-yield calibration outside the frozen tolerance blocks the gate.
- Two-generation status cannot be asserted from one settlement generation.

## Success criterion

The positive fixture compiles to independent offspring rank `2`, minimum
measurement cost, two promoted children, `R_k = 2`, `R_e = 0`, and
`supercritical_mechanism_candidate`. Every negative fixture fails at its
declared identity, rank, settlement, calibration, error, or generation gate.

## Kill conditions

Kill the mechanism if child count can exceed residual rank, if one scalar
outcome is double-counted as independent settlement, if any authority
coordinate crosses, if the cost basis is not minimal, if false-edge dynamics
are omitted from criticality, if calibration is descriptive only, or if one
synthetic generation is called capability takeoff.

## Claim boundary

Passing establishes a substrate-neutral algorithm and synthetic discriminator
for epistemic autocatalysis. It does not settle H97, establish live ARC
offspring, demonstrate two generations, establish broad capability takeoff,
or establish literature novelty.
