## Interpretation

H94 established prospective judgment inside one exact observation fiber. H95
shows that this local response can be reactivated after the observation
changes without embedding similarity, memory prose, role labels, or outcome
leakage. The admissibility decision depends on a commuting object/contract/
proposal square plus a lawful intervention re-rendering. When a required
object disappears, the same mechanism refuses before paying controller cost.

This is a stronger result than a static retrieval gain. A prior randomized
response changed later decisions under a new observation, and the compiled
transport predicted both typed uptake and cost-adjusted external value. It is
one certified reactivation edge, not yet an accumulated chain.

The next discriminator is path-sensitive composition. Two individually valid
one-hop transports may reach the same endpoint while disagreeing after
composition. A useful next operator must measure that loop defect and refuse
or discount the response before outcome when route history breaks the
consequence square.
