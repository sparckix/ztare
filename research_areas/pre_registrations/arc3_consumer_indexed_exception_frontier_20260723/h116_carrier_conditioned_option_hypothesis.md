# H116: carrier-conditioned option lowering

Date: 2026-08-06

Hypothesis:
`H-GPSA-CARRIER-CONDITIONED-OPTION-LOWERING-20260806-116`

Status: pre-registered after H115 settlement and before executing `(0,0,0)`
from any H114 active source

## Trigger

H115 shows that historical exact guards abstain under state shift and scalar
interpolation confidently fails. The accepted carrier already predicts
primitive transitions from a concrete state and is used by factored search,
but guarded option reindexing consults only witnessed graph edges. A motor
memory should nominate an operation word; current-state forward prediction
should determine its provisional effect and viability.

## Eigenquestion

Can the frozen accepted carrier lower a remembered word into correct ordered
effects on exact states absent from the historical skill corpus?

## Hypothesis

For remembered discovery word `(0,0,0)`, the carrier will be defined and
predict the exact ordered relative-effect signature on every one of H114's 51
active source states before environment execution. The predictions will span
at least two effect signatures while no word execution crosses a task boundary.

## Discriminating test

Using H114's frozen representative prefixes and the accepted H63 carrier:

1. verify H113--H115 results, carrier execution identity, projection, and
   cached environment;
2. verify `(0,0,0)` was retained from discovery and every target exact source
   is absent from the H113 discovery corpus;
3. replay one representative prefix to reconstruct each source grid and time;
4. before environment execution, roll the carrier forward through `(0,0,0)`
   and record predicted intermediate states, relative effects, and final state;
5. if any carrier step is undefined, emit typed refusal for that source;
6. execute the same word in the cached environment and compare ordered effect
   signature and final grid to the frozen carrier prediction;
7. distinguish exact historical-guard abstention from carrier-conditioned
   prediction and require at least one pair of distinct starts with the same
   predicted/observed effect;
8. mutate carrier execution, source, word, predicted intermediate, effect,
   final state, and environment identities in receipt-only controls.

Every environment action is charged. Environment outcomes cannot revise a
prediction within this audit.

## Success criterion

Stage A is supported only if:

- all 51 sources are exact-state novel relative to discovery;
- the carrier is defined for all three steps on all sources;
- every predicted effect signature and final grid matches environment
  execution exactly;
- predictions exercise at least two effect signatures;
- no word execution crosses a task boundary;
- at least one same-effect pair has distinct initiation states; and
- all identity mutations are detected.

## Kill and refinement rule

Reject on one defined prediction mismatch, undefined carrier step, final-state
drift, insufficient effect diversity, boundary crossing, source leakage,
post-outcome prediction change, or mutation leakage. A mismatch remains a
carrier counterexample; it cannot be repaired by loosening effect identity. A
pass licenses implementation of a carrier-conditioned option candidate whose
provenance remains predictive until external settlement.

## Claim boundary

Passing would establish carrier-conditioned effect transport for one remembered
word on one active state set. It would not establish task credit, task
transfer, controller benefit, benchmark gain, catalytic learning, criticality,
takeoff, or literature novelty.
