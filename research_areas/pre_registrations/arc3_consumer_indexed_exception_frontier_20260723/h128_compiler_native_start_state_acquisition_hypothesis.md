sessions. Treatment embeds the exact H127 selected digest reconstructed at run
time from raw H119 evidence and the fresh start grid under fresh context

scope/evidence/cost identities; it may not write target palette, coordinates,

## Outcome

The primary compiler-consumption prediction passed. Treatment acquired the
registered seam `[0,3,3,1]` in `3/3` arms, while control acquired it in `0/3`;
the difference was +3. The task prediction failed: treatment completed in
`1/3` arms and control in `0/3`, below the fixed completion threshold.

The independent audit passed 16 global checks across six unique sessions and
60 successful exchanges. It reconstructed the H127 compiler output, verified
the 15 declared redaction leaves and equal 3,912-byte envelopes, and replayed
every transition. The result is therefore
`supported_compiler_consumption_only`.

The two failed treatment arms preserved the learned start-to-seam behavior
but drifted after later state changes. This separates successful sparse recall
from recurrent state-conditioned control and makes online re-binding the next
discriminator.
