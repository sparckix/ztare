# H101: authority-typed capability autocatalytic closure

Date: 2026-08-04

Status: pre-registered, offline mechanism test not yet run

## Correction to scalar criticality

H98/H99 treat validated judgments as one branching species. H100 shows that
their reproduction depends on a separate measurement-design capability: the
same three-child settlement costs eight trajectories under the complete
factorial and four under a sparse Walsh code. Judgment, design, compression,
and external settlement therefore participate in joint-input reactions. A
scalar offspring ratio cannot represent these catalytic dependencies.

## Eigenquestion

Does the architecture contain a closed, authority-compatible set of capability
reactions that is food-generated, internally catalyzed, net productive in
capability coordinates, nonproductive in error coordinates, and feasible under
the primitive-contact budget?

## Hypothesis

Represent each durable capability as a species and each evidence-backed
improvement as a reaction with exact reactants, products, catalysts, primitive
cost, and authority. Fixed model competence and external observations form the
food set. A candidate critical-mass core must satisfy four independent gates:

1. **Food generation:** every reaction reactant is reachable from the food set.
2. **Reflexive catalysis:** every admitted reaction has a catalyst in the food
   closure or among capabilities produced by the admitted reaction set.
3. **Stoichiometric production:** a nonzero bounded integer flux produces a
   strict positive net amount of every declared capability species and a
   nonpositive amount of every error species.
4. **Resource feasibility:** the same flux fits the primitive-contact budget.

This is a catalytic reaction network rather than a simple graph. A cycle that
has no food-generated reactants, an uncatalyzed productive route, a
budget-infeasible route, or a route that amplifies errors cannot establish
critical mass.

## Discriminating offline test

Compile a four-species network tied to H99/H100 identities:

- food: base inference and externally settled evidence;
- capability `J`: lineage-bound judgment;
- capability `D`: sparse settlement design;
- error `E`: false-edge propagation.

Use three reactions:

1. food-backed bootstrap produces one `J`;
2. one `J` is retained while producing one `D`, representing bottleneck
   inversion from the H99 factorial wall to H100's design revision;
3. two `J` plus external evidence produce three `J`, catalyzed by `D`, with
   sparse cost `80` and no `E` product.

Under budget `100`, the compiler must find a food-generated, reflexively
catalytic flux with strict positive net `J` and `D`, nonpositive net `E`, and
status `productive_autocatalytic_core`. Replacing reaction three's sparse cost
with the complete-factorial cost `160` must remove resource-feasible closure.

## Negative fixtures

- Cross-authority species or reactions are rejected.
- A catalytic cycle whose reactants cannot be generated from food is not a RAF.
- A net-productive but uncatalyzed reaction is excluded.
- A RAF with no strictly positive capability flux is not productive closure.
- Any feasible flux with positive net error is blocked.
- Topological strong connectivity cannot substitute for stoichiometric flux.
- Synthetic reaction evidence cannot authorize a live takeoff claim.

## Success criterion

The sparse positive network passes all four gates with an inspectable exact
integer flux and net-production vector. Each negative fails its declared gate.
The legacy-cost counterfactual fails only resource feasibility, demonstrating
that H100 changes the critical-mass state of the same reaction topology.

## Kill conditions

Kill this formalism if a mere cycle passes, if food reachability or catalysts
are inferred from names, if a capability can cross authority, if floating
scores replace exact stoichiometry, if error growth is ignored, if cost is
checked on a different flux, or if an offline reaction fixture is reported as
observed recursive self-improvement.

## Prior-art and claim boundary

RAF theory, chemical reaction networks, stoichiometric autocatalysis, Petri
nets, hypercycles, Gödel-style self-improvement, and editable meta-agents are
established. The candidate research surface is their compilation with exact
agent-capability authority, external settlement evidence, primitive-contact
cost, false-edge species, and rank-quotiented epistemic lineage. A pass is a
theory/algorithm discriminator. It does not establish literature novelty,
endogenous meta-improvement, live ARC compounding, or capability takeoff.
