changes the consumer object to a pair of monotone predicate transformers over
an evidence-owned partial action system:

- `Pre_may(X)`: at least one witnessed successor enters `X`;
- `Pre_must(X)`: at least one successor is witnessed and every witnessed
  successor enters `X`.

An edge-valued task relation seeds the fixed point. A guarded skill is the
composition of its primitive transformers. The operative equality is agreement
on the task-conditioned predecessor interval, not equality of full future
trees.
