# Boundary-complete partial-action transport

Date: 2026-07-25

Parent tick: `tick-arc3-consumer-indexed-exception-frontier-20260723`

Frozen parent goal:

> Determine whether the Level 3 acquisition stall is caused by conflating transition-state equality with acquisition support, and either repair the consumer-indexed state-action frontier without substrate-specific coordinates or falsify that route with a replayable counterexample.

## Eigenquestion

When adapter and repeated-outcome evidence declares a source-operation
consequence to be a task-open boundary, does compiling that no-good into the
same partial-action relation prevent the planner from transporting through the
collapsed fiber and select a distinct evidence-backed frontier?

## Hypothesis

Boundary-complete transport will change control in three measurable ways:

1. an excluded source-operation witness becomes a boundary effect in the
   partial-action relation;
2. an open-loop route may traverse only relations with one effect, one target,
   and no boundary alternative;
3. any admissible witnessed frontier route takes precedence over a
   budget-exhausted simulated continuation.

On the next no-worker governed probe, the prior 12-step degeneration route
must not recur under the same projected boundary identity. Acceptable yield is
one of:

- a level event;
- a distinct unsupported operation pair reached through a single-valued
  witnessed route;
- a new projection counterexample whose source-operation class names the
  missing invariant;
- a typed `mechanism_frontier_exhausted` result.

## Discriminating checks

1. Unit replay: a relation containing both a successor and a boundary effect
   is not traversable by a fixed action sequence.
2. Boundary lowering: a controller exclusion becomes
   `("boundary", "control_exclusion")` and has no synthetic target.
3. Priority: a 5,000-state `search_budget_exhausted` continuation cannot
   replace an available witnessed frontier route.
4. Live replay: the next sealed trace does not repeat the previous projected
   boundary as a transport edge.
5. Authority: task discharge remains solely with the environment level-count
   adjudicator.

## Kill conditions

Refine or reject the representation if:

- the same projected relation again appears single-valued before execution
  and reaches a known boundary;
- a no-good can affect model search without appearing in the relation receipt;
- the planner executes through a relation with multiple targets, effects, or a
  boundary alternative;
- a simulated continuation overrides an admissible witnessed route;
- the repair requires a game-specific constant or solution sequence.

## Preflight result

The active-only audit using the exact sealed boundary edge changed the proposed
route from 13 to 15 actions while preserving zero ambiguous edges. The target
source fiber and operation remained the same. This admits a live probe only
because the governed controller has a stronger pre-existing rule: after two
presentations share the acquisition consumer identity, it transports the
no-good across that support fiber. The live relation must consume that
predicate. Reaching the same transported boundary therefore satisfies the
first kill condition.
