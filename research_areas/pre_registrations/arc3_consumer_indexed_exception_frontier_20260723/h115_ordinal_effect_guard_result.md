## Interpretation

Budget is a correlated feasibility summary, not a sufficient causal guard for
word `(2,1)`. The active effects reveal what the discovery corpus concealed:
the two operations independently translate, consume a budget item, or become
identity depending on local spatial relation. At the same budget, changing the
controlled base changes which effect occurs.

This rules out two weak generalizers: exact categorical lookup abstains under
shift, while monotone interpolation confidently predicts the wrong effect.
The accepted forward carrier already owns local transition prediction on a
concrete state. Skill execution should use that carrier to lower a remembered
word into a predicted effect path at the current state, then preserve model
provenance and require environment settlement. Historical effect clusters are
useful compression and calibration data; they cannot replace current-state
forward prediction.
