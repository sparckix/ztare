## Brain and automata conjecture

Exact episodic fibers and consolidated predictive state have different jobs.
The former preserve what happened and retain falsification authority. The
latter should be the coarsest provisional state for which a frozen family of
interventions has the same predictive consequences. This resembles a bounded
interventional Myhill-Nerode relation, but H108's target is a stochastic,
costed, receipt-backed learning operator:

- exact fibers are immutable and never deleted by quotienting;
- equivalence is learned from matched causal contrasts, not observational
  similarity or labels;
- a finite pre-outcome feature catalog and candidate projection library are
  frozen before fitting;
- the coarsest projection with no training sign contradiction is selected;
- its prediction is sealed before fresh controller forks;
- a failed fresh continuation refuses promotion and emits an epoch-bound
  counterexample for partition refinement; it cannot mutate the old epoch.

## Eigenquestion

Can exact matched episodes be consolidated into a coarser causal predictive
state that forecasts a fresh withheld controller branch, while exact episode,
assignment, primitive-cost, scope, and evidence authority remain recoverable?

## Authority and algorithm

The epoch authority binds:

- task, context, controller, complete choice set, and action vocabulary;
- response program, derivative, residual eligibility predicate, intervention
  set, utility measure, primitive-cost unit, and restored prefix;
- ordered pre-outcome feature catalog and complete candidate projection
  library;
- training settlement-set identity, epoch number, minimum matched support,
  prediction rule, and counterexample rule.

Each exact fiber binds one pre-proposal feature vector, parent/fork identities,
offer and withhold transition/evidence identities, matched primitive costs,
and externally adjudicated task and composite deltas. Training chooses the
coarsest candidate feature projection for which every quotient cell has the
target first stage, no negative matched value delta, nonnegative task sum, and
at least one positive value delta. Ties are resolved by canonical feature-key
order. The model predicts the exact rational training-cell mean and its sign.
It carries no promotion authority.

Fresh settlement maps each fiber through the frozen projection. Promotion
requires fresh evidence only, at least two distinct exact fibers in the same
predicted state, target first stage on every pair, no negative pair delta,
nonnegative total task delta, positive mean composite delta, at least one
positive pair, and exact prediction-sign agreement. A promoted child is the
predictive quotient state plus its immutable fiber witnesses, not a relabeled
training episode.

## Stage-B success criterion

- two fresh distinct exact fibers map to the frozen predicted state;
- causal proposal transport is `2/2`, placebo spontaneous transport `0/2`;
- causal-minus-placebo task delta is nonnegative;
- mean matched composite delta is positive and at least one pair is positive;
- no pair has negative composite delta;
- sealed positive-sign prediction accuracy is `2/2` when zero is counted as
  nonnegative but not as a win;
- the fresh quotient cell meets support two and promotes exactly one child;
- all training and fresh fiber/evidence identities are disjoint;
- reproduction changes `0 -> 1`; no supercritical claim is emitted.

## Counterexample/refinement rule

If a fresh pair has negative delta, target first-stage failure, an unknown
quotient cell, or sign-prediction failure, H108 rejects. It emits an immutable
counterexample receipt containing the failed fiber and the minimal set of
frozen pre-outcome feature keys that distinguish it from training witnesses.
That receipt may seed H109, which must create a new epoch and retest on new
evidence. H108 may not split, refit, or rescue its partition after seeing the
fresh outcome.

## Kill conditions

Kill H108 for any controller/scope/program/derivative/cost/prefix drift; exact
fiber loss; pooling by human label; feature or projection change after training;
post-outcome feature use; unmatched offer/withhold cost; duplicated training
or holdout evidence; a negative fresh delta; fewer than two target first
stages; nonpositive fresh mean; zero wins; sign-prediction error; child
promotion from H97 construction data; or mutation of the frozen epoch.
