# H126 result — relational judgment produced replicated task gain

Status: **supported task effect**.

At the exact Level-2 branch state, three fresh Sol-max treatment controllers
received H125's source-supported pose-to-motion relation and compiled
head-on/transverse route judgment. Three matched controls received the same
action map, object identities, graph, route lengths, goal role, uncertainty,
and 1,820-byte recall cost, with that relation and judgment withheld.

Treatment selected `down` in `3/3` sessions; control selected it in `1/3`.
Treatment completed Level 2 in `3/3`; control completed in `1/3`. Both
differences are +2 and pass the preregistered task-effect threshold. Every
treatment followed the exact seven-action oracle suffix, so the gain survived
the full oracle-tight remainder instead of stopping at the first decision.

The audit covers six unique controller sessions and 42 successful exchanges.
It verifies exact capsule lengths and declared leaf differences, canonical
one-shot prompt injection, restored branch identity, action-output equality,
and deterministic replay of every prefix and controller transition. A first
sandboxed attempt failed during app-server initialization before producing an
action or session; its trace is retained and excluded from the six-arm sample.

This is the first repeated task-level causal gain in the H87–H126 campaign.
The operative unit is the conjunction:

```text
palette/D4 invariant token identity
  + covariant pose -> motion relation learned from transitions
  + route-relative exposure and budget viability
  -> decision-seam intervention consumed by a fresh controller
```

The result remains within one game and one frozen branch. The probe generated
the target capsule from the offline compiler; it did not test autonomous
compiler invocation, online settlement of the target motion hypothesis,
cross-game transfer, or successive generations of improved memory. Those are
the remaining requirements for compounding.
