# Joint-test strength for compatibility-frontier acquisition

Date: 2026-07-26

Parent result: `predictive_support_identity_result.md`

## Eigenquestion

Can a consumer choose a support-completion experiment from a compatibility
relation without treating pairs that merely lack evidence as strong analogues?

## Claim

Every compatible source pair needs a separate joint-test strength:

- count operations witnessed on both members;
- retain only pairs whose shared tests have compatible effects and successors;
- rank a support gap first by joint-test count, then by exceptional effect
  value and route cost.

This preserves the distinction:

- compatibility is a possibility relation;
- joint-test strength is evidence for transport;
- a support gap is the missing experiment;
- behavioral identity remains unresolved until tests close.

## Discriminating test

Add joint-test strength to each support-gap receipt and replan from the current
Level 3 origin. Inspect the selected tested peer, untested consumer, operation,
shared-test count, route, and concrete evidence refs.

## Success criterion

The plan targets a reachable untested source, reports at least one jointly
witnessed compatible operation with its tested peer, and does not borrow the
peer’s outcome as a transition.

## Kill conditions

- a zero-joint-test pair outranks a supported pair;
- exceptional effect magnitude substitutes for compatibility support;
- an unreachable source is selected;
- the plan cannot name both concrete peers;
- a missing transition is inserted into the action relation;
- quotient class IDs become the option program’s persistent identity.
