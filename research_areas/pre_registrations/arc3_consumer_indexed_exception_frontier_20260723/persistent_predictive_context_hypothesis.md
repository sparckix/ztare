# Persistent predictive-context refinement

Date: 2026-07-26

Parent result: `relation_lineage_ownership_result.md`

## Eigenquestion

Is the one remaining non-commuting source-operation relation an artifact of
finite action/effect history, or does it witness a missing persistent
observation-derived context coordinate?

## Hypothesis

The accepted factor projection identifies concrete presentations that agree on
the current controlled, configuration, presentation, operation-domain,
feasibility, budget, availability, and finite-history coordinates but disagree
under a witnessed intervention. Extending either action history or
operation/effect history through suffix length 32 will therefore leave exactly
one non-commuting relation.

The collision should expose at least two relation-owned concrete witnesses whose
source observations differ outside the currently admitted factors. A
substrate-general counterexample-guided refinement may split them only if that
observation residual predicts a witnessed future-test difference without
copying the complete rendered state or naming substrate coordinates.

## Discriminating test

1. Recompile the latest sealed evidence with exhaustive action and
   operation/effect suffix candidates through length 32.
2. Emit the exact surviving relation: source identity, operation, effect
   identities, relation-owned evidence references, concrete source digests,
   current factor identities, and the observation residual between conflicting
   witnesses.
3. Search for the minimum persistent observation-derived partition that
   separates the conflicting future-test signatures. Evaluate it on held-out
   witnessed relations and reject source-injective or coordinate-memorizing
   partitions.

## Predictions

- Both history families retain exactly one non-commuting relation at length 32.
- The conflicting rows agree on every admitted factor and selected finite
  history but have distinct source observations.
- Their differing observation residual contains a persistent component or
  statistic that recurs beyond the two collision rows and predicts a future-test
  distinction.
- Adding that statistic as a context refinement reduces non-commutation or
  creates more than one witnessed context while preserving relation lineage.

## Success criteria

The refinement is derived from observation/evidence structure, is smaller than
the rendered source identity, replays deterministically, improves held-out
future-test prediction, and preserves all evidence-owned transitions and
references. It must not increment or imply the external level counter.

## Kill conditions

Any longer history candidate resolves the relation; the collision has no
distinct concrete source observations; no recurring observation residual
distinguishes the witnesses; the proposed split is source-injective,
coordinate-specific, or fails held-out prediction; or the apparent ambiguity
comes from evidence-lineage contamination rather than state identity.

## Claim boundary

This test concerns predictive state identity and acquisition control. It does
not establish task completion or teach a Level 3 solution.
