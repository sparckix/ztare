## Interpretation

The conjecture supplied one useful decomposition: online episode admission and
terminal value strengthening need distinct owners. This blocks the controller
from turning novelty, information gain, or episode similarity directly into
task value. The replay contract now owns counterfactual pair identity.

The conjecture also proposed that novelty magnitude should control which
episodes enter replay. H100 did not adopt that proposal because no result yet
shows that such filtering improves task judgment. Admission currently requires
a complete yield receipt, not a high yield value.

The remaining acquisition gap is narrower: ARC must emit a compatible
observed-information-yield receipt for a selected protocol. Predicted
identification is available at selection time, but the post-intervention
readout must be derived from an evidence-owned posterior reduction rather than
from level gain or task status.

## Claim boundary

This result establishes controller-neutral offline episode collection and
sealed replay binding. It does not establish an ARC observed-yield instrument,
automatic ARC play-loop collection, live counterfactual forks, H97 support, or
score gain.
