## Trigger and apparatus correction

H108 rejected its sealed positive prediction. Its two fresh exact-parent pairs
preserved causal proposal transport `2/2` and placebo transport `0/2`, but had
matched composite deltas `0` and `-93/100`, task delta `-1`, and zero wins.
The failed pair showed the offered residual changing a controller action that
the placebo branch later converted into a level gain.

Inspection exposed a prior contract defect. The H97 controller instructions
say to infer game rules from the supplied observation/action chronology. The
stored H96 prefix contains seven actions, eight settled observations, seven
environment transition receipts, and exact prefix SHA
`9fa0f034da648f03fbc1b64e313404a4466ea0eaa26acf223639d59533174a65`.
The H97/H108 initial controller call supplied only the final observation and
its object catalog. None of the seven action/observation transitions entered
the controller input. The persistent thread preserved later reasoning but had
no sensorimotor calibration history to preserve.

H97 and H108 retain their recorded verdicts under the endpoint-only controller
identity. H109 creates a new controller identity and output lineage; it cannot
repair, relabel, or promote either prior experiment.

## Brain-derived conjecture

Episodic recall should reinstate a perception-action trajectory before a
consolidated semantic residual is allowed to influence control. A semantic
route that is detached from the actor's learned action map can overwrite a
better working policy. H109 tests the first half of that conjecture by making
the exact prefix a controller-visible, content-addressed sensorimotor carrier.
It does not assume that chronology alone solves policy conflict.

## Stage A: chronology-carrier discriminator

Extend the H97 harness with two explicit initial-history modes:

- `endpoint_only`, the byte-identical default used by H97/H108;
- `exact_prefix_chronology`, a new controller coordinate.

The exact carrier must verify:

1. the prefix hash from the complete actions, observations, and transitions;
2. every settled-observation content hash;
3. action arity, action bounds, and `n` actions / `n+1` observations / `n`
   transitions;
4. every transition's index, action, source observation, and successor
   observation link;
5. equality between the final chronology observation and the current endpoint;
6. a deterministic rendered-input hash containing all eight observation
   receipts and images in order, with each intervening action named;
7. controller-scope change only in the controller coordinate, with the exact
   history authority bound into that coordinate.

The default path must reproduce the existing endpoint-only manifest and test
fixtures without a hash or payload change. Tampered action, observation,
transition, endpoint, renderer, or mode must refuse before controller contact.

## Stage B: fresh prospective discriminator

## Success criterion

- the chronology carrier passes every Stage-A identity/link check and every
  negative fixture refuses without controller or environment contact;
- causal proposal transport is supported on every pair and placebo
  spontaneous transport is zero;
- causal-minus-placebo task delta is nonnegative;
- no pair has negative composite delta;
- mean composite delta is positive with at least one positive pair;
- the result is recorded only under the chronology-restored controller
  identity and cannot reproduce an H97/H108 child.

## Kill and refinement rule

Kill H109 for endpoint-only/default drift; missing or reordered chronology;
invalid content or transition links; controller-coordinate laundering; any
first-stage miss; a negative fresh pair; negative task sum; nonpositive mean;
zero wins; evidence reuse; or cost drift.

If Stage A passes and Stage B still has a negative pair, chronology was a
necessary apparatus repair but not the missing policy mechanism. The next
epoch must bind recalled knowledge to an executable option with initiation,
grounded action policy, termination/failure, and a prospective advantage or
conflict gate. It may not add more advisory memory text and rerun the same
test.
