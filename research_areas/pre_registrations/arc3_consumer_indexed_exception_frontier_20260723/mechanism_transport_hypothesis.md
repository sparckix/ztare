# Partial-action transport and degeneration frontier

Date: 2026-07-25

Parent tick: `tick-arc3-consumer-indexed-exception-frontier-20260723`

Frozen parent goal:

> Determine whether the Level 3 acquisition stall is caused by conflating transition-state equality with acquisition support, and either repair the consumer-indexed state-action frontier without substrate-specific coordinates or falsify that route with a replayable counterexample.

## Prior result

The consumer-indexed support repair changed the selected intervention family,
but the second governed leg remained at two completed levels. It executed 86
active interventions, acquired two transitions, exhausted four lives, and
ended `plan_exhausted`. The factored consumer returned
`projected_frontier_exhausted`; the generic fallback then selected routes that
reached the same degeneration after 22–23 interventions.

This rejects the claim that separating transition equality from acquisition
support is sufficient.

## Eigenquestion

Does the acquisition stall persist because the control loop treats states and
state-action support as its governing objects, while the transferable object
is a partial action system whose operations, factor effects, undefined edges,
and degeneration fibers must be transported together?

## Governing identities

| Object | Job and owner | Lifecycle and authority | Equality or compatibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Witnessed fiber | Constrained reconstruction of one abstract state from evidence; owned by the abstraction producer | Bound to an evidence epoch and observation chart; diagnostic until certified | Equal only when forward projection returns the same abstract state and the evidence reference resolves |
| Partial operation | Relate a source fiber, intervention, and successor fiber or undefined boundary; owned by the action-system producer | Evidence-scoped; steering only | Compatible when source, operation, chart, and lifecycle identities match |
| Mechanism effect | Record which invariant factors change under a partial operation; owned by the consumer-specific factor projection | Evidence-scoped; steering only | Equal under the declared factor-effect relation, never raw presentation equality |
| Degeneration | Mark where an operation leaves the admitted lifecycle or loses feasibility; owned by the adapter boundary classifier | Adapter authority for the boundary; no task-discharge authority | Transportable only with a witnessed boundary-preserving relation |
| Task discharge | Decide whether the level advanced | Owned by the environment adapter | Environment-attested successor event only |

## Hypothesis

A consumer-indexed partial action representation will concentrate the two
failed live traces into one small degeneration family and expose at least one
distinct, feasibility-preserving mechanism frontier that state-support rank
does not prioritize. The representation must:

1. project observations into consumer-declared factors;
2. retain witnessed concrete fiber members as the only reconstruction surface;
3. classify each operation by its factor effect or adapter-attested boundary;
4. report non-commuting source-operation pairs instead of merging them;
5. rank low-support effect classes and degenerations separately from support
   volume.

No game-specific cell, color, coordinate, object name, or solution sequence is
admissible in the common object.

## Discriminating tests

1. **Witnessed section.** For every retained abstract fiber, selecting its
   evidence-backed representative and projecting it again must recover the same
   abstract identity.
2. **Action commutation.** Equal abstract source-operation pairs must either
   share the declared factor effect or emit a counterexample naming the erased
   factor.
3. **Boundary concentration.** The two governed traces must collapse their
   repeated 22–23-step failures into a smaller degeneration family without
   erasing the predecessor intervention.
4. **Opposing ranks.** Support rank and exceptional-effect rank must be emitted
   separately. At least one low-support mechanism or boundary class must move
   upward under the exceptional rank.
5. **Control consequence.** An exhaustive factor-consumer result must not fall
   through to a planner that omits its feasibility relation. The next action is
   either a feasibility-preserving mechanism experiment or a typed bounded
   obstruction.
6. **Governed probe.** A subsequent no-worker leg must produce one of: a level
   event, a previously unwitnessed mechanism effect, a commuting-projection
   counterexample, or a bounded obstruction tied to this representation.

## Kill conditions

Reject or refine this representation if:

- reconstruction invents a fiber member not present in evidence;
- an action effect depends on raw presentation after the declared factor
  projection;
- a boundary is inferred without adapter or repeated-outcome support;
- the exception rank is only inverse frequency and does not select a distinct
  mechanism or degeneration;
- the control consumer repeats a feasibility-losing route already represented
  by the same degeneration;
- the common object requires an ARC-specific noun or coordinate.
