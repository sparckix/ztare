# H-GPSA-GUARDED-PATH-CATEGORY-20260727-45

## Governing object

Before choosing state coordinates, take finite interaction histories as
objects with intervention words acting on the right.

Two histories are equal for the learner only when every admitted continuation
experiment has the same observable consequences and boundary disposition.
This is a partial, evidence-indexed right congruence. A distinguishing
continuation refutes a merge. A word that induces the same guarded partial map
across several initiation witnesses is a candidate reusable generator.

## Left fingerprint: action-history learning

- **carrier:** finite histories under right-concatenation by interventions
- **equality:** indistinguishable consequences under every admitted continuation
- **unit:** one witnessed intervention
- **composition:** concatenation where every prefix remains admitted
- **refinement witness:** a shortest continuation distinguishing two merged histories
- **compression objective:** a minimal generator basis for witnessed partial maps
- **fallback:** interpret primitive interventions when no compiled generator is admitted
- **authority:** observation and boundary receipts decide consequences

## Right fingerprint: guarded trace compilation

- **carrier:** execution traces under instruction continuation
- **equality:** observational equivalence under every guarded suffix
- **unit:** one interpreted instruction
- **composition:** concatenate trace regions when exit and entry guards agree
- **refinement witness:** guard failure or deoptimization side exit
- **compression objective:** a compact compiled region basis with positive amortized reuse
- **fallback:** resume the reference interpreter at the side exit
- **authority:** reference semantics decides compiled-trace correctness

invariants and selects the bounded discriminator above. Kill on same-basin
