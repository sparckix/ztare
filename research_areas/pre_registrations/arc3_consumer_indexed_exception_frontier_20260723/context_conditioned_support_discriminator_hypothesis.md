# Context-conditioned predictive support discriminator

Date: 2026-07-26

Parent result: `joint_test_strength_result.md`

## Eigenquestion

At two sources that agree on one witnessed operation, does the untested
consumer’s operation `1` select one of the peer’s two context-conditioned
effects, expose a third effect, or encounter a lifecycle event?

## Discriminating test

Execute one no-worker acquisition transaction with exact budget `16`. Require:

- policy `predictive_compatibility_support`;
- consumer action `execute_predictive_support_discriminator`;
- the exact 16-operation route recorded in the parent result;
- no ambiguous or boundary traversal edge;
- the queried consumer source and tested peer named in the support receipt.

## Predictions

Known contextual branch:
the queried operation yields one of the peer’s two effect classes. This closes
one consumer-indexed support gap and identifies which existing context branch
transports.

New exceptional branch:
the effect is disjoint from both peer effects. The compatible pair is refuted
or the relation gains a third context-conditioned alternative, concentrating
the next mechanism test.

Boundary or task event:
the external adjudicator reports a lifecycle edge or level increment. That
event owns task status and terminates the within-epoch comparison.

## Kill conditions

- the online planner selects a coverage-only frontier;
- the joint-test count is zero;
- the route or target identities differ from the preregistration;
- execution stops before the queried operation;
- a model worker proposes or judges an action;
- the peer outcome is inserted as if observed at the consumer;
- no sealed ordered lineage is written.
