## Intervention

Run three matched pairs with fresh game modules and fresh controller sessions.
Both arms receive the same exact H128 compiler capsule on turn zero. Before
turns 1 through 9, both arms invoke the same observation-bound H129 compiler
and receive one exactly 2,048-byte refresh envelope.

Treatment receives the compiler's current working-revision identity and one
current action. Control is a fixed mechanical redaction of its own current
revision: it preserves source-memory identity, exact observation/scope,
remaining budget, lifecycle-dependent settlement identity, refresh index, and
compilation cost, while withholding working-revision authority and the current
direction/action/contact fields. Control receives no alternative action.

Each treatment refresh authorizes one action only. The successor observation
must be scored before another revision is issued. When a controller ignores a
compiled action, that transition cannot settle the unexecuted prediction; the
provider must recompile from the observed state or emit a typed refusal. No
probe literal may name the target palette, coordinates, route, or oracle
suffix.

Arm order alternates from the frozen initial-capsule/H129 hash. Every arm runs
all ten primitive actions unless the environment itself ends the level; raw
prompts, outputs, turns, refresh receipts, sessions, and deterministic replay
are persisted.

## Success and alternative dispositions

1. `supported_recurrent_task_effect`: treatment completes at least `2/3` and
   exceeds control completions by at least two.
2. `supported_action_consumption_only`: treatment follows the compiler's
   current action on at least 90% of refresh turns and exceeds control by at
   least 20 percentage points, but the task criterion fails.
3. `refuted`: treatment completion and current-action adherence are each no
   greater than control's corresponding compiler-counterfactual metrics.
4. `inconclusive`: any other valid outcome.

## Audit and kill conditions

Audit six unique sessions, 60 successful exchanges unless an environment
boundary terminates an arm, identical turn-zero capsules, exactly nine dynamic
refresh opportunities per full arm, 2,048 bytes per refresh, declared
redaction paths, exact observation binding, one-use injection, source-memory
stability, settlement uniqueness, action-output equality, and deterministic
transition replay.

Kill for any target/oracle literal in the provider, any controller or game
state shared across arms, refresh outside the current observation, unequal
refresh bytes, undeclared control content, repeated authorization, failed
exchange, or replay drift.

## Claim boundary

A pass establishes recurrent within-game behavioral causality. It does not
establish cross-game transfer, a later non-equivalent acquisition derivative,
second-generation reproduction, critical mass, biological fidelity, or
literature novelty.
