## Interpretation

The prior holdout result measured compression of repeated control paths, not
state generalization. Its 138 saved control tokens remain valid, but the
chronological partition supplied no initiation-state intervention.

The one-coordinate discovery gates are candidate causal state variables. They
cannot be promoted from observational separation alone: another coordinate
may be correlated throughout the repeated corpus. The next discriminator must
actively vary the other factor coordinates while holding the candidate gate
fixed, then execute the frozen word and compare the predicted relative effect.
