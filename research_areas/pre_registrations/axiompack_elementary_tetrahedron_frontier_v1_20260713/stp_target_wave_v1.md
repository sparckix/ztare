## Discriminating sequence

1. Freeze the five-card deck without proof bytes in the prover-facing view.
2. Run four hidden-proof attempts per card to measure calibration difficulty;
   this stage earns no mathematical-result credit.
3. Reveal each seed proof to a target-conditioned Conjecturer and request at
   most two extensions, for at most ten candidates.
4. Apply namespace-aware statement parsing, well-formedness, cheap-closure,
   recurrence, and coordinate/term-equivalence checks.
5. A separate Guide ranks target relevance, formulation, non-redundancy, and
   discriminating power. Admit at most six.
6. Run finite/SMT refutation first where the candidate has executable finite
   semantics, then LeanMill proof search. Preserve `language_gap` and
   `unresolved` as typed outcomes.
7. Replay the frozen objective at matched `K=4`, with and without each admitted
   result. Count direct use, a changed proof residual, or an eliminated branch.

Runtime: Codex subscription transport, model `gpt-5.5`, ultra reasoning for
Conjecturer and Guide, no metered API. Each role has a distinct agent identity;
raw prompts, outputs, schemas, dispatch receipts, and wall time are retained.

## Candidate families

- weaken or remove middle injectivity, faithfulness, source fixing,
  normalization, or diagonal identity;
- distinguish reconstruction through some basepoint from every basepoint;
- formulate label factorization modulo the action kernel when the action is
  nonfaithful;
- generalize identity inverse-slices to a unary extraction and identify the
  resulting factorization law;
- find a minimal additional T2-groupoid identity forcing basepoint
  factorization;
- prove or refute converse constructions from label/cocycle data;
- transport only through verified coordinate or term equivalences, with the
  differential-mode matches retained as recurrence controls.

## Success and kill conditions

Continue if at least one admitted candidate yields a kernel-accepted lemma
used by the objective replay, a certified counterexample eliminating a stated
branch, or a typed capability gap that enables a previously unavailable target
experiment.

Redesign or stop this wave if all candidates are card replays, proof golf,
typeclass transfer variants, coordinate recurrences, or known differential-mode
statements; if solve rate rises while objective use stays flat; if all sampled
pass rates are zero or one; if malformed cards or unavailable language dominate;
or if six resolved candidates leave the characterization lattice unchanged.

## Claim boundary

The existing orbit/differential-mode classification is calibration/recovery.
The `Fin 3` reconstruction counterexample is a frontier-priority candidate with
kernel support and bounded prior-art review, not a confirmed originality claim.
This wave earns a new-mathematics claim only after target impact, unrestricted
formal verification, and a fresh primary-literature audit.
