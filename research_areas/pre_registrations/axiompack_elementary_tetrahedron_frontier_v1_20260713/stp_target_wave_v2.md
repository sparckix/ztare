## Hypothesis and eigenquestion

The affine hidden-fiber theorem and the complete pointed Boolean
classification expose a narrower residual than the original counterexample
question. Target-conditioned conjecturing should now distinguish at least one
of these possibilities:

1. a source-independent characterization of the full extraction fiber;
2. a necessary-and-sufficient reconstruction criterion extending beyond the
   known associative-ring construction;
3. a finite-cardinality or minimality classification not implicit in the
   published families; or
4. a certified reduction showing that these apparent extensions are also
   consequences of prior constructions.

The eigenquestion is whether the carried theorem deck plus the corrected
source context changes the characterization lattice, rather than generating
another presentation of `T(x,y,z)=y+xz`.

## Success and kill conditions

Continue a candidate only if it changes a declared necessary/sufficient branch,
produces a new finite obstruction, or supplies a kernel-accepted lemma used by
the exact extraction-fiber characterization.

Stop this branch if all candidates reduce to published associative-ring maps,
the already formalized affine calculation, proof variants, coordinate changes,
or unexecutable generality. A negative result is useful if it sharply identifies
which part of the apparent classification is already forced by a known family.
