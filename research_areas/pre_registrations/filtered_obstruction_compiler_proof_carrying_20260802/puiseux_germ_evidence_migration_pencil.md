# Shared Puiseux-germ evidence migration

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** supported; paired migration and rejection tests pass

## Selection rule

The plain polar-Witt lifecycle has five Boolean premises but its Jacobian
adapter is deliberately rejected and is not used in the successful global
branch.  The single-flow and two-flow Puiseux lifecycles contain six Boolean
premises between them and jointly certify the critical terminal consumed by
the successful split polar-tensor induction.  Positive dependency outranks
raw assertion count, so the paired Puiseux migration is next.

## Eigenquestion

Can both flow-obstruction compilers share one content-bound local-germ
identity and four non-substitutable propositions while preserving the
single-flow, two-flow, polar-tensor, and unrestricted-minimax semantic
digests?

## Candidate contract

One Puiseux context owns the germ identity, local coordinate, first
fractional exponent, exact expansion artifact, and canonical content hash.
Four proposition types are available:

1. the regular linear coefficient is nonzero;
2. the displayed first fractional coefficient is nonzero;
3. the germ is governed by the single-flow Julia identity; and
4. the germ is governed by the declared two-flow factorization identity.

The single-flow compiler requires claims 1--3.  The two-flow compiler
requires claims 1, 2, and 4.  In its proportional branch, claim 4 plus the
compiler-owned reduction derives a Julia-identity receipt with compiler
authority; the caller does not assert a second Boolean.

## Discriminating tests

- Preserve the existing alien single- and two-flow theorem digests.
- Preserve the Jacobian critical-terminal, polar-tensor, and global minimax
  semantic digests.
- Reject tampering, duplicate or missing claims, claim substitution,
  cross-germ/context grafts, finite truncation, finite-experiment authority,
  and a coefficient receipt not owned by the context expansion.
- Confirm that neither migrated problem dataclass has Boolean fields.

## Kill conditions

Reject the migration if six receipt-shaped flags replace six Booleans, the
single- and two-flow compilers acquire incompatible germ identities, the
proportional reduction still trusts a caller Boolean, finite-series evidence
is promoted to an exact flow identity, or any established semantic digest
changes.

## Formal surface

multiplicity, finite-route analyticity, infinity degree comparison, and

not add a new mathematical axiom.
