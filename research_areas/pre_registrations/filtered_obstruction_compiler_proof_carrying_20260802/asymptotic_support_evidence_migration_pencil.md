# Asymptotic occurrence-support evidence migration

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** supported; graph-bound migration and rejection tests pass

## Selection rule

After the tail-composition and polar-tensor migrations, the remaining
positive dependency of the unrestricted Jacobian certificate with the
smallest avoidable trust surface is the asymptotic induction compiler.  Its
input still accepts one Boolean assertion plus one unattached digest for the
claim that occurrence support is infinite.  This edge directly produces the
least-positive-contact component consumed by the global minimax compiler.

## Eigenquestion

Can infinite occurrence support be represented as one authority-typed,
content-bound proposition whose context is the already compiled induction
transition graph, without changing the asymptotic theorem digest or the
global Jacobian conclusion?

## Candidate contract

The finite induction compiler already returns the governing graph identity.
The asymptotic compiler should require exactly one receipt with:

- claim `infinite_occurrence_support`;
- scope `all_unbounded_occurrence_indices`;
- subject equal to the asymptotic problem identity;
- context equal to the induction transition-graph SHA-256;
- conclusion `cardinality=infinite` and `index_cofinality=unbounded`; and
- authority `adapter_exact` or `filtered_obstruction_compiler`, but never
  `finite_experiment`.  A kernel authority is deliberately unavailable until
  it is constructed from the existing LeanMill ratification receipt rather
  than from a caller-supplied label.

The compiler continues to own positivity of the affine order slope,
same-order payment, no rebilling, exhaustive closing witnesses, and the rate
inequality.  The adapter continues to own the substrate recurrence theorem
carried by the evidence digest.

## Discriminating tests

- Preserve the alien asymptotic theorem digest under migration.
- Preserve the Jacobian least-positive-contact digest and the global
  tail-minimax digest.
- Reject receipt tampering, a finite scope, finite-experiment authority,
  wrong subject, and a receipt bound to a different induction graph.
- Confirm that the migrated problem dataclass has no Boolean fields.

## Kill conditions

Reject the migration if the graph digest remains unattached to the support
claim, a finite computation can authorize an all-order conclusion, the
receipt can be grafted across induction graphs, the established semantic
digest changes, or substrate terminology enters the common evidence layer.

## Formal surface

the matching rate comparison.  This migration changes premise identity in
Python and should require no new mathematical axiom.
