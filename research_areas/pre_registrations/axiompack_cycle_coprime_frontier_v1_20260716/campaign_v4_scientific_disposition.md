# Campaign v4 Scientific Disposition

Date: 2026-07-17

## Status

`retired_before_navigation / target_already_resolved`

The frozen v4 campaign remains immutable. It should not be launched against its
original open-question premise.

## Reason

The campaign reconstructed a valid indecomposable 49-point cycle set with a
nontrivial 3-cycle coprime to its carrier size, then the temporal source audit
located earlier and stronger counterexamples:

- Kanrar–Rump published a family in 2024, with the smallest reported example
  of size 256;
- Bonatto–Castelli v3, revised 2026-05-07, explicitly records that family and
  gives simple counterexamples of size `p^p`; `p = 3` gives size 27.

The 49-point Lean certificate is mathematically valid and useful as a worked
translation of the Cedó–Okniński construction. It carries no priority as the
resolution of the decomposition question.

## Preserved outputs

- exact targeted `UNSAT` checks at orders 6 and 7;
- replay of 5,251,733 published order-8/9/10 tables;
- the common-prime cycle pattern on all 152 indecomposable tables in that
  database;
- displacement-transitive and singularity residual audits;
- deterministic and Lean checks of the 49-point witness.

These are bounded or recovery results. None licenses an unrestricted theorem.

## Apparatus consequence

The pre-freeze source check treated an older paper's open-question wording as
current. Future frontier selection must bind the target to:

1. latest-version and publication dates;
2. forward citations and directly related later constructions;
3. executable target-predicate replay over retrieved examples;
4. a typed `catalogued_recovery` disposition before navigation spend.

The target-predicate replay route and temporal source-coverage checks are the
general repair. A cycle-set-specific blacklist would overfit this incident.

## Next mathematical use

campaign. Any successor needs a different, currently verified residual; it may
not inherit the Question-30 objective by renaming it.
