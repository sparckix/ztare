# Targeted order-6 coprime-cycle boundary

**Status:** Exact bounded result; unrestricted question remains open.

Date: 2026-07-16

## Result

The targeted order-6 query is `UNSAT`: no nondegenerate cycle-set table on six
elements simultaneously has

1. a transitive action generated by its left translations, and
2. a nontrivial left-translation cycle whose length is coprime to six.

At order six the second condition is exactly the presence of a 5-cycle. This
extends the campaign's exact no-counterexample chart from orders 2–5 through
order 6 without enumerating every order-6 isomorphism class.

## Encoding

The solver uses one binary operation `*` and asserts:

- every left row is a permutation;
- the diagonal map `x ↦ x*x` is a permutation;
- `(x*y)*(x*z) = (y*x)*(y*z)` for all `x,y,z`;
- every nonempty proper carrier subset has a membership-crossing edge under
  some left translation, which is equivalent to transitivity of the generated
  action on this finite carrier;
- some left row has cycle type `[5,1]`.

The 5-cycle predicate is the full disjunction over row indices, fixed points,
and 5-cycles. The reported result therefore does not depend on the faster
two-case relabeling normalization used during the pilot.

## Matched controls

- Base theory plus a 5-cycle, without transitivity: `SAT`; the fixed witness
  has action orbits of sizes 5 and 1.
- Base theory plus transitivity, without a 5-cycle: `SAT`; the fixed witness is
  the constant action by a 6-cycle.

Both witness tables pass a separate concrete replay of row bijectivity,
diagonal bijectivity, the cycle-set identity, cycle types, and action orbits.

## Claim boundary

This is an exact finite SMT result for order 6 under the displayed encoding.
It supplies no unrestricted implication and no priority claim. A universal
advance still requires an invariant or representation theorem, while a
negative answer still requires a checked counterexample at a larger order.
