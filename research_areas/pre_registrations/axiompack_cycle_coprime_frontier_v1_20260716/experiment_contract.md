# AxiomPack coprime-cycle decomposability frontier campaign

Frozen before campaign preflight or navigation on 2026-07-16.

## Printed residual

For a finite cycle set `(X, ·)`, Ramírez and Vendramin asked whether the
existence of a nontrivial cycle of length coprime to `|X|` in some left
translation forces `X` to be decomposable. Castelli proved the implication for
finite multipermutation cycle sets and recorded the unrestricted question as
Question 30 in *Publ. Mat.* 69 (2025), 429–444. Exact-phrase and forward-topic
search before freeze found no later resolution.

Primary sources:

- https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/pubmat/pubmat_a2025v69n2/pubmat_a2025v69n2p429.pdf
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.04483
- https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-90653-4_1

## Hypothesis

An exact small-order cycle-set chart, combined with agent-authored executable
translation-cycle and action-orbit coordinates, will do at least one of the
following:

1. produce a fully checked indecomposable counterexample to the unrestricted
   implication;
2. isolate a compact invariant or representation that strictly strengthens the
   published finite-multipermutation argument;
3. return an exact representation-gap or reject-all receipt showing why the
   current AxiomPack language cannot discriminate the residual.

Reproducing the published restricted theorem or finding no bounded
counterexample does not satisfy the hypothesis.

## Eigenquestion

Can the campaign turn anonymous operation tables into the permutation-action
coordinate needed by the printed question, then use that coordinate to change
the mathematical status of the unrestricted implication?

## Executable substrate

The typed signature contains a primary binary operation, its rowwise inverse,
and the inverse of its diagonal map. Five frozen equations encode finite
nondegenerate cycle sets. The initial exact SMT census covers carrier sizes
2–6; sizes 7–9 are boundary strata. The published counts through size 6 are an
external calibration checksum, not discovery credit.

The initial language deliberately omits cycle length, gcd, action orbits,
retraction, and decomposition. A navigator must request those through the
language-successor compiler or a reviewed campaign-local capability. Metadata
without executable coverage is rejected.

## Discriminating outcomes

1. Exact census fails to reach final UNSAT at any initial stratum: preparation
   incomplete; no scientific inference.
2. The navigator remains inside the short-equation chart: objective unmet.
3. A reviewed coordinate computes translation cycles and action orbits but does
   not change a held-out prediction: representation lift only.
4. A candidate counterexample fails a cycle-set law, coprimality, or
   indecomposability check: rejected witness.
5. A checked indecomposable table has the required coprime nontrivial cycle:
   negative-answer candidate, then Lean and independent literature gates.
6. A general implication survives finite and formal boundaries but uses the
   published finite-primitive or multipermutation hypothesis: recovery.
7. A stronger formal implication removes or replaces that hypothesis and
   survives post-freeze source review: frontier-novel candidate, not a priority
   claim.
8. Budget exhaustion with complete per-lineage receipts: closed null or typed
   successor request; never a mathematical absence claim.

## Harness falsifiers

- Four isolated lineages must receive distinct durable call namespaces.
- Every compiler, navigation, forge, boundary, and interpretation call must
  persist prompt and result bytes incrementally.
- Campaign-local capabilities may not mutate the global adapter registry.
- A typed language request must be compiled once by the campaign advancement
  state machine; rejection or runtime unavailability must return to navigation
  as typed feedback.
- Terminal evidence retained after a budget stop must pass cold provider-free
  replay before campaign closure.

## Claim boundary

The run may establish a finite counterexample, a formally checked implication,
or a representation advance. It cannot establish global priority from source
absence, convert finite persistence into an unrestricted result, or claim a
classification of all cycle sets.
