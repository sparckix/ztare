# Campaign v4 control amendment

Frozen on 2026-07-16 before v4 preflight or navigation.

This amendment leaves the printed Ramírez–Vendramin question, anonymous typed
signature, five base equations, cold-navigation policy, runtime roles, budgets,
and scientific claim boundary unchanged. It changes only where carrier size 6
enters the lifecycle.

## Change

- Initial exact context: carrier sizes 2, 3, 4, and 5.
- Held-out boundary: carrier sizes 6, 7, 8, and 9.
- Initial per-stratum deadline: 900,000 ms.
- Size 6 may be queried through a targeted executable predicate or a reviewed
  representation capability. A complete size-6 taxonomy is not an admission
  prerequisite.

## Reason

The v3 discriminating test reached `unknown: canceled` at size 6 after the
frozen 90-minute deadline. Exact sizes 4 and 5 already distinguish the target
phenomenon: indecomposability and the coprime-cycle premise both occur, while
their intersection is empty. The open question asks about that intersection,
so a targeted size-6 counterexample query has higher information yield than
enumerating every size-6 isomorphism class before an agent can inspect the
chart.

## Claim boundary

The initial context is complete only for sizes 2–5. Any size-6 or larger result
must carry its own boundary receipt. Absence of a held-out counterexample never
becomes an unrestricted implication. A checked held-out counterexample, or a
formal invariant that advances the unrestricted implication, remains the
scientific success condition.
