# Prelaunch exact action audit: carrier sizes 4 and 5

Date: 2026-07-16

This audit was computed outside the navigator loop from two preserved,
provider-free exact AxiomPack contexts. It is not included in the cold campaign
inputs.

## Question tested

For each canonical finite cycle-set table, compute:

1. the orbits of the group generated by the left translations
   `L_x(y) = x · y`;
2. whether any `L_x` contains a nontrivial cycle of length `k` with
   `gcd(k, |X|) = 1`;
3. whether both conditions hold with a transitive generated action.

For finite cycle sets, transitivity of the generated action is the standard
indecomposability test. Thus item 3 is the finite counterexample predicate for
the campaign target.

The size-5 split is particularly discriminating: the coprime-cycle premise is

campaign should therefore seek an action-level explanation of the disjointness
or its first failure, rather than spend its budget merely detecting coprime
translation cycles.
