# Formal residual coverage compiler

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** pre-registered; compiler implemented, Puiseux coverage active

## Eigenquestion

Can AxiomPack compute the exact formalization residual of a broad mathematical
claim from theorem-scoped governed receipts, while preventing partial leaf
coverage from being mistaken for formal authority over the root claim?

## Governing identity

The object is a finite content-bound claim-decomposition DAG.  Its job is to
name one root proposition, its required leaf propositions, and the inference
rules that assemble children into parents.  Its owner is LeanMill because
formal support must be replayed against governed theorem records.  Equality is
canonical DAG-content equality.  Compatibility requires an exact proposition
identity, not a similar theorem name or a digest-shaped value.

The existing audited conjecture DAG has a different lifecycle: it proposes and
kernel-audits proof-search decompositions.  The common graph carrier validates
diagnostic provenance.  This compiler consumes already-declared mathematical
claim structure and governed theorem receipts to measure coverage; it neither
plans proofs nor substitutes for the kernel.

## Candidate coverage theorem

Each node has a unique identity, one proposition identity, and zero or more
children.  A formal proposition identity binds the target identifier,
normalized target-signature digest, posed-source digest, and Lean toolchain
identity.  Leaves require a governed theorem whose complete formal proposition
identity equals the declared leaf identity.  Every internal node analogously
requires a governed inference theorem with the exact declared identity.
Coverage is the least fixed point generated by covered leaves and supported
inference rules.

The compiler emits:

- directly covered propositions and inference rules;
- the bottom-up covered node set;
- uncovered leaves;
- uncovered inference rules and their blocked children;
- whether the root is derivable from governed leaves and rules;
- whether an exact governed theorem directly ratifies the root; and
- the exact residual frontier.

Root promotion eligibility is deliberately stricter than bottom-up coverage:
all reachable leaves and inference rules must be covered, the root must be
bottom-up covered, and an exact governed root theorem must also exist.  The
compiler emits eligibility only.  Authority issuance remains the existing
governed-ratification transition.

## Identity kinds

A proposition identity is either:

1. `lean_target_signature`, in which case a formal receipt may cover it only
   by the exact content digest of target, signature, posed source, and
   toolchain identity; or
2. `adapter_semantic`, which records a mathematically declared residual before
   a Lean signature exists and is never coverable by a formal receipt.

This distinction prevents a semantic adapter statement from being declared
formal merely by attaching a theorem with a convenient name.  Formalizing such
a leaf creates a new DAG version with a precise Lean target signature.
Inference identities obey the same distinction.  A semantic composition rule
cannot be covered by supplying a theorem receipt whose digest merely resembles
its content hash.

## First Jacobian decomposition

The root is the critical two-sided Puiseux terminal exclusion used by the pure
contact-zero branch.  Its initial required leaves are:

1. exact selected algebraic-germ expansion at `x=-2`;
2. nonzero regular and first fractional coefficients plus the impossibility of
   integral multiplicity `5/2`;
3. Julia's formal-flow identity for the critical holonomy;
4. the degree-independent two-polynomial-flow factorization obstruction; and
5. an inference theorem composing those leaves into the terminal exclusion.

Leaf 2 already has governed formal receipt
`dc3c205e70124f469a5fd5421873568c0dce822e4b4233fdddc0a3db55e0eeb0`.
Leaves 1, 3, 4, the inference rule, and the root theorem are expected to form
the initial formalization residual.  A finite Julia series check may remain a
stress test but cannot cover the all-order formal-flow leaf.

Before attempting a complete Puiseux-series encoding, the first tractable
mechanism target is the universal arithmetic used inside leaves 2 and 4:

1. cancellation of a nonzero fractional coefficient in Julia's root branch
   forces an integral multiplicity to equal `5/2`; and
2. for `1 < e < d`, the two-flow transition exponent
   `1 + (d-e)/(d-1)` lies strictly between one and two and therefore differs
   from `5/2`.

These theorems may become governed formal subleaves, but they do not by
themselves formalize the selected algebraic germ, Julia's all-order identity,
or the structural two-flow alternative.  The decomposition must retain those
semantic obligations after the arithmetic refinement.

The selected-germ leaf admits one further exact refinement.  A separate Lean
carrier can prove the polynomial discriminant factorization, its linear scale
`24` at `x=-2`, the simple-zero factor in the radical coefficient, and its
nonzero quotient `-25/1344`.  The passage from these scales through the chosen
square-root branch, logarithmic integration, and the endpoint `u^(5/2)` term
must remain an adapter-semantic series obligation until formal series or local
analytic germs are encoded.

## Proof skeleton

1. Replay the decomposition schema, exact node set, unique proposition hashes,
   root reachability, and acyclicity.
2. Replay every formal support through the existing governed theorem bridge.
3. Reject duplicate receipts, extra signatures, cross-DAG support, nonformal
   authority, or a formal receipt offered for an adapter-semantic leaf.
4. Match receipts to proposition and inference-rule signature hashes.
5. Compute leaf and rule coverage, then the bottom-up fixed point in
   topological order.
6. Emit the residual frontier and a content-addressed coverage certificate.
7. Require complete leaves, rules, bottom-up root, and a direct root receipt
   before reporting promotion eligibility.

## Attack vectors and counterattacks

- **Leaf-count laundering:** one formal arithmetic leaf is presented as the
  terminal theorem. Require exact coverage of every reachable leaf and rule.
- **Name graft:** a theorem with a similar target name covers a semantic leaf.
  Match complete governed formal proposition identities only.
- **Cross-context signature graft:** an alien source defines the same printed
  identifier differently while preserving the target signature text. Bind
  target, normalized signature, posed source, and toolchain as one identity.
- **Declarative decomposition laundering:** a caller asserts that trivial
  leaves imply a broad root. Require governed inference-rule signatures and a
  directly ratified root before eligibility.
- **Disconnected debt hiding:** omit a difficult node from root reachability or
  add it as unreachable decoration. Reject unreachable nodes and derive leaves
  from the root graph.
- **Cycle masking:** a parent and child cover one another without a base leaf.
  Reject cycles before coverage.
- **Duplicate support masking:** reuse one receipt in multiple incompatible
  roles. Require unique receipt identity and exact signature matching.
- **Finite-to-formal promotion:** use a finite series check for an all-order
  identity. Adapter-semantic leaves remain uncovered until an exact Lean target
  exists and is governed.
- **Compiler authority inflation:** treat bottom-up coverage as a formal root
  receipt. Emit eligibility only; issue no content-bound formal receipt.

## Exact kill conditions

The proposal is rejected if a partial leaf set produces root eligibility; if
unreachable, cyclic, duplicate, extra, cross-context, or signature-mismatched
support passes; if adapter-semantic content can be covered by a formal receipt;
if the compiler creates formal authority itself; if alien transfer requires
Puiseux vocabulary; or if any established Jacobian semantic digest changes.

## Recurrence and amnesia check

Semantic primitive retrieval surfaced `decomposition_dag_audit`,
`derive_conjunctive_dag`, `DecompositionCache`, graph carriers, and the new
governed theorem-to-evidence bridge.  Repository inspection confirms that the
first three own proof-search proposal/audit/cache and source-claim carriers own
evidence provenance.  None computes the formal coverage residual of a declared
mathematical claim.  This compiler will reuse governed receipt replay and
canonical content identity without absorbing proof search or provenance
lifecycles.

## Intended formal surface

Python owns DAG replay, governed-receipt matching, coverage fixed point, and
residual reporting.  Lean owns each leaf theorem, each inference theorem, and
the root theorem.  For the Puiseux application, no new Lean statement will be
accepted merely because it encodes a conclusion as a premise; every proposed
leaf must expose the algebraic or formal-series mechanism used by the Python
adapter.
