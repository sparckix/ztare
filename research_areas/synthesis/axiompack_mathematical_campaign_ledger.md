\quad\text{plus failure-domain and helper-availability constraints}.
\]

Matched controls must replay known optimal constructions and degraded-repair
failures. A successful artifact could be relevant to cloud-storage design;
neither the present binary witness search nor minimum distance by itself
supports a deployment or cost-savings claim.

**Queue status:** promising application-facing successor after a primary-source
residual audit; no AWS-product or savings claim.

**Source-audited target ranking (2026-07-18).** The best first campaign is the
exact maximum (4)-uniform local arc in (PG(2,13)). Determine (M_4(13)),
equivalently the longest Singleton-optimal

\[
[4m,3m-3,6]_{13}
\]

code with disjoint locality-(3) repair groups. The current audited interval is
(5\le M_4(13)\le12). A terminal positive needs an explicit block system; a
terminal optimum additionally needs a replayable SAT or ILP exclusion
certificate for the next value. Controls reproduce the exact (q=7,8,9,11)
cases, accept the known (q=13,m=5) construction, and reject duplicate-point
and collinear-triple mutations. Primary anchors:
[Ihringer--Zhou](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23692) and
[Xiong--Lu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21494). The audit also caught a stale
(q=9) gap in the earlier paper that the later paper closes, making
revision-aware forward-citation replay part of the campaign contract.

The higher infrastructure-relevance successor is simultaneous intermediate-
(h) rack-aware MSR repair with optimal access. One finite probe fixes

\[
(\bar n,u,n,\bar k,v,k,\bar d,\bar s,h,\ell)
=(4,4,16,2,1,9,3,2,2,12)
\]

over a frozen finite field and asks for MDS recovery plus exact bandwidth and
access support for every two-node failure inside one rack. The literature
provides endpoint controls at (h=1) and (h=3), while the audited interior

canonicalization. These are substrate-class capabilities, not target-specific
flags.

### 1. Improve the lower bound for \(R(5,5)\)

The strongest broader follow-on is to construct a graph \(G\) on 43 vertices
such that every five-vertex set spans between one and nine edges:

\[
|V(G)|=43,
\qquad
0<\left|E(G)\cap {S\choose2}\right|<10
\quad\text{for every }S\in{V(G)\choose5}.
\]

Such a graph has neither a \(K_5\) nor an independent five-set and would improve
the lower bound from \(R(5,5)\ge43\) to \(R(5,5)\ge44\). The current upper-bound
paper proves \(R(5,5)\le46\); the lower-bound study verifies Exoo's construction
and reports 43-vertex near-misses with few monochromatic \(K_5\) subgraphs.
The exact witness predicate is small, near-misses expose concrete offending
five-sets, and the agent may choose Cayley, circulant, block, orbit, switching,
or another representation. Primary anchors:
[Angeltveit--McKay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15709) and
[Ge--Jayasooriya--Qiu--Sun--Yuan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12630).

**Queue status:** leading post-binary campaign; graph witness adapter and
kernel-certificate control still required.

### 2. Construct a Hadamard matrix of order 668

The direct target is

\[
H\in\{-1,1\}^{668\times668},
\qquad HH^{\mathsf T}=668I.
\]

A 2025 primary source still describes 668 as the smallest open order and gives
a 64-modular approximation using a special Golay quadruple. This offers strong
compressed representations and a decisive witness, but is close in apparatus
shape to the binary-code campaign. Primary anchor:
[Eliahou](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/93/ajc_v93_p422.pdf).

**Queue status:** verifier-first backup after \(R(5,5)\).

### 3. Raise the plane chromatic lower bound to six

The witness target is a finite exact unit-distance graph in the Euclidean plane
with no proper five-coloring. A successful graph would prove that the plane
requires at least six colors. The accepted lower-bound construction currently
establishes five. This target has high conceptual upside, but exact algebraic
coordinate replay and an independently checkable coloring-UNSAT certificate
must be available before launch. Primary anchor:
[de Grey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02385).

**Queue status:** deferred until its coordinate and UNSAT certificate boundary
passes matched controls.
