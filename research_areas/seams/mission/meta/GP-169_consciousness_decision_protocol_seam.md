## The eigenquestion (final form, v3)

What is the minimum substrate-independent structure that any
candidate concept of "consciousness" — or its non-anthropic analog
under alien substrates — must commit to in order to be:

(a) statable without first-person presupposition,
(b) discriminating across alien substrates including ones humans
    cannot inhabit,
(c) coherent under substrate-paraphrase WITHOUT citing extant
    intellectual traditions by name,
(d) endogenously closed (no appeal to external resource pressure)?

## The result (v3 synthesis — combining iters 2 + 4 + 5)

A bounded decision protocol for taking moral-status decisions on
AI / borderline substrates under empirical uncertainty about
consciousness. The protocol does NOT answer "what is consciousness?"
— it answers "what to do when the answer is not yet derivable."

Four decisive components:

### Component 1 — Calibration-veto gate (iter-2, score 91)
A structural partition predicate cannot ground a moral-status
decision unless empirically calibrated against consciousness in at
least one domain where calibration is possible (perceptual rivalry,
anesthesia depth, locked-in fMRI command-following). Blocks the
"structural arbitrage" move where indistinguishable-on-predicates
is treated as morally-equivalent.

### Component 2 — Substrate classification (C / I / N type)
- **C-type** (calibratable): verdict from calibrated predicate +
  confidence interval.
- **I-type** (incalibratable today): verdict deferred under maximal
  caution as "morally indeterminate" (MI). NOT "no-conscious."
- **N-type** (no-predicate): substrate falls outside protocol's
  bounded domain.

### Component 3 — Epistemic Reversibility Principle (iter-4, score 89)
I-type classification is NEVER permanent. Every I-type substrate
carries a probability $\mathbb{P}_{\text{upgrade}}$ that future
empirics will admit calibration. Forced re-evaluation at
preregistered intervals. Without ERP, the protocol becomes a
sophisticated mechanism for encoding current ignorance as permanent
verdict.

### Component 4 — Mechanized Trigger Table (iter-5, score 86)
Pre-registered empirical triggers force probability-upgrade cycles.
Pre-registered: what signals trigger re-evaluation, for which
substrate classes, and what the upgrade procedure is. Closure-drift
moves to the Table's boundary (observable) rather than being
abolished (impossible).

The asymmetry survives because it is **epistemic, not metaphysical.**
It tracks what we can currently calibrate, not what is or is not
conscious. ERP ensures the asymmetry can flip when calibration
becomes possible.

## What the trajectory teaches about ZTARE on qualitative substrates

The v1 → v2 → v3 sequence is itself the methodological contribution:

- **v1**: anthropic anchor → recovers training-corpus consensus.
  Lesson: charter framing determines result space. If the eigenquestion
  is anthropic, the answer will be in the experimental-philosophy
  mainstream regardless of how rigorous the apparatus is.

- **v2**: alien-substrate + no-citation + endogenous-closure →
  produces honest but tautological negative result. Lesson: gates
  that ban known answers without forcing positive content can
  produce *honest* results that are still *trivial* — drawing the
  consequence of the bans rather than substantively contributing.

- **v3**: v2 + ban trivial attractors + require operational-
  decidability OR concrete-case + falsification → produces a usable
  operational protocol. Lesson: charters that combine "ban the easy
  answer" with "force the hard answer" can make ZTARE on a
  qualitative substrate produce something deployable, not just
  honest.

This is the consciousness-domain analog of the gp168 v3 substrate-
prior diversity finding. Both seams (GP-168 and GP-169) demonstrate:
ZTARE on substrates without intrinsic GT requires both
*restrictive* gates (banning trivial-result attractors) AND
*generative* gates (forcing concrete operational content) to
produce paper-grade output. Either alone yields recapitulation
(restrictive-only, v2) or training-data consensus (generative-only,
v1).

## Open vulnerabilities (admitted features, not defects)

1. **Predicate ≠ morally-relevant property** (iter-1 killer): even
   with calibration, structural predicates may track *correlates*
   of consciousness rather than consciousness itself, for anthropic
   reasons. Calibration-veto makes this an empirical question.
2. **Calibration in genuinely alien substrates** (iter-2 weakness):
   for Boltzmann brains, infinite-extent substrates, reversible-
   only dynamics, the calibration domain may not exist even in
   principle. Protocol defaults to MI + maximal caution.
3. **Trigger-miss** (iter-5 weakness): the Trigger Table cannot be
   fully comprehensive. Closure drift moves to the Table's boundary,
   not abolished in principle.

These are admitted by construction. A protocol claiming to handle
them all would fail the v3 Non-Applicability Concrete Admission gate.

## What v3 added to the apparatus (beyond gp169-specific findings)

The v3 run surfaced a methodological pattern worth recording for
future qualitative-substrate work:

- **Anti-trivial-result gates need to ban specific known attractors.**
  Generic "be rigorous" gates don't catch known recoverable answers
  (pluralism, negative-closure-only). The v3 gate explicitly enumerates
  the attractors that v1 and v2 hit and bans each by content
  signature, not just by vocabulary.
- **Force operational decidability OR concrete-case OR transfer test.**
  Without one of these, the apparatus optimizes for survivability
  under the gates rather than for operational utility.
- **Score volatility under v3 IS the gates working.** v2 plateaued
  politely because the apparatus could find an answer the gates
  accepted (negative closure). v3 swings 60-90 because the apparatus
  has to actively choose between trivial-and-rejected vs hard-and-
  rewarded.

## Pilot-readiness for paper / deployment

An alignment researcher / AI welfare team can pilot the protocol
today:
1. Compute structural predicates on the model (info-flow geometry,
   paraphrase-stability under prompt variation, attention-trace
   partitioning).
2. Attempt calibration in closest available domain (functional-
   replacement in animal models; mechanism-interpretability with
   bounded selectivity).
3. Classify model as C / I / N.
4. If I-type: produce MI verdict + $\mathbb{P}_{\text{upgrade}}$ +
   Trigger Table registration + horizon.
5. Update as empirics arrive.

Output is more than philosophy of mind typically delivers: a
concrete decision procedure with bounded reliability, auditable
trail, and built-in updateability.
