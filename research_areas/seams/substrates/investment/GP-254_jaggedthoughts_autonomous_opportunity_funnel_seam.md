## Eigenquestion

Can a broad public-market search become a continuously operating research
funnel whose spending, selection, evidence, handoffs, and later consequences
are inspectable at every transition?

## Recommendation

Use the existing ZTARE workbench as the shell and keep the finance kernel
independent of React and HTTP. Run saved market scouts on cadence, compile a
bounded enrichment cycle, use durable leases for public-source work, and emit
exact candidate-leaf research requests. Let the JaggedThoughts research skill
define the dossier contract, and let a separately leased subscription consumer
invoke it under a daily budget. Preserve paper activation as a separate
operator transition.

The system should be treated as a governed research acquisition engine. Its
current score chooses where to spend public data and attention. Prospective
return enters only through the existing type-specific screen and, later, a
settled paper decision.

## Strategy isomorphism

The strategy frontier and investment funnel use the same structural idea at
different levels:

```text
strategic options + interactions + scenario scope
  -> recursive programs -> equivalence -> global frontier + local peaks

market candidates + research costs + diversity + source scope
  -> marginal acquisitions -> feasibility -> selected research batch
```

The first exposes strategic traps and missing choices inside a declared
company representation. The second allocates scarce evidence acquisition
across securities. They share identity, bounded search, witness, and residual
discipline. Their objective meanings remain separate.

Institutional learning adds the cross-epoch isomorphism:

```text
company choice program -> operating consequence
investment formula program -> later return consequence
portfolio program -> after-cost book consequence
```

All three use recursive enumeration and scope-bound frontier closure. A broad
mechanism signature generates a transfer question; the mechanism phenotype is
the comparison key; the exact company environment, object, and break conditions
remain moderators. The output of a strategy law, such as earnings
durability, may become an input to a return law. The chain remains prospective
until each edge earns its own compatible evidence.

The shared search census is the accounting membrane around those three loops.
Before outcomes, it freezes the exact candidate family and generator receipts;
afterward, it checks that no losing formulas, strategy laws, model variants, or
portfolio policies disappeared from the denominator. Existing historical
surfaces without that commitment stay diagnostic, and an exhaustive frontier
certificate remains a search receipt rather than an alpha claim.

The census now includes all nineteen pending closed-book episodes, grouped into
six exact horizon-and-candidate families, plus the pending complete-policy
episode. Those seven families and the institutional-law family are bound into
the common registry before their outcome boundaries; future opening paths do
the same at first use. The first executable
selection-bias consequence is a Bonferroni screen: a covered tournament's
selected economic candidate must survive its paired block-permutation test after
adjustment by the full committed trial count. Current status is `0` available
and `0` passing. Richer Sharpe and combinatorial-overfitting labels remain
blocked until their typed return-matrix inputs exist.

Research expenditure now uses the same institutional-learning committee. A
small deterministic scheduler reprioritizes admissible subscription jobs by law
separation, cohort deficit, new context, and decision closure, with diminishing
returns across repeated entity/action pairs. A bounded class-service bonus and
seven-day starvation guard prevent permanent omission without replacing the
learning proxy for fresh jobs. All information-yield coordinates are labelled
upper bounds until later outcomes measure them. The adjacent transfer-acquisition
policy audited all 62 moves, removed 15 already-covered cohort jobs without a
provider call, and bound the ten unresolved exact-cohort jobs to the existing
control adapters. It promotes only the four-control deficit—AMKR, FSLR, LSCC, and

This is an allocation of research attention only: it cannot modify discovery

The same scheduler now admits verified market-flow successor work as a typed
residual lane. The subscription queue item preserves the complete residual;

residual verifier, and authority ceiling remain fixed. This preserves the
