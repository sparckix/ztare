## Eigenquestion

Can a typed operator grammar enumerate integrated strategic options and issue a
scope-bound frontier certificate that says exactly what was considered,
eliminated, left unresolved, or exposed as representation debt?

## Problem Statement

Strategy research already supplies several pieces of the problem:

- rugged-landscape models explain why interdependent choices produce multiple
  local peaks;
- options-led strategy work asks decision makers to generate integrated choice
  bundles and state what would have to be true for each bundle to succeed;
- industry analysis supplies external constraints, complementors, rival
  responses, and dynamics;
- search research studies local, integrated, chunked, and representation-level
  search;
- recent language-model systems improve textual or executable strategies by
  tree search and simulation.

The missing governed object is a bounded search space whose coverage can be
inspected. Existing methods can stop after a budget, produce a shortlist, or
report a high-scoring option without distinguishing these states:

1. all admissible programs in the declared grammar were considered;
2. some programs were omitted because the enumeration budget ended;
3. programs remain unresolved because their burdens of proof lack evidence;
4. a governing strategic distinction is absent from the grammar itself.

The principal's AWS billing grammar provides an architectural precedent: a
small typed operator vocabulary transforms sources, observations, states,
mechanisms, traces, cohorts, and positions. Recursive enumeration and closure
over that vocabulary supported service-packaging analysis. The strategy
substrate should preserve the operator-algebra shape without importing billing
terms or assuming that a flat product of choices is always the right grammar.

## Scope

This seam governs:

- a substrate-local typed operator grammar for strategic programs;
- deterministic bounded enumeration by type, depth, and program budget;
- QF_LIA+Bool closure of associative/commutative option-set compatibility before
  canonical typed-program construction;
- exact program identity bound to grammar name and version;
- external, internal, and dynamic burden-of-proof claims;
- exact behavioral equivalence under a declared evaluation model;
- Pareto dominance, explicit neighborhood-relative local peaks, and a jagged
  strategic frontier;
- separate scope-closure and decision-closure predicates;
- an explicit representation audit that prevents grammar exhaustion from
  being presented as exhaustive strategic imagination.

It does not govern:

- automatic scoring of companies from prose or web data;
- a universal ontology of strategy;
- claims that a selected strategy is optimal in an open world;
- external outreach or firm-specific strategy recommendations;
- extraction or publication of copyrighted teaching-note contents;
- a shared cross-substrate operator-grammar library before the strategy
  lowering and the prior billing lowering have been compared structurally.

separate.

### Commitment and recourse

The investment lowering next composes static choice systems into a two-stage
policy. The first stage freezes irreversible now-option IDs. The second stage
maps observable typed trigger regions to immutable recourse bundle IDs already
witnessed by the compatible choice-space certificate. Trigger identity includes
company, metric coordinate, unit, comparison, threshold basis, availability
epoch, and source identity;
future information cannot select an earlier action.

Each option declares sourced prerequisite edges and linear uses of capital,
capacity, or management bandwidth. The existing policy-region surface owns
totality, determinism, unreachable regions, and overlap counterexamples. For
each reachable region, the compatibility solver checks now-options plus recourse
against prerequisites and resource bounds, returning either a feasible witness
or a tracked blocking core. Only policies feasible in every reachable region
advance to the existing scenario/frontier evaluator.

This seam gives Z3 no empirical authority. The solver checks the frozen policy
and arithmetic constraints; it does not establish that a trigger matters, a
threshold is calibrated, a resource estimate is accurate, a scenario is likely,
an effect is causal, or a strategy is profitable. These remain source-bound
claims with later outcomes. The contingent-policy receipt remains research-only
and carries no security-ranking, portfolio-weight, or capital authority.

inside the existing company frontier compiler. It freezes a feasible commitment,
resolves every recourse leaf to an immutable already-enumerated bundle, builds

certificate. The compiler rejects recourse before commitment, unused trigger

## Option Analysis

### Option A - Flat morphological product

Represent strategy as one value selected from each axis, then enumerate the
Cartesian product.

- Advantage: small, deterministic, easy to explain.
- Limitation: assumes the axes and arity in advance; awkward for contingent
  policies, multi-step commitments, or operators that change the strategic
  state.

Verdict: retain as a restricted profile and baseline.

### Option B - Untyped textual mutation

Ask a language model to generate and revise strategic options, stopping after a
fixed number of iterations.

- Advantage: broad semantic reach and low setup cost.
- Limitation: no derivation completeness, unstable identity, and no mechanical
  account of omitted options.

Verdict: use later as a proposer or grammar-revision source, never as closure
authority.

### Option C - Typed operator programs

Declare terminals, typed operators, and bounded program depth. Enumerate every
well-typed program in the frozen scope, then lower terminal programs to
strategy options for feasibility, evaluation, equivalence, and frontier
analysis.

- Advantage: contains the flat product as a special case; supports recursive
  policies and state-changing operators; produces inspectable derivations.
- Limitation: grammar quality remains an external epistemic obligation.

Verdict: recommended.

## Recommendation

Build JaggedThoughts as a substrate-local typed operator-program enumerator and
frontier compiler. The first certificate must distinguish:

- `scope_closed`: the frozen grammar and declared bounds were exhausted and
  every target program has a witnessed disposition;
- `decision_closed`: scope closure plus a passed representation audit;
- `representation_status=unassessed|residual|passed`: the status of the
  grammar's ability to express the decision's governing distinctions.

The initial implementation should remain deterministic and evaluator-agnostic.
Business semantics enter through declared terminals, operator signatures,
burden claims, evaluation receipts, neighborhood edges, and representation
audits.

## Scientific Positioning

The seam inherits typed grammar enumeration, SMT solving, CEGIS/CEGAR,
multi-objective frontiers, robust control, dual control, Bayesian experiment
design, and LLM hypothesis proposal. The candidate contribution is their
strategy-specific composition: source-bound strategic traces feed executable
rival mechanisms; the surviving committee feeds exhaustive bounded contingent
policies; policy evaluation feeds a frontier and declared-neighborhood peaks;
counterexamples select representation repair; and one authority membrane
governs probes and actions. The separate `scope_closed` and
representation-audited `decision_closed` predicates are part of that candidate
bundle. No constituent method is claimed as new.

[Model Discovery Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09696) is a close comparator
for the mechanism-discovery portion. It demonstrates an LLM-proposer,
Bayesian-inference, held-out-check, and value-of-information loop on synthetic
physics, chemistry, and biology benchmarks. That result supports the division
of labor and supplies a baseline; it does not establish strategic autonomy.
Its evaluation has known truth, controlled interventions, bounded experiment
budgets, designed intervention spaces, and simulator or likelihood access.
This seam additionally faces sparse observational histories, endogenous
rivals, nonstationarity, delayed and irreversible actions, multi-objective
authority, and price-relative
investment outcomes. MDA does not test the seam's policy enumeration, closure
predicates, local peaks, governance, transfer across firms, or returns. The
current version-space is not Bayesian posterior evidence or causal
identification. The shared information-yield kernel can price caller-supplied
posterior weights and stochastic predictions, but no admitted strategy leaf
currently produces those beliefs from a calibrated likelihood or simulator.

The next publication-grade discriminator is a frozen-time, concealed-outcome
study with decision-episode-blocked evaluation. It compares flat morphology,
unconstrained LLM search, the typed compiler with and without representation
repair, persistence and regularized predictive controls, and an MDA-style arm
where simulator or likelihood assumptions hold. Outcome-blind frozen endpoints are
false closure, concealed-transition predictive loss, frontier stability,
cost-normalized probe value, and prospective decision regret. Strategic
operating outcomes and investment returns are settled separately. A broader
claim fails if the profile author encodes the winner, independent challenges
regularly add frontier-changing behavior after closure, or the strongest
comparator matches the predictive and decision results.
