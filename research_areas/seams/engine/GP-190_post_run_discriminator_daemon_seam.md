## Eigenquestion

Which parts of the recent `gp163d` gravity and NS Millennium loops were
decisive operator-supervisor moves that should become ZTARE primitives,
and which parts should remain outside the apparatus because they still require
underspecified judgment?

## Bounded Thesis

The evidence supports a narrow claim:

> ZTARE plus a disciplined operator-supervisor loop works as a discovery
> instrumentation system. It turns failed fits, nulls, adversarial LLM
> critiques, and numerical anomalies into falsifiers, substrate audits, and
> reusable gates. It does not yet support the stronger claim that ZTARE
> autonomously discovers theory.

The practical conclusion is not "add a smarter agent." GP-120 remains right:
do not blur the M-form with a probabilistic bridger. The right move is artifact
enrichment:

1. record the next-discriminator selection as a first-class object;
2. require local-positive claims to report exported/background debt;
3. license cross-domain transfers only after they name the shared primitive,
   the non-shared physics, and the discriminator that would make the analogy
   meaningful.

## Why This Seam Exists

The `gp163d` and NS workstreams independently converged on the same apparatus
gap.

In `gp163d`, the valuable moves were not just candidate equations. The value
came from instrument repair and hostile discriminators: row-wise nulls, feature
collapse audits, superfluid kill-shots, empty-box AQUAL controls, Krylov
background repair, large-box controls, rotation ladders, and the later
orientation/action bottleneck. Each move narrowed a tempting physics story to a
smaller measurable claim.

In NS, the same pattern repeated: knot-core growth became a bounded near-miss;
pressure-footprint claims moved from radial mass to signed angular moments;
static stealth candidates forced a dynamic-admissibility pivot. Again, the
progress came from picking the next discriminating test, not from accepting the
first positive mechanism.

Today, that selection mostly lives in chat and operator routing. It is later
compressed into E-rows, F-rows, seams, or paper prose. That is too late for a
discovery instrument. The next discriminator should be written while it is
chosen, in a format the apparatus can replay, criticize, and eventually
automate.

## What Worked

1. **Instrument repair before interpretation.** Empty-box AQUAL tests, Krylov
   background checks, face-flux stencils, raw-before-derived NS snapshots, and
   dynamic-admissibility audits prevented numerical artifacts from becoming
   physics claims.

2. **Inversion pressure.** Cold LLM critiques and Codex reviews repeatedly
   downgraded attractive stories into narrower falsifiers. The useful output
   was not the prose analogy; it was the kill condition.

3. **Ledger discipline.** E-rows and F-rows preserved the downgrades. The
   record now shows not only what survived, but which shortcuts were killed.

4. **Cross-domain compression after downgrade.** The admissible shared object
   is not "fluid equals gravity." It is the portable pattern:

   ```text
   local mechanism -> exported/background debt audit -> control ladder
   -> dynamic/admissibility bridge
   ```

## What Stayed Too Manual

1. **Next-test selection.** The operator still asks "what now?", sends cold
   shots, and chooses between GPU, offline mining, proof routing, and closure.

2. **Narrative-shortcut detection.** The apparatus does not yet automatically
   notice the common promotion errors: local-to-global, static-to-dynamic,
   scalar-to-tensor, aligned-to-stable, and analogy-to-isomorphism.

3. **Background debt reporting.** A high local gain can be meaningless if it
   exports debt into the halo, boundary, pressure field, `mu` saturation, or
   cleanup channel. This was learned manually in both `gp163d` and NS.

4. **Frontier launch admissibility.** GPU runs still need human babysitting for
   smoke tests, `PYTHONPATH`, JAX/GPU parity, ntfy, artifact presence, and
   kill/retry decisions. This is instrumentation debt, not science.

## Primitive 1: `next_discriminator_queue.jsonl`

Every closed run, promoted champion, or frontier anomaly should emit proposed
next tests in a typed queue:

```json
{
  "timestamp": "2026-04-30T10:55:30-04:00",
  "project": "gp163d_unified_accel",
  "trigger_artifact": "path/to/source.md",
  "claim_under_pressure": "local separator survives repaired AQUAL solve",
  "narrative_shortcut": "local_positive_to_universal_law",
  "rival_explanations": [
    "finite_box_boundary_artifact",
    "orientation_lock_artifact",
    "background_debt_export"
  ],
  "cheapest_discriminator": "L=4 rotation ladder with binary invariant check",
  "kill_condition": "90deg fails to recover aligned symmetry or binary suppression shatters",
  "instrument_risk": "Krylov background CPU-side stall",
  "required_artifacts": [
    "source summary json",
    "background residual",
    "mu/off-core debt profile",
    "orientation angle metadata"
  ],
  "cost_estimate": {
    "mode": "GPU",
    "wall_clock": "hours",
    "dollars": "operator-supplied"
  },
  "auto_testable": false,
  "needs_human": true
}
```

This is the minimal bridge between GP-119's Inverter and GP-188's primitive
compilation boundary. The Inverter proposes. The daemon writes structured
objects. The operator remains the chooser when the discriminator is still
underspecified.

## Primitive 2: Background-Debt Ratio Ladder

Any claim of a local mechanism must report the second column: what debt is
exported elsewhere?

Generic schema:

```text
local_gain = measured improvement or anomaly at the focal object
background_debt = off-core load, halo load, pressure-footprint cancellation,
                  boundary stiffness, cleanup tax, or saturation proxy
ratio = local_gain / max(background_debt, eps)
sweep_axes = intensity, scale, resolution, orientation, locality
success = ratio remains interpretable under the sweep and has a stable
          asymptotic/control behavior
failure = ratio collapses, flips sign, or depends on a known instrument axis
```

Examples:

- `gp163d`: report separator gain together with off-core field debt, `mu`
  saturation, background residual, and orientation sensitivity.
- NS: report local stretching/danger gain together with pressure-footprint
  cancellation, reset tax, dynamic exposure, and identity/churn stability.

The primitive is deliberately domain-neutral. Each domain supplies the debt
proxy, but the promotion rule is the same: local profit without the liability
column is not a promotable finding.

## Primitive 3: Cross-Domain Transfer License

Before importing an insight from NS to gravity, gravity to NS, or any other
domain pair, the apparatus should require:

1. **Shared primitive:** the smallest structural object that is genuinely
   common, e.g. "local gain audited against exported background debt."
2. **Non-shared physics:** what is explicitly not being transferred, e.g.
   "AQUAL is not Navier-Stokes; static elliptic shielding is not viscous
   vorticity dynamics."
3. **Forbidden claims:** what the analogy cannot license.
4. **Discriminator:** what test would make the analogy meaningful rather than
   decorative.

This prevents a useful analogy from becoming an unearned isomorphism.

## Implementation Plan

### Phase A — Retroactive extraction

Run against closed `gp163d` and NS artifacts only. No paid LLM calls and no new
GPU runs.

Output:

- `projects/<slug>/workspace/next_discriminator_queue.replay.jsonl`
- one row per historically important operator/Codex move
- taxonomy labels for narrative shortcuts and discriminator classes

Success criterion:

- at least 80% of decisive next moves in the reviewed `gp163d`/NS sequence
  map to six or fewer templates;
- each mapped template names a runnable or artifact-backed discriminator;
- failures are explicit: "not recoverable without chat" or "domain-specific
  judgment remains."

Failure criterion:

- the decisive moves depend on unrecorded chat intuition rather than artifacts,
  or they fragment into many one-off templates that do not compile.

### Phase B — Queue writer

Add a lightweight writer that any post-run analyzer, cold-shot runner, or
manual Codex session can call to append a discriminator proposal. This is not
autonomous execution.

### Phase C — Post-positive hook

After a champion/finding candidate appears, ZTARE asks for the first inversion
object:

```text
What would make this positive result uninterpretable?
What is the cheapest test that attacks that path?
What artifact must be written so tomorrow's agent can evaluate it?
```

The answer becomes a queue row. Auto-testable rows may execute under existing
gate rules. Ambiguous rows go to the operator.

### Phase D — Promotion gate

Before an F-row or INS-row can be promoted from a local positive result, the
track-record closure helper checks whether at least one discriminator queue row
exists and whether its result has been recorded or explicitly deferred.

## What This Does Not Do

It does not mechanize abductive eigenquestion selection completely. It only
turns recurring, observed discriminator moves into replayable artifacts.

It does not add a bridger agent. GP-120's warning stands: probabilistic
integration can blur the generation/evaluation boundary. This seam enriches
boundary objects instead.

It does not strengthen any physics claim by itself. It strengthens the
methodological claim that the apparatus can learn from how it failed.

It also does not let a weak test launder promotion. A queued discriminator is
not automatically a promotion license. Severity matters:

- L1: smoke / import / NaN checks;
- L2: sanity / instrument checks;
- L3: local falsifier;
- L4: hostile control;
- L5: decisive ladder or dark-domain discriminator.

Only commit-stage L4/L5 rows can support promotion. Scratchpad rows are allowed
to preserve lateral "idea feliz" exploration, but they cannot justify an F-row
or INS-row until converted into a commit-stage discriminator with a kill
condition and required artifacts.

## Paper 7 Implication

Paper 7 should not say "ZTARE discovered a gravity law." The stronger bounded
claim is:

> `gp163d` demonstrates ZTARE as a substrate and instrument prober. The
> apparatus did not merely fail to fit; it localized missing substrate axes,
> forced instrument repairs, and converted operator-supervisor falsifications
> into reusable gates. The remaining architecture gap is post-run
> discriminator selection, which this seam turns into a first-class target.

## 2026-04-30 18:11:00 EDT — Paid Batch Fail-Closed Stop Rule

The gp163d `normal_face_flux` `L=4.0,n=128,gamma=0.25` GPU batch exposed a
Research Director failure mode: treating a paid independent angle ladder as
"collect all rows, then interpret" even after the first completed config
already failed a hard source-loaded solver admissibility gate.

Observed failure:

- empty tidal AQUAL backgrounds converged at `~1e-6`;
- source-loaded AQUAL solves reported `converged=false` with residuals
  around `5e-3..1e-2`;
- ratios exploded to physically meaningless values (`UDG ~922..1213`,
  binary `~1.26..5.31`);
- the remaining angle should have been killed immediately.

Mechanized correction:

- `run_gpu_domain_validation.py` now enforces background/source residual
  admissibility after every config;
- any inadmissible config returns a non-zero code and sends a high-priority
  notification;
- the Research Director and Manager mandates now require killing remaining
  independent paid configs after the first hard-gate failure;
- the GPU runbook states source-loaded residual convergence is required and
  empty-box convergence alone is insufficient.

Principle:

> Batch completion is not the objective. Information yield per dollar is.
