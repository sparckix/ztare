## Bounded Claim

Paper 7 should make a methodological claim, not a physics-autonomy claim:

> The `gp163d` sequence supports ZTARE as a substrate and instrument prober.
> In combination with a disciplined operator-supervisor loop, the apparatus
> converts failed fits, nulls, and adversarial critiques into substrate
> diagnoses, instrument repairs, and reusable gate candidates. The evidence
> does not show fully autonomous theory discovery; it shows a partially
> mechanized discovery-instrumentation loop whose remaining bottleneck is
> post-run discriminator selection.

This is stronger than "an LLM helped with analysis" and weaker than "ZTARE
discovered a law." It is the claim the evidence can bear.

## Why `gp163d` Matters Methodologically

The important Paper 7 object is not any one gravity formula. The important
object is the sequence:

1. row-wise symbolic regression failed to bridge regimes;
2. R26 localized a substrate feature-collapse ceiling;
3. apparatus self-improvement split into ALU/RAM loops;
4. subsequent gravity work repeatedly converted attractive hypotheses into
   sharper controls: superfluid sign falsifier, empty-box AQUAL audit, Krylov
   background repair, large-box check, rotation ladder, and source-action /
   orientation-stability bottleneck.

The sequence shows ZTARE doing useful scientific work even when the physics
claim is not settled. It does not only optimize scores; it names why the
instrument cannot answer a question in its current form.

## What The Symbiotic Loop Did

The operator-supervisor loop contributed four separable functions:

1. **Eigenquestion compression.** It repeatedly asked what smallest question
   would change the next action.
2. **Narrative-shortcut inversion.** It attacked local-to-global,
   static-to-dynamic, scalar-to-tensor, aligned-to-stable, and
   analogy-to-isomorphism promotions.
3. **Instrument repair routing.** It distinguished physics nulls from
   instrument nulls: missing features, bad boundary controls, solver stiffness,
   unlogged fields, and launch-environment failures.
4. **Closure discipline.** It converted results into E-rows, F-rows, seams, and
   paper-grade claims only after the scope was narrowed.

That is real work, but it is not yet autonomous. The strongest moves often
entered through chat and were later recorded. GP-190 is the response: move
those discriminator choices into first-class artifacts.

## What To Mechanize Next

Paper 7 can point to the next architecture target:

1. **Post-run discriminator queue.** Every champion, null, or anomaly emits a
   structured next-test proposal with rival explanations, kill condition,
   instrument risk, required artifacts, and cost.
2. **Background-debt ratio ladder.** Every local-gain claim must report the
   liability column: off-core field debt, halo load, pressure-footprint
   cancellation, `mu` saturation, cleanup tax, or an explicit domain-specific
   analogue.
3. **Cross-domain transfer license.** Analogies must state the shared
   primitive, non-shared physics, forbidden claims, and discriminator required
   for the analogy to become meaningful.

> an LLM discovered a gravitational law, but that a disciplined LLM+operator
> system can turn failed theoretical search into reusable instrumentation.
